## 1. Summary

1. A fort or fortress protects adjacent sea with a once-per-ship shot against hostile traffic.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue identifies no movement-triggered coastal battery; existing fortification values alone do not fire.
3. This requirement owns shot eligibility, strength and result, leaving ship repair to R-0098.

## 2. Behavior

1. When a hostile ship enters a sea square adjacent to an owned coastal colony with a fort or fortress, that colony may fire once at that ship per owner's round. A stockade, inland colony, nonadjacent step or nonhostile ship does not fire.
2. Fort strength is 4, fortress 6, plus 2 for each full artillery and 1 for each damaged artillery stationed on the colony tile. Use the accepted `A/(A+D)` draw against the ship's real defense; a hit damages the ship and sends it through R-0098 repair, a miss leaves it sailing. Do not sink or loot it through R-0097's separate battle outcome.
3. A canceled/rejected move has no shot. Store the once-per-ship/owner-round record so Save/Continue, a second adjacent step or screen reopening cannot duplicate fire. Another eligible colony has its own record. UI explains a hit/miss and any forced repair.
4. UC-077 checks stockade/fort/fortress, full/damaged artillery, hit/miss, adjacent versus distant and reload/two-step boundaries.

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0177-refinement)): After a legal ship step, eligible coastal forts fire in stable colony-ID order, each under its own once-per-ship/round limit. Stop remaining events as soon as a hit sends the ship to repairs. If the ship remains, resolve one patrol interception under the accepted strength/ID selection; do not duplicate either event on Continue.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model calculates coastal battery eligibility/strength from actual colony construction and tile occupants, then resolves and records the shot during a successful ship step. Reuse R-0098 damage destination and common ship defense; GUI/AI do not calculate damage.
2. At refinement, reconcile shot order if multiple eligible colonies and a hostile patrol also affect the same step, preserving one committed movement outcome and no repeated RNG on reload.
3. Proposed model/UC-077 checks cover every strength increment, exact probability boundary, once-per-round record, repair, multiple batteries and no-fire cases under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no coastal fire implementation or runtime acceptance is claimed.
5. Refinement update 2026-10-20: implement the [accepted decision](../gap-analysis.md#r-0177-refinement) in the existing model owner and its UI/AI consumers; extend the named checks above for its boundaries, rejection, persistence and exactly-once effects. This is specification work only: preserve current status and do not claim implementation or runtime acceptance; UI regression runs on CI, not locally, under the user’s current instruction.
