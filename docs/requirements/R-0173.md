# R-0173 — Resolve native roles and terrain ambush in land combat

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P17/P18; [COMBAT-003/004/006/007/011](../gap-analysis.md#land-combat-naval-combat-and-capture), NAT-008; [UC-061](../use-cases.md#domorode-narody)/[UC-073/074](../use-cases.md#boj-a-dobyvani) (Hra).
4. Dependencies: native actors [R-0151](R-0151.md), raid selection [R-0156](R-0156.md), ordinary strength/resolution [R-0093](done/R-0093.md)/[R-0094](done/R-0094.md), artillery [R-0095](R-0095.md), terrain variants [R-0123](R-0123.md).
5. Primary area: [A09](areas.md#a09).
6. Related areas: A08 native hostility/AI; A05 terrain/role identity; A03 strength explanation.
7. Origin: native combat roles and ambush remain outside the delivered ordinary European combat and artillery slices.

## 1. Summary

1. Native braves need legal attacks and defenses whose strengths reflect their actual role and terrain, without using the ordinary European soldier as a substitute.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue's missing-native observation is historical source assessment, not a runtime result. R-0093/0094 already deliver ordinary land strength and resolution; R-0095 owns artillery lifecycle.
3. This requirement owns native role combat, native terrain exception and the integration of R-0095's fortified-artillery anti-raid bonus. Native AI target choice remains R-0156.

## 2. Behavior

1. Brave, armed brave, mounted brave and mounted warrior use aid-card base strengths 1, 2, 2 and 3 respectively. A native unit attacks only with an eligible role and a legal known adjacent target; the same role is a valid defender. No ordinary European equipment or veteran status is silently applied.
2. A native receives the terrain-card ambush bonus when attacking or defending in forest, hills or mountains: ordinarily forest +50%, swamp forest +75%, hills +100%, mountains +150%. Ordinary European defender terrain bonuses do not apply against a native attacker. Bare marsh/swamp and other terrain grant no **native ambush**; any applicable ordinary rule remains separate. The strength explanation and resolver use the same final values; European-versus-European battles retain their delivered ordinary modifiers.
3. A full or damaged artillery piece defending its own fortified colony against a real native raid receives R-0095's accepted +75% modifier; an ordinary European attack or outdoor gun does not. The existing artillery damage progression remains R-0095's rule.
4. A defeated unarmed European facing natives dies; it is not captured as a European civilian. Cancel/stale attack changes no movement, relations or random state. A confirmed battle uses R-0094's odds and ordinary participant exhaustion, then R-0154 receives the actual native attack event once. UC-061/073/074 compare each role, terrain exception, artillery qualification, civilian fate and Save/Continue.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model adds explicit native combat-role strengths/eligibility to the shared strength snapshot and resolver. Reuse R-0093 terrain entries and R-0094 probability, with one visible exception branch for native opponents; do not add GUI-side odds or an AI-only battle path.
2. Apply the manual's PDF p. 37 terrain exception with card PDF p. 135 values through the shared strength snapshot. Connect the native raid predicate to R-0095's existing gun rule and R-0156's legal AI action; conquest of a settlement is R-0174. Resolve the battle-tile selection question before readiness.
3. Proposed model/UC-061/073/074 checks cover all four role bases, eligible terrain/open ground, both attack directions, ordinary European regression, fortified/outdoor artillery and unchanged rejection under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no native combat implementation or runtime acceptance is claimed.
