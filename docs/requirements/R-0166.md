# R-0166 — Incite a native attack on a selected European power

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-20
3. Related: P19/P21; [NAT-020](../gap-analysis.md#native-nations-settlements-and-relations); [UC-067](../use-cases.md#domorode-narody) (Hra). See the [accepted incitement rule](../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations).
4. Dependencies: missionary expertise [R-0163](done/R-0163.md), tribe/mission count [R-0150](R-0150.md)/[R-0164](R-0164.md), attitude [R-0153](R-0153.md), native raid AI [R-0156](R-0156.md).
5. Primary area: [A08](areas.md#a08).
6. Related areas: A04 pending decision/target persistence; A03 quote/confirmation; A09 legal native attack.
7. Origin: NAT-020 paid native target selection, not a generic European treaty or direct player attack.

## 1. Summary

1. A missionary may pay to urge a tribe against a chosen European rival, with a real possibility of refusal and a real AI target on success.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` reports no paid-target diplomacy. This is source inspection, not runtime acceptance.
3. This requirement owns quote, confirmed attempt, result and target order. R-0156 chooses legal native attacks afterward.

## 2. Behavior

1. Quote the selected target and `ceil(500 × (1 + own hostility/100) / (1 + 0.1 × own missions in this tribe))` gold. A confirmed ordinary missionary succeeds 25% of the time, an effective expert 50%; Brebeuf grants the latter to ordinary missionaries through R-0163.
2. Charge only a confirmed affordable attempt, once. On success, set tribal anger T toward the selected European to at least 75 and give the tribe a persisted target that its legal native AI pursues. A failed confirmed attempt pays for that attempt but sets no new target; cancellation, insufficient gold or stale eligibility changes no money, target, relationship or random state.
3. Show the real price/risk before confirmation. Save/Continue preserves a pending attempt and a successful target without reroll or double debit. UC-067 checks target isolation, price directions, ordinary/expert/Brebeuf draws, acceptance/refusal and subsequent native action.

4. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0166-refinement)): A successful incitement target persists for 20 turns of that native nation, replacing the proposed ten-turn lifetime. Prefer legal known targets of the paid European power; if no eligible paid target exists, use ordinary hostile-target/patrol behavior. End the order when the target nation ceases to exist or relations fall below hostile; a new successful incitement replaces the old target. The user’s travel rationale leaves the pursuit radius as the narrow follow-up below, not an authorization for hidden-map knowledge.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model validates caller, selected target, mission count, relation and treasury before drawing/charging; persist target and pending quote/draw. AI R-0156 consumes the target through shared legal movement/attack operations; GUI does not decide success or order braves directly.
2. On selection, connect target priority to the existing nearest-enemy raid policy without an endless or illegal pursuit. Native combat remains a separate prerequisite.
3. Proposed model/UC-067 checks cover price, 25/50%, exact debit, failed/canceled/stale paths, target persistence and bounded AI action. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no gameplay change or runtime acceptance is claimed.
5. Refinement update 2026-10-20: implement the [accepted decision](../gap-analysis.md#r-0166-refinement) in the existing model owner and its UI/AI consumers; extend the named checks above for its boundaries, rejection, persistence and exactly-once effects. This is specification work only: preserve current status and do not claim implementation or runtime acceptance; UI regression runs on CI, not locally, under the user’s current instruction.

## 4. Open questions

1. Does paid incitement authorize travel toward known targets beyond the normal two-tile native raid radius? The user extended lifetime to 20 turns because travel may take longer than ten, while the initial proposal retained ordinary range. Proposed: pursue the nearest known reachable target of the paid nation for those 20 turns, without the ordinary home-patrol radius, using only known legal paths; with no reachable known target return to ordinary behavior.
