## 1. Summary

1. A hostile native brave should act on nearby enemies, while improved relations stop native-initiated attacks.
2. The 2026-10-17 source catalogue at `8f3ce118` has no native actor/AI, and European AI does not establish native relation-driven behavior. This is source assessment only.
3. This requirement owns bounded native raid/patrol selection, not combat probabilities, settlement conquest or European AI strategy.

## 2. Behavior

1. On its own turn, a hostile brave chooses the nearest reachable enemy within two tiles and attempts a legal attack through shared combat. If none is reachable, it patrols toward/around its home without fabricating an enemy or remaining in an endless decision loop.
2. At content, uneasy or angry states below hostile, the brave does not initiate an attack. When relations improve from hostile, subsequent native decisions honor the calmer state; existing European actions still follow their own legality.
3. Selection uses only native-known reachable targets and actual movement/attack limits. If a target vanishes or becomes illegal before action, the brave safely rechecks or ends its turn; no illegal mutation. Save/Continue retains home, position and relation state without replaying a raid.
4. UC-061/068 cover two candidate targets at different distances, blocked/no target, hostile versus calmer state and a bounded multi-turn return/patrol.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. A08 native AI selects from R-0151 braves and R-0153 attitude, using shared model path/range/attack operations. The model validates every move/attack; AI neither mutates relations nor reads hidden European state through GUI.
2. On selection, reconcile target tie and exact patrol waypoint with accepted path/turn budgets; the future A09 native combat owner must provide a legal attack. European expansion/combat AI retains its separate requirements. R-0154 consumes any actual attack event.
3. Proposed AI/model/UC-061/068 checks cover nearest reachable target, two-square limit, legal attack, no target, de-escalation, turn termination and Continue. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no gameplay change or runtime acceptance is claimed.
