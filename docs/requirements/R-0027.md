## 1. Summary

1. Give the first rival a bounded economic objective: land a colonist and establish a viable first colony instead of only wandering.

2. The catalogue reports that `SimpleAiPlayer` moves/attacks/embarks, with no founding or economic planning in `turnStarted`.

3. One rival fleet→landing→first colony objective and a safe no-site outcome. Sustained production planning, trade, expansion and military strategy remain later DIP-001 slices.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for individual refinement; capture does not approve implementation.

1. From the agreed reference position, the rival explores only known/reachable options, lands its colonist and founds one colony at a model-valid site.

2. Founding uses the same ownership, location, naming and identity rules as the human action. It neither creates bonus settlers nor bypasses movement/cargo restrictions.

3. Choose among candidate sites with an explicit deterministic ranking for tests. A blocked path or taken site triggers bounded replanning rather than an endless turn.

4. If no legal site exists within the chosen search policy, end the turn safely and retry later. Existing human colonies and units remain untouched.

5. Save/load during travel preserves or deterministically reconstructs the goal; it does not create a second colony after the first succeeds.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Keep goal selection in `ai` and reuse shared model operations, topology and Z02 founding. Review `SimpleAiPlayer` and `ContinentTargets` before adding a small strategy state.

2. Define candidate scoring, bounded search and goal completion; persist only necessary intent and stable object references.

3. Proposed `TC_rival_first_colony_test` observes an ordinary-start rival over a bounded number of turns. Seeded AI/model tests cover blocked shore, no site, competing site and continuation; verify each turn terminates.
