## 1. Summary

1. The player needs an explicit way to pay the required arrears and resume trade in a boycotted commodity.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Back-tax payment was excluded from R-0030’s demand/protest slice and has no accepted quote/settlement flow.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected as requirement 17 of the Claude batch; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Accepted behavior; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Select a boycotted commodity → show the exact settlement quote and resulting trade permission before payment.

2. Confirm with sufficient treasury and unchanged boycott → debit once and lift the intended restriction.

3. Cancel, insufficient funds, obsolete quote or already lifted boycott → no charge; unrelated boycotts remain.

4. Save/load retains arrears and restrictions. Fugger’s one-time effect is a separate founder outcome and must not be simulated by this payment action.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Freeze commodity-specific arrears at protest and clear only that boycott on sufficient payment.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Reuse R-0102's `Player`/`TaxState` commodity arrears. An immutable payment quote binds player identity, commodity, exact frozen amount and the current tax-demand generation; the model revalidates the live active actor, European trade access, debt and available gold before mutation. A repeated or replaced quote changes nothing. Payment removes one debt and debits once, without modifying tax rate, other debts or Congress.

2. In Europe expose a named back-tax action when debts exist. The dialog lets the player select a commodity, see its exact arrears and restored-trade consequence, and confirm or cancel. Show insufficient funds and expected stale rejection; enable confirmation from the model's eligibility, not duplicated UI rules. Keep the existing paused tax-demand controls and English/Czech localization.

3. Persist through the existing arrears map; no schema migration or new payment history is needed. Bind a quote to the current demand generation so clearing and recreating an identical debt cannot reuse an old confirmation. Fugger remains the existing one-time founder effect.

4. Add boundary model tests for exact/insufficient funds, cancel/read-only quote, duplicate and stale confirmation, wrong actor/paused/independent state, unrelated debts, changed market prices, save/load and restored purchase/sale eligibility. TC79/F71 uses a real protest followed by cancel and successful payment, verifies exact gold and one restored commodity, and preserves it across Continue. Reuse existing scenario/page helpers and the [functional test contract](../functional-tests.md).

5. Targeted TC79/TC78/TC27 and model acceptance passed on 2026-10-18; see [evidence](../functional-tests.md#r-0103-back-tax-payment-2026-10-18). Main applies the shared formatter and documentation checks. Full regression and publication remain at the twentieth requirement. Architecture and validation follow the [package map](../architecture.md).
