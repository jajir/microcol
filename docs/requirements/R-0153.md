## 1. Summary

1. Native nations need tribal anger and each settlement's local alarm toward each European power as distinct, persistent values.
2. The 2026-10-17 source catalogue at `8f3ce118` finds no native relationship matrix. This is source assessment, not runtime acceptance.
3. This requirement owns the state and quiet/continuous update formula. Discrete attack, gift, trade and mission effects are R-0154 or their action owners; display is R-0155.

## 2. Behavior

1. Start tribal anger T and settlement-local alarm L at zero for each European. Retain fractions, clamp both to 0..100. Displayed hostility is based on `max(T,L)`: content [0,25), uneasy [25,50), angry [50,75), hostile [75,100]. Updating one settlement/European does not copy its L to others.
2. Each peaceful update reduces T and L by 1. Local positive inputs are +2 per unpaid worked/claimed tile, +0.25 per nearby European resident within the claim radius, +2 per armed unit within two squares, and +2/+3 for a nearby fort/fortress. R-0152's one-time +5 taking is a discrete input. Apply accepted local reductions after summing positive inputs, preserving fractions: France halves positive local alarm, not direct tribal anger.
3. Recruiting Pocahontas once sets this European's positive T and L to zero, immediately making those relationships content. Thereafter she halves positive local alarm generation only; direct tribal anger is not halved. France and Pocahontas multiply, so a +10 local input becomes +2.5. Existing and newly founded settlements use the same membership; no repeated reset on Continue.
4. At the end of the update add `mean(local alarm)/20` to that tribe's T, then clamp. The update runs once at its owning national boundary; Save/Continue does not repeat it. A local pressure source affects the corresponding tribe and European only. UC-003/061/095 compare France with another nation and tension before/after Pocahontas, including fractional accumulation.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model owns persisted nation×European T and settlement×European L, a shared clamped fractional update and typed discrete-effect entry points. Reuse settlement IDs R-0150 and R-0052's completed-turn boundary; GUI and AI read model state. Accept R-0152 unpaid-land inputs when its transaction owner is delivered, without making the core T/L engine depend on completed land purchase.
2. On selection, reconcile positive-input order with the accepted rule and R-0104 founder membership; apply Pocahontas's join reset exactly once and preserve France×Pocahontas fractional effects. Do not recompute relationship values in UI or duplicate an update on loading.
3. Proposed model/UC-003/061/095 checks cover pair isolation, 0/25/50/75/100 edges, every continuous input, French half, Pocahontas join reset and subsequent half, their +10→+2.5 combination, mean propagation, quiet decay and Save/Continue. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
