# R-0161 — Buy one of three offered native goods

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P19; [NAT-013](../gap-analysis.md#native-nations-settlements-and-relations); [UC-064](../use-cases.md#domorode-narody) (Hra). See the [accepted native market](../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations).
4. Dependencies: offered stock/admission [R-0159](R-0159.md), preceding confirmed sale/gift [R-0160](R-0160.md), cargo capacity [R-0132](done/R-0132.md).
5. Primary area: [A08](areas.md#a08).
6. Related areas: A06 cargo and Europe ask reference; A04 pending offer persistence; A03 purchase choice.
7. Origin: NAT-013 second-stage native purchase, not the Europe buy operation R-0020.

## 1. Summary

1. After selling or gifting, the trader may choose one of three locally available goods to buy from the settlement.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` finds no native purchase stage; this is a source observation only.
3. This requirement owns the second-stage offer and actual purchase, using R-0159 stock rather than creating free goods.

## 2. Behavior

1. Only after a confirmed sale or gift, show three goods ordered by local surplus then ID, their real available quantity and price at the Europe ask. The player may choose, negotiate where offered, or reject. A pending offer survives Save/Continue without becoming a new sale/gift or rerolling.
2. Confirmed purchase is bounded by settlement stock, carrier capacity and buyer gold; transfer the actual quantity and gold once. A partial purchase uses actual confirmed quantity. Empty stock, full carrier, insufficient gold, changed state, reject and cancel do not mutate either side.
3. The purchase closes the current second-stage choice without repeating the preceding sale/gift; the settlement stock and European cargo remain conserved across a turn and Continue. UC-064 checks all three offers, one successful/partial purchase and refusal.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model owns pending second-stage offer and one validated settlement-to-carrier transfer, reusing R-0159 stock and shared cargo/gold operations. GUI renders model quantities/price and handles expected stale rejection.
2. On selection, connect any permitted negotiation to a persisted quote/draw under the accepted native market rule; do not infer an unapproved discount or extra haggle probability from European trading.
3. Proposed model/UC-064 checks cover three goods, ordering, stock/cargo/gold bounds, reject/cancel/stale atomicity, partial quantity and Save/Continue without repeated first stage. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no gameplay change or runtime acceptance is claimed.

## 4. Open questions

1. What negotiation terms, if any, apply when buying native goods? NAT-013 permits negotiation, but the accepted 50%/25% +10% haggle rule specifies increased European receipts for a native *purchase* of European cargo, not this reverse trade.
