# R-0138 — Jettison a selected quantity of ship goods

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P08; CARGO-016; UC-026 (Hra). See the [cargo rule](../gap-analysis.md#transport-ships-and-atlantic-travel), [accepted jettison choice](../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport) and [cargo use case](../use-cases.md#lode-naklad-a-obchodni-trasy).
4. Dependencies: existing partial goods-removal operation and ship hold identity; one-hold goods cap [R-0132](R-0132.md) is shared but not the jettison trigger.
5. Primary area: [A06](areas.md#a06).
6. Related areas: A05 ship location/ownership; A03 quantity confirmation/feedback; A04 saved cargo balances.
7. Origin: CARGO-016 player-command residual in the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md#transport-ships-and-atlantic-travel).

## 1. Summary

1. A player at sea should be able to discard a chosen amount of ship goods deliberately, without accidentally losing passengers or another hold.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue source assessment at `ffae3396` credits a partial goods-removal operation but finds no player jettison control. That existing removal is reused.
3. Scope: validated ship/hold/quantity selection, confirmation and one goods decrease; ordinary selling and unloading remain separate.

## 2. Behavior

1. On an owned ship at sea, the player selects one goods hold and a quantity from 1 through its current amount, reviews the discard and confirms it. Only that hold loses the chosen amount; the ship stays in place and receives no gold.
2. Passengers, equipment and every other hold remain unchanged. Jettison cannot choose a passenger or empty hold. An unavailable ship, wrong owner, stale amount or changed hold rejects without any discard.
3. Cancel/Escape changes nothing. A repeated obsolete confirmation cannot discard twice. Save and Continue retain the reduced goods quantity and never repeat the command on load.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Wrap the existing goods-removal primitive in a model-validated ship action binding live owner, ship, hold and amount to the confirmed choice. UI displays eligible holds and a quantity/confirm/cancel flow; it does not mutate cargo directly.
2. Not prepared for development. On selection, inspect map-sea versus Atlantic-transit interaction and decide where the player may invoke this command beyond an ordinary sea tile.
3. Proposed checks: partial/full quantity, 0/overstock rejection, passenger/other hold preservation, wrong owner, stale/repeated confirm, cancel and save/Continue. Add a named UC-026 player path under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: captured only; no gameplay change or runtime test is claimed.

## 4. Open questions

1. Is jettison available during an Atlantic crossing as well as from a ship on a map sea tile? The source permits throwing cargo overboard but does not settle this UI/location boundary.
