# R-0176 — Intercept a ship passing a hostile armed patrol

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P17; [COMBAT-015](../gap-analysis.md#land-combat-naval-combat-and-capture); [UC-076](../use-cases.md#boj-a-dobyvani) (Hra).
4. Dependencies: legal sea movement [R-0054](done/R-0054.md), shared costs/range [R-0125](done/R-0125.md), ship attack roles [R-0097](done/R-0097.md) and relations/privateer exception [R-0087](done/R-0087.md)/[R-0099](done/R-0099.md). Rival patrol choice is [R-0171](R-0171.md).
5. Primary area: [A09](areas.md#a09).
6. Related areas: A05 movement/range; A08 naval AI/relation; A03 interruption feedback; A04 draw persistence.
7. Origin: passing-ship interception missing beyond R-0097's delivered deliberate naval battles; no automatic battle is added.

## 1. Summary

1. A ship passing an armed hostile patrol may go unnoticed, lose one remaining move or be stopped for the turn.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue describes no per-step patrol check. R-0097's ship battle result does not intercept a moving ship.
3. This requirement owns the movement-side check and result, not AI placement or a new naval battle.

## 2. Behavior

1. After each legal ship step adjacent to an armed hostile ship, check once for that mover step. If several qualify, use the strongest patrol then stable ID. A 50% draw leaves the ship undetected; the next 25% removes one remaining movement point; the last 25% removes all remaining movement. No automatic battle or cargo transfer occurs.
2. A friendly, unarmed, nonadjacent or otherwise nonhostile ship cannot intercept. A blockade at a harbor approach follows the same adjacent-step rule; it does not create an impassable tile by itself. Normal sea movement and port legality remain in force.
3. The resulting remaining movement, chosen patrol and draw are consumed once. Save/Continue or redraw of path/range does not repeat an already completed step. UI reports slowdown/stop; undetected passage does not disclose a hidden patrol.
4. UC-076 checks all three controlled draw bands, two-patrol priority, port approach, no eligible patrol and continuation.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model attaches one patrol check to successful ship-step execution, after legal movement cost and before a next step. Use a saved result/event and the common movement allowance; path/range can show possible interruption without predicting hidden RNG. AI invokes the same model operation.
2. At refinement, verify exactly which relation states count as hostile for interception and how a privateer's exception affects patrols. Do not infer a war declaration or automatically call R-0097.
3. Proposed model/UC-076 checks cover 0.5/0.25/0.25 boundaries, strongest/ID selection, no battle, remaining-point floor, harbor step and reload under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no patrol implementation or runtime acceptance is claimed.

## 4. Open questions

1. Does a privateer at peace count as a hostile patrol against ordinary foreign shipping, or is patrol interception limited to declared enemies? The accepted privateer rule covers deliberate attacks, not passive detection.
