## 1. Summary

1. During revolution, a colony attacker gains strength from the share of residents supporting its cause.
2. R-0100 supplies the authoritative support/affiliation value; this requirement owns only its situational combat use.

## 2. Behavior

1. A rebel attacking a colony receives an attack-strength percentage equal to that colony's exact rebel share; a Crown attacker receives the complementary Tory share. Thus 60% rebel support gives +60% rebel or +40% royal, before the accepted additive multiplier and final strength odds.
2. The modifier applies only to a qualifying revolutionary colony attack, using the colony's **current** support/affiliation at confirmation. It does not modify countryside battle, defender strength or pre-declaration fighting. Bombardment/ambush from R-0178 remain separate visible modifiers.
3. Cancel/stale support or target change spends no movement/RNG. Preview and resolver agree; Save/Continue uses persisted current support. UC-073/084 compare both causes, exact 60/40 and 0/100 boundaries, occupation and no-war cases.

4. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0173](../gap-analysis.md#r-0173-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.
