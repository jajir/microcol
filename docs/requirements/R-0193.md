# R-0193 — Apply the 1600, 1800 and 1850 game boundaries

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P25; [END-001/002/003](../gap-analysis.md#end-conditions-and-scoring); [UC-111](../use-cases.md#konec-hry-ulozeni-a-informace) (Hra); [accepted date policy](../gap-analysis.md#end-dates-and-scoring).
4. Dependencies: delivered calendar [R-0051](done/R-0051.md), declaration [R-0105](done/R-0105.md), revolutionary results [R-0191](R-0191.md)/[R-0192](R-0192.md), score snapshot [R-0195](R-0195.md)/[R-0196](R-0196.md).
5. Primary area: [A10](areas.md#a10).
6. Related areas: A04 calendar/save boundary; A03 deadline feedback; A07 last-colony state.
7. Origin: calendar end/resume residual; legacy 15-turn zero-colony and fixed 1800 ending are not the accepted dates.

## 1. Summary

1. The game needs distinct last-colony and scored-play deadlines, with limited unscored continuation.
2. This requirement owns automatic date transitions; R-0194 owns the player's Exit/Retire choices.

## 2. Behavior

1. At entry to year 1600, a nation with zero owned colonies loses even if its last colony disappeared earlier; from then onward losing the last colony ends ordinary play. Before 1600, a zero-colony position does not end solely for that reason, and no fixed turn-count substitute applies. At entry to spring 1800, a nation without declared revolution records its score once and may continue **without scoring** until spring 1850.
2. A revolution already underway continues scored play past 1800 until victory/loss or spring 1850; if unresolved at 1850 it loses. Spring 1850 ends all play. A nation cannot declare after the accepted pre-1800 boundary.
3. The 1800 result is frozen: later unscored turns, retirement, loss or Save/Continue cannot replace its score. UC-111 checks differing start dates/turn counts, zero colonies already at 1600 entry, last colony lost just after 1600, revolution just before 1800, unresolved 1850, one score and resume.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model calendar/end evaluator records scored-ended, unscored-continuing and terminal states separately, using R-0051 entry-to-spring timing and R-0195/0196 result identity. GUI offers the permitted continuation without recomputing the frozen score.
2. Proposed model/UC-111 checks cover each year transition, one-time score, rebel exception, unscored continuation and reload under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
3. Current result: capture only; no deadline implementation or runtime acceptance is claimed.
