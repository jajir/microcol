## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a school to train eligible residents into the selected teacher’s supported skill.

2. Current situation: The building catalogue includes education facilities, but constructing or buying an expert does not establish a teacher/student training cycle.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Assign an eligible teacher and student → show the skill, required facility and progress before committing the assignment.

2. Schoolhouse/college/university support their documented one/two/three teacher limits and allowed skill levels.

3. Advance the agreed teaching interval → promote the eligible student once along the supported criminal/servant/free/expert progression.

4. Cancel, transfer or lose a participant → handle progress under the agreed policy without duplicating skills or workers. Save/load retains assignments and progress.
