## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a school to train eligible residents into the selected teacher’s supported skill.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The building catalogue includes education facilities, but constructing or buying an expert does not establish a teacher/student training cycle.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This selected scope includes school education and productive-work experience from the accepted behavior below; native settlement teaching remains separate.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance, refined 2026-10-17 from the teaching matrix and resolved education rules.

1. Assign an eligible teacher and student → show the skill, required facility and progress before committing the assignment.

2. Schoolhouse/college/university support their documented one/two/three teacher limits and allowed skill levels.

3. Advance the agreed teaching interval → promote the eligible student once along the supported criminal/servant/free/expert progression.

4. Cancel, transfer or lose a participant → handle progress under the agreed policy without duplicating skills or workers. Save/load retains assignments and progress.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [native and european relations](../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations), [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Use the agreed per-tier teaching durations, pause/reset rules and productive-work experience; native teaching remains its own path.

6. A schoolhouse, college and university supports one, two and three qualified teachers respectively. Use each skill's minimum tier from the shared matrix; higher buildings also accept lower-tier skills. One teacher teaches one eligible resident at a time; the student remains in the same colony and keeps its ordinary job. Teachers occupy school slots and do not produce job goods there. Reject unqualified teachers, duplicate participants, self-teaching and students outside the colony before changing state.

7. Training takes 4/6/8 owner colony updates according to the taught skill's minimum tier; each criminal→servant→free step takes 4 updates first. Keep the same unit identity, placement and equipment. Military veteran status remains independent from civilian specialty; veteran teaching uses the military status implemented by R-0111. Completion changes the current step once, reports it and retains a valid following social step until the selected skill is acquired.

8. Removing or losing the teacher pauses the saved course. Reassigning the same skill preserves progress; changing the taught skill resets it. Explicit cancel or the student's departure from the colony clears the course. Saving/loading and merely inspecting choices do not advance training. The player can see teacher, student, target skill, progress and paused state before confirming changes.

9. A free colonist gains the corresponding supported expertise after 20 updates with positive actual output in the same job. Changing the job resets progress; temporarily producing nothing retains it. Food from land and water are separate jobs. Do not credit unattended output or merely potential manufacturing output when inputs are missing. Resolve school completion before work experience so one update cannot award two skills. Preserve the already earned military rank.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Keep teaching and experience rules in the existing model owner. Reuse school construction slots, the shared skill/type catalogue, Colony's one-per-owner-update production path and current-schema persistence. Model queries provide immutable assignment/progress data; commands validate all participants before mutation. GUI consumes those commands without calculating graduation or progress itself.

2. Add persisted colony course data and per-person work history with explicit load validation. Promote the registered student in place so colony fields, slots, selection and existing references retain identity. Extend current unit restoration for every supported school-matrix skill, including Europe-excluded planters, without adding those skills to the European purchase roster. Preserve R-0111's military rank independently. Do not introduce save migrations or native education behavior.

3. Commit education after the surviving residents' actual production is known and before construction completion. For manufacturing, attribute the already-capped actual building output deterministically through the existing expert-first slot order; a worker advances only for a positive share, without consuming inputs twice. Fields and non-consuming buildings use actual staffed output. Pure forecasts remain side-effect free.

4. Reuse the colony's school building and worker-choice patterns for teacher staffing and an assignment/progress dialog. Show required skill tier, social step and progress, allow cancel and same-skill replacement, and report graduation through existing turn feedback. Ensure school controls are actually visible and reachable, including after upgrading the building.

5. Model checks cover capacities, allowed skills, invalid atomic commands, each social step, exact duration, teacher removal/replacement, student departure/cancel, zero/short input, job change, same-update school/work collision, veteran preservation and save/load without replay. Add TC_68_F59 school/player acceptance with assignment/cancel, mid-course Continue and actual graduation, plus productive-work acceptance. Main owns review, formatting, full model/Docker checks, evidence and publication. Detailed bounded worker contracts belong in the handoff.
