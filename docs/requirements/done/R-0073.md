## 1. Summary

1. The player needs an explicit waiting order that suppresses repeated activation and boards eligible people when a ship departs.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): R-0022 provides explicit individual passenger transfers. Persistent sentry orders and their automatic departure boarding behavior remain separate gaps.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected and refined under the remaining-backlog authorization on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Acceptance refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Place an eligible unit on sentry → it stops normal activation until explicitly activated or awakened by the agreed nearby-foreign-unit condition.

2. Depart with a compatible owned ship → board eligible sentries according to available holds and an explicit selection order; retain equipment and identity.

3. People without the order are not silently loaded. Full or incompatible carriers leave passengers ashore with understandable feedback.

4. Cancel activation/boarding or reject a stale departure → no partial transfer. Save/load retains the order and does not board twice.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [exploration, work and transport](../../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport)): Board eligible sentries by stable identity up to actual free capacity, leaving the others waiting.

6. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): Reuse the arrival/action boundaries in [national turn processing](../../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing); resuming a saved turn must not synthesize another arrival or boarding trigger.

7. Sentry and Activate are explicit localized actions for an owned map unit or eligible passenger on the European pier, during the active fully prepared turn. Sentry suppresses automatic selection without spending remaining movement; Activate clears this order and preserves remaining movement, including zero. Selecting a unit alone does not cancel its order. Canceling the action menu changes nothing. A clear order marker identifies waiting units.

8. Visible foreign movement onto a neighboring square wakes a map sentry immediately without changing its remaining movement. Invisible foreign movement must not reveal information or wake it. Boarding uses existing passenger rules: same port/pier costs zero, adjacent land-to-ship costs one passenger move and no ship move. An adjacent exhausted passenger stays ashore. Non-passengers, foreign units and units without sentry are never collected.

9. A successful ship departure from Europe or its map square collects eligible sentries in stable ID order into actual empty holds. A rejected departure leaves all units and orders unchanged. Boarding clears the boarded passenger's sentry order; excess passengers retain it and remain visible ashore. Loading a save neither departs nor boards passengers. Existing manual loading remains available.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model owns validated Sentry/Activate commands, current order queries, movement-triggered wake-up and deterministic departure boarding. Reuse `UnitActionType`, its persisted representation, `AbstractUnit` passenger eligibility/transfers and existing ship movement/departure boundaries. Keep validation before mutations; do not introduce GUI dependencies or a general command framework.

2. Persist sentry explicitly through `UnitActionPoAdapter` and restore without replay. Main checks supported unit types, automatic selection, both map movement paths, Europe departure, cargo capacity, equipment identity and no-partial-effect rejection. Use the [package map](../../architecture.md) and [national turn contract](../../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing).

3. Reuse map order controls and European pier context menus with English/Czech labels, keyboard access, order visibility and expected stale-state feedback. Model queries determine enabled actions. Keep new immigrant queue defaults and priority UI with their own requirements; this scope boards units explicitly placed on sentry.

4. Add focused model boundary tests and a player scenario for map/pier Sentry, Activate/cancel, real departure, full ship leftovers and Continue.
