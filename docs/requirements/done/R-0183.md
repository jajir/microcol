5. Primary area: [A10](../areas.md#a10). Cross-area dependencies: A05 remembered map knowledge, A08 European colonies and A04 one-time turn settlement.

## 1. Summary

1. When Coronado joins, the player learns where every currently existing European colony stands and what terrain immediately surrounds it.
2. This is a one-time discovery. It does not give permanent sight of foreign activity or reveal colonies founded later.

## 2. Behavior

1. At actual founder arrival, reveal each existing European colony tile and all eight immediate neighbors, clipped to the map boundary. This reuses the [agreed radius decision](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0183-refinement).
2. Remember the colony and terrain under ordinary fog rules. Unknown foreign units and private cargo or warehouse contents remain hidden; current visibility is not extended. The owner can distinguish remembered information from current sight.
3. Reveal only for the founder's owner. Selecting, canceling or failing to earn the founder reveals nothing. Native settlements are excluded. Later colonies do not become known through this completed event.
4. Save/Continue retains the discovered terrain and remembered colony information without replaying the effect. Ordinary later sightings continue to update knowledge normally.
