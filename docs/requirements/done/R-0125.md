## 1. Summary

1. A player planning and making a move needs the route, highlighted reach and actual remaining movement to use one terrain cost.
2. The 2026-10-17 source assessment at `f902be8a` reports one-point execution and step-count path/range while a separate terrain-cost function is unused on those paths. This is source inspection, not a runtime result.
3. This requirement owns the shared cost contract and the complete printed base-terrain/forest movement cells. Road building [R-0121](R-0121.md) and river state [R-0126](R-0126.md) own their respective connected one-third discount; their delivery must use this contract. It does not replace the completed legal one-step/coastal-port rules [R-0054](../done/R-0054.md).

## 2. Behavior

1. On each legal destination terrain/forest variant, charge the aid-card-4 entry cost from the [terrain audit](../../gap-analysis.md#terrain-reference-data-audit). Examples: plains and grasslands cost forested/bare 2/1, marsh and swamp 3/2, hills 2, mountains 3, ocean/sea lane 1. A planned route and highlighted reach use the same base cost and legality as actual execution.
2. If a legal entry costs more than the unit's **full** turn allowance, a unit still at that full allowance may take exactly that step and finish at zero. A partly spent unit with insufficient points rejects the move without changing location or movement. Thus full 1 entering cost 3 succeeds once; remaining 1 of full 3 cannot pay cost 2. Zero movement cannot move.
3. Store/compute movement in exact thirds so no sequence of fractional discounts gains or loses a move through rounding. Once a connected road or river segment exists, its owner applies the accepted one-third cost in either direction through the same execution, path and range contract. A disconnected improvement does not grant the discount. The road/river integration is separately accepted with R-0121/R-0126; this requirement does not wait for either to deliver base terrain costs.
4. Failed or stale movement does not partially move or spend points. Save and Continue retain the exact remaining allowance and route behavior without replaying a step. Whole UC-012 remains partial until real connected-road and river cases pass their owning requirements.

5. Accepted on 2026-10-18: ordinary ships may enter and cross fresh-water lakes under the same access, movement cost and transport rules as ocean water. A lake is not a Europe sea-lane exit; water road restrictions apply. See the [shared lake rule](../../gap-analysis.md#terrain-reference-data-audit).

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Implemented the terrain/forest cost matrix and integer-third movement state in the shared model. Actual steps, weighted routes, range and UI affordability use the same entry costs and full-allowance exception. Coastal-port and special terminal-action rules remain in their existing model owners.
2. Saved units now require `movementThirds`; bundled maps, scenario files and generated fixtures use the current schema directly. Invalid missing, negative and over-speed allowances are rejected. No legacy-save migration was introduced.
3. UC-012 acceptance is covered by `TerrainMovementTest`, `MovementAllowanceSaveTest` and `TC_87_F78_terrain_movement_test`: terrain costs, weighted route/range, atomic rejection, exact remaining movement, real UI moves and Save/Continue. Zero movement prevents entering move mode; positive but insufficient movement displays rejection. Connected-road/river integration remains in R-0121/R-0126.
4. Verified on 2026-10-19: 2,397 model checks, selected UI regressions and final three TC87 scenarios plus three source-contract checks pass. See [execution evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0125-terrain-movement-2026-10-19) for exact selections, failures and limits. This is requirement acceptance in the batch branch; batch regression, integration and publication remain separate.
