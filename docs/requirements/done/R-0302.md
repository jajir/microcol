# R-0302 — Correct bundled free-play geography and expose its map contents

1. Status: done
2. Updated: 2026-10-26
3. Related: P02/P04, SETUP-001, MAP-001/MAP-004 and UC-001; [use cases](../../use-cases.md).
4. Dependencies: river placement [R-0301](R-0301.md), authored landscape [R-0287](R-0287.md), river corridors [R-0290](R-0290.md).
5. Primary area: [A05](../areas.md#a05).
6. Related areas: A02 bundled map choices; A03 documented map previews; A07 existing colony sites and resource stock are preserved.
7. Origin: the user requested the same river restrictions for existing New free game choices, correction of the lake in the sea, and visible maps with their features.

## 1. Summary

1. A player choosing a bundled world must receive valid river and lake geography, and be able to inspect a complete map with a legend.
2. Original World has coastal and upland river squares and a freshwater lake directly touching ocean. Original World, Rival Expedition and Reference also misclassify the enclosed water at (15, 12) as ocean. Two Islands already meets these placement rules.
3. Correct the bundled templates used by new games. Existing player saves and unrelated campaign/test scenarios are not rewritten.

## 2. Behavior

1. Every river square, including endpoints, is land other than hills or mountains and has no water neighbour in any of the eight directions.
2. Bundled lakes are enclosed by land: neighbouring lake squares are allowed; ocean/high-sea neighbours, including diagonals, are not. Enclosed water disconnected from the ocean is classified as lake. Ships cannot sail directly from the surrounding ocean into an isolated lake.
3. Original World retains its dimensions, starting expeditions, colonies, all natural terrain families, forest/resource diversity and finite mineral stock. River courses and conflicting resources move; the lake keeps its identity and fishery behind a dry shore. New game, landing, colony founding and Save/Continue remain usable.
4. All four fixed choices get complete, labelled previews with coordinates and a shared legend; Original World additionally gets a readable mainland detail. These are data diagrams with all terrain revealed, not in-game visibility or an invented generated-world preview.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Edit the existing map schemas: reroute Original World rivers, enclose its coastal lake, and relabel the isolated water at (15, 12) in Original World, Rival Expedition and Reference. Preserve resource counts and move associated mineral reserve coordinates with their deposits; remove trees on the new river course.
2. Extend `FreePlayMapsTest` across every `FreePlayMap`; update `OriginalWorldTest` for enclosed freshwater and inland river endpoints while retaining landscape, resource and persistence assertions.
3. Update `TC_24_F15_free_play_setup_test.TC_12_original_world_river_landing_and_colony_survive_continue` and dedicated `FreePlaySetupPage` checks for the new endpoint and blocked ocean-to-lake voyage. Run TC01/TC12 and the page contract, then the mandatory model/smoke gate.
4. Generate SVG/PNG diagrams directly from template terrain/feature coordinates and inspect them visually. Maintain the [map catalogue](../../free-play-maps.md), [shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#terrain-reference-data-audit) and active [R-0209](../canceled/R-0209.md). Detailed results belong in [functional evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0302-bundled-map-geography-2026-10-26); local acceptance is complete.
