## 1. Summary

1. A player checking economic results needs bought and sold quantities and money spent or received separately for each commodity and European nation.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `ffae3396` finds current holdings statistics but no cumulative trade accounts or complete economic report. This is a source assessment, not a runtime test.
3. This requirement owns the trade accounting and economic report together; the other advisor families remain A03 scopes, and market price pressure stays R-0143.

## 2. Behavior

1. Each confirmed European purchase increments bought quantity and actual money spent for its nation and goods type; each confirmed sale increments sold quantity and actual money received. Do not merge purchase with sale or gross with settled tax-adjusted gold. The report shows those four values separately for each relevant nation and commodity, including European rivals as the documented report requires.
2. A canceled, rejected or stale trade changes no counter. A partial trade records its actual confirmed quantity and price once; later dynamic repricing does not rewrite history. Current goods holdings are not substituted for cumulative quantities.
3. The economic advisor view reads the model account, with no hidden unit cargo or unrelated foreign detail revealed. Save and Continue preserve totals without replaying the last transaction or double-counting on report opening.

4. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0145-refinement)): Count actual quantities and net revenue from revolutionary custom-house exports in that nation/commodity trade totals, with an explicit revolutionary-custom-house source label. This accounting does not reopen home Europe or alter the agreed export calculation.
