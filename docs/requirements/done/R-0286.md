## 1. Summary

1. Give the main screen's panel boundaries a restrained hand-drawn appearance consistent with its buttons.
2. The current panel rules are straight CSS borders. The first proposed thick strokes were rejected; the thinner second proposal is agreed.

## 2. Behavior

1. Opening or continuing a game shows a single dark, slightly irregular separator below the toolbar, beside the right panel, above the status bar and between its description, gold and year fields.
2. Separator strokes have the same optical weight and ink as toolbar button outlines, including at wide and narrow window sizes. They do not form heavy frames or double lines.
3. Existing text, icons, keyboard focus, hover explanations, map area and responsive toolbar remain readable and usable in English and Czech. Resizing and Continue preserve the appearance and game state.
4. This decoration is automatic and needs no player action or cancellation. Other screens retain their existing styling.
