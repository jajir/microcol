## 1. Summary

1. Refine the spacing of the two voyage groups in Europe after R-0380. Move them slightly downward so their headings sit clearly below the horizon, and bring each ship row closer to its heading.
2. Use the selected [variant C](../../graphics-assets/europe-voyage-spacing-2026-10-31/variant-c.png), the most compact of the three proposals. It is an ImageGen design reference, not a pixel-exact specification or a screenshot of implemented behavior.

## 2. Behavior

1. Apply the same vertical placement and compact heading-to-row spacing to both “Sailing to Europe” and “Sailing to New World”, including when either list is empty. The groups sit a little lower than the current R-0380 layout; avoid a large relocation within the harbor.
2. Leave a small, clearly visible gap between the heading and the ship selection frame, matching variant C. Text, ship artwork and selection/focus frames must not touch or overlap. Use the same layout in English and Czech.
3. Keep the headings' existing shared font and size, plain appearance without ribbons, and centered alignment. Preserve the ships' 45-pixel image size, horizontal scrolling, selection, voyage identity and remaining-turn information.
4. Keep the hand-drawn routes visually connected to the voyage rows when adjusting their upper portions. Their dock endpoints, the departure button, harbor palette, town, quay, cargo, goods and toolbar retain the R-0380 design. Red review rectangles are not UI.
5. This is a layout refinement only. Boarding, button/drag departure, travel orders, timing, cargo and other player actions retain their existing prerequisites and outcomes. No new action or confirmation is introduced.
6. The headings stay below the painted horizon in every supported window height, because the sea illustration stretches with the window. Heading text is never truncated, in either language, at narrow (1000 px) or wide (2560 px) windows.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Reuse the existing GUI owners: `EuropePanel` places both voyage groups and the route decorations, `PanelHighSeas` owns each group's heading and ship row, and `EuropeBackground` owns the stretched illustration's geometry. No model, AI, save, localization or new control changes are needed under the [architecture contract](../../architecture.md).
2. Place both groups at one shared offset below the painted horizon instead of a fixed distance from the top. The horizon lies at a quarter of the sea illustration above the 112-pixel quay; `EuropeBackground` exposes that position. Exact offsets are routine calibration against the saved reference.
3. Compact the ship row: reduce the voyage row and its scroll area so a selected ship frame sits a few pixels below the heading, keeping room for the horizontal scrollbar without moving ships when it disappears. Keep the existing heading style, 45-pixel artwork, bottom-aligned ships and selection/focus styles.
4. Move only the upper ends of both route decorations with the shifted rows; their lower dock and departure-button ends keep their R-0380 positions. The existing SVG sources and PNG exports are reused unchanged, so no new artwork export is required.
5. Extend `EuropeHarborPage` with `verifyVoyageHeadingSpacing()`: both headings below the horizon, sharing one placement, centered over their own list, not truncated, a 2–12 px gap to the list, at least 2 px to every ship frame, and at most 12 px to a selected frame. Call it in `TC_194_F226_europe_harbor_test`:
   1. `TC_01_independent_scrolls_and_last_ship_trade` — English at 1280×900 and 1000×760.
   2. `TC_02_czech_crowded_harbor` — Czech at 1280×900 and a new 1000×760 step.

   4. `TC_06_F06_buy_and_sell_goods_test.TC_06_moving_goods` — all groups empty.
   5. Retain every gameplay, scrolling and departure assertion, including `TC_04_depart_loaded_ship_with_shared_button`; `verifyContentAlignment` uses the R-0380 heading selector.
6. Acceptance follows the normal [local acceptance policy](../../publishing.md#local-acceptance-policy): model/JUnit, targeted Docker/Xvfb `TC_194_*`, `TC_06_*` and `PageVerificationContractTest`, fixed smoke, documentation checks and strict MkDocs, plus inspection of the saved runtime screenshots against variant C.
