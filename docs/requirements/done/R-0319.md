## 1. Summary

1. Selecting a river square should let the player recognize the same river and coastal opening in the right panel as on the map.
2. Source inspection at `c74f77be` confirms that the map draws a separate river layer while the preview draws only terrain, overlays and permitted occupants. This follow-up extends the completed unknown-preview correction.

## 2. Behavior

1. Clicking an explored river square shows its small or large course, bends, visible connections and any bridge in the existing preview. Resource markers and permitted occupants remain above the river.
2. Selecting a coastal endpoint shows its two open arms; selecting the adjoining explored sea square shows the part of the mouth and island lying on that square. The preview is clipped to the selected square, with its existing side margins.
3. Use only explored river connections and known ocean neighbours. Selecting an unknown square retains the existing fog and location-dependent question mark. The preview must not expose hidden rivers, terrain or occupants.
4. Panning and Save/Continue retain the same appearance. Switching to another square clears the previous picture. Selection outlines, grid and moving water animation are not part of the stationary preview. The action needs no dialog or cancellation; clicking another square changes the selection.
