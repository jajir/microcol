## 2. Behavior

Acceptance refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Move an ordinary unit and a longer-sighted role to the same square in equivalent worlds → reveal the agreed radius for each.

2. Spending movement without changing observation capability does not shrink explored knowledge.

3. Map edges clip sight safely; upgrades/equipment that do not alter sight do not change the radius accidentally.

4. Save/load retains explored terrain. Existing hidden overlays and tooltips stay hidden outside permitted knowledge.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [exploration, work and transport](../../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport)): Use explicit one-square ordinary land sight and two-square scout/ship sight with eight-direction distance, independent of movement speed.

6. For existing equipment roles, an unarmed mounted colonist is a scout (radius two); adding muskets makes a dragoon with ordinary land sight (radius one). Losing a sight capability never erases terrain already discovered. Becoming a scout reveals the larger area immediately when on the map. Units in cargo, Europe or Atlantic transit do not independently reveal map tiles.

7. Integration refined on 2026-10-17: when De Soto actually joins Congress, immediately reveal the minimum two-square radius around every owned on-map unit without moving it. Cargo, indoor workers, Europe, voyages and repair do not reveal independently. Membership applies to existing and newly created units, and to the current owner after transfer. Continue preserves discovery without repeating recruitment. The separate lost-city effect remains outside this sight requirement.
