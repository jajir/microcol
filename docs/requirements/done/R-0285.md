## 1. Summary

1. Replace the shared placeholder pictures with the selected walking family 09, making existing unit types recognizable on the map.
2. Keep contours clear at actual map size and reuse the same artwork throughout the existing unit views.

## 2. Behavior

1. All 35 current types receive their own selected-family picture. Four actual colonist equipment states and damaged artillery remain separate variants, for 40 pictures in total.
2. Actual horses, muskets and tools take precedence over the profession picture in the existing order; removing equipment restores that profession. Profession props are identifiers, not evidence of equipped goods or new capabilities. An unarmed veteran remains visibly without a musket.
3. The drawings have transparent backgrounds, flat color regions and substantial dark contours at 45 × 45 px, visually targeting approximately 2 px rather than hairlines. Complete pictures stay inside their map tile; the existing nationality/action marker remains a separate overlay.
4. Map, unit panels, colony, Europe, combat and illustrated help share the same type/state selection. Existing westward mirroring for caravel, merchantman and galleon stays intact.
5. Unit rules, ownership, visibility, selection, movement, cargo and saved game meaning stay unchanged. The normal map gesture and Continue retain the unit's identity, position and artwork state.
