## 1. Summary

1. Rebels win only after reclaiming every surviving rebellion colony and reducing all surviving royal ground forces to the accepted limit.
2. This requirement owns victory evaluation; R-0192 owns loss and must run first.

## 2. Behavior

1. Maintain the rebellion-colony set: colonies owned at declaration plus later founded/acquired by the rebel, with membership tracking capture and removal on destruction. Victory requires **every surviving member rebel-controlled** and **at most two REF ground units alive total**, including reserve in Europe, carried at sea and landed. REF ships alone do not prevent victory.
2. Evaluate after each completed gameplay action/update only after declaration and after R-0192 loss. Three surviving ground units or one Crown-held member prevents victory; two and all recovered win once. Outside unrelated colonies do not silently enter the set.
3. On victory, persist one terminal result and let scoring R-0195/0196 use completion order; Save/Continue cannot announce or score the same victory twice. UC-085 checks 3→2, hidden reserve/ship cargo, occupied/recovered colonies, same-action loss precedence and reload.

4. Accepted on 2026-10-18: defeated Continental and royal infantry/cavalry are destroyed together with their equipment. They do not downgrade, survive as colonists, or leave recoverable equipment. See the [shared defeat rule](../../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention).

5. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0186](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0186-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.
