## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Use an eligible contact route → show the rival and the tribute request before commitment.

2. Accepted tribute transfers the agreed amount once; refusal applies only the explicitly agreed diplomatic consequence.

3. Cancel or stale/invalid contact → no gold transfer and no implicit war declaration.

4. Save/load preserves resolved/pending state; repeated confirmation cannot mint gold.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [native and european relations](../../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations)): Use eligible visitors, the ten-turn pair cooldown and the strength/treasury-limited tribute response; refusal alone causes no war.

6. Soldiers, dragoons and scouts can demand tribute from a contacted AI colonizing rival during peace or war. Ordinary unarmed, unmounted colonists, ships and wagons cannot make this demand. A confirmed demand succeeds only when the demander's armed land strength is at least twice the rival's; it transfers up to 200 gold, limited by the rival's treasury. The same shared strength definition used for peace applies.

7. A confirmed demand, whether paid or refused, starts the pair's ten-turn cooldown. A canceled, merely viewed or invalidated proposal does not. Refusal, payment and cancellation never declare war or end a war. A zero-gold rival cannot pay; the result explains that no gold was received.

8. The player can postpone the proposal and resume it through Continue. Confirmation rechecks contact, role, relation, quoted balances and strength, and the cooldown before committing. A stale proposal must be canceled and requested again; it cannot silently change the reviewed amount or outcome.
