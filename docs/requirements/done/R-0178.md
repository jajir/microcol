## 1. Summary

1. Royal and Continental forces must fight with their own chart strengths and only receive bombardment or ambush where the revolution rules allow.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue reports no REF-specific strength branch. This requirement does not create the REF roster or transport it; A10 owns those later outcomes.
3. This requirement owns combat-role identities/strengths and battle modifiers consumed by actual deployed forces.

## 2. Behavior

1. Continental infantry/cavalry have base strength 4/5; royal regular/cavalry have 5/6. The roles persist and are distinguishable from ordinary colonial soldiers, retaining ordinary attack/defense legality and the shared resolver. Actual roster growth and delivery belong to A10.
2. Royal regulars attacking a rebel colony receive +50% bombardment. Delivered intervention forces receive the accepted +50% bombardment only against a Tory/Crown-held colony. A nonqualifying role, target or direction receives neither bonus.
3. During independence war, colonial units attacking or defending against the King's regular army outside a colony gain the appropriate terrain-card bonus in forest, hills or mountains, analogous to native ambush: ordinarily forest +50%, swamp forest +75%, hills +100%, mountains +150%. It does not apply inside a colony, before declaration or against an ordinary rival. Preview and resolver use the same effective strengths, terrain/fortification stacking and controlled odds; do not add a second veteran bonus.
4. Accepted on 2026-10-18: defeated Continental and royal infantry/cavalry are destroyed together with their equipment. They do not downgrade, survive as colonists, or leave recoverable equipment. See the [shared defeat rule](../../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention). The resulting living/destroyed REF identity feeds R-0185/R-0191. Cancel/stale battle changes no war, movement, troops or random state. UC-073/082 compare roles, colony versus countryside, before/after declaration, intervention absent/present, loser fates and Save/Continue.

5. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0173](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0173-refinement), [R-0186](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0186-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.
