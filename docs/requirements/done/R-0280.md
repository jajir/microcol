## 1. Summary

1. Replace the current bright square town picture with a compact pen-drawn colony marker that matches the shared map style.
2. Show size, ownership and fortification with a few readable strokes anchored to one map square, with slight visual overhang permitted. A large illustration reduced until its lines disappear does not meet this need.
3. On 2026-10-25 the user selected B — Do hloubky from the [proposal gallery](../../graphics-colony-proposals.md), requested implementation and answered the remaining threshold question: 1–3 residents show one house, 4–7 two houses, 8 or more three houses. Refinement is complete; implementation is authorized.

## 2. Behavior

1. The colony name appears directly below the map marker without a plaque or box. A small national flag identifies its owner.
2. Show one house for 1–3 internal residents, two for 4–7 and three from 8 upward. Units stationed outside do not count. A transient empty colony uses the smallest marker. Population and completed fortification are independent.
3. Each size supports four independent defense states: none, stockade, fort and fortress. Fortification must not be inferred from population alone, including after forced population loss.
4. The marker remains anchored to the existing 45 × 45 logical-pixel tile with a consistent visible pen stroke. The user's subsequent decision permits slight overhang of houses or fortification; the selected artwork extends at most 4 px on either side without covering the colony name. Names and adjacent important map markers must remain readable.
5. Apply the same artwork on the map, in the selected-tile preview and in the colony field panel. The national flag follows the owner, including the corresponding nation during Crown occupation. Keep the existing name placement and stale-observation opacity.
6. Implementation must preserve selection, opening a colony, labels, visibility and remembered-colony information. Hidden growth or fortification changes must not leak through an old observation.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Reuse B vector geometry at the original 1.35 px stroke. Store a transparent 2× atlas in the existing image pipeline: 12 house/defense combinations plus four national flags. `gui.image` selects artwork; `PaintService` draws it at the existing map scale. The sidebar reserves the small horizontal overhang.
2. Read internal population and the highest completed defense from `Colony`. Persist both in `ColonySighting` so every remembered view uses the same immutable observation as its name and owner. Save current facts for visible colonies and preserve hidden observations. Validate required saved fields; current development does not require old save compatibility. Update handcrafted fixture observations.
3. Verify boundary counts 3/4 and 7/8 and all defense states with `ColonyImagesTest`; verify observed growth/defense, loss of sight, reload and malformed observations with `CurrentSightTest`. Preserve `TC_26_F18_fog_of_war_test` assertions. Add `TC_189_F222_colony_markers_test` for all 12 actual map markers, national flag artwork, overhang, the sidebar, opening a colony and Continue. These cover the visual parts of UC-006/UC-031/UC-047/UC-077 without claiming their entire gameplay acceptance.
4. Implementation and local acceptance are complete. The shared formatter, targeted Docker/Xvfb scenarios, all fast model/JUnit tests, fixed smoke gate and documentation checks are recorded in [functional evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0280-colony-markers-2026-10-25). Publication remains independently verified under the project workflow.
