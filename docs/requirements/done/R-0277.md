# R-0277 — Calm terrain colours and sparse living water

1. Status: done
2. Updated: 2026-10-25

5. Primary area: [A03](../areas.md#a03), shared visual language.
6. Related areas: A05 supplies terrain identities and attributes; A04 supplies the existing animation clock. No gameplay or save-schema change.
7. Origin: user approved the refined terrain proposal and implementation on 2026-10-25.

## 1. Summary

1. Players should recognize terrain from its flat palette colour without a repetitive symbol on every square.
2. The current map repeats the selected terrain drawing on every square, while most land types share one background colour.
3. Reuse one selected drawing per terrain, keep meaningful overlays readable, and restore sparse moving water decoration across the map and its shared previews.

## 2. Behavior

1. Opening a game automatically shows one distinct flat background colour per terrain. Colours follow the shared palette; coastal edges match adjacent terrain. No additional action or cancellation is needed.
2. Decoration frequencies on eligible squares are: hills/mountains 100%; ocean/high sea/lakes 5%; grassland/prairie/plains 10%; desert/arctic 15%; savannah/tundra 20%; marsh/swamp 25%. These are probabilities, not a regular grid. Land placement survives panning and Save/Continue.
3. Forests, fields, roads, rivers, known settlements/colonies, visible rumours and resource bonuses suppress ordinary terrain decoration. Hills and mountains retain their relief even with an attribute. Meaningful overlays retain their existing rules and visibility; units do not reshuffle decoration.
4. Water drawings appear and disappear at staggered intervals across eligible squares, retaining approximately 5% coverage. Land drawings remain stationary. Hidden terrain reveals no decoration.
5. Help and terrain selection previews always show the identifying drawing on the matching colour. Forest previews remain distinct from decorative variants. Production, movement, save data and resource outcomes remain unchanged.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Extend the selected SVG/PNG atlas with transparent versions of its existing terrain drawings. Reuse the preview row as the colour reference, and share it through `gui.image`.
2. Extend seeded `ImageRandomProvider` selection and the shared `PaintService` path, using the existing animation tick and model-provided attributes. Tint the existing coastline masks to neighbouring terrain colours without adding model dependencies on GUI.
3. Cover density, determinism, attribute priority, transparency and mixed coast colours with JUnit. Extend `TC_80_F72_terrain_identity_test` for rendered decoration, panning, water changes and Continue; retain `TC_100_F91` reference, `TC_88_F79` road and `TC_91_F82` resource/fog checks.
4. Shared Java formatting, 21 targeted checks, all 4,748 required model/JUnit plus smoke checks, visual review, documentation consistency checks and strict MkDocs passed. Exact selections, failed attempts and final results are recorded in the [functional evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0277-sparse-terrain-2026-10-25). Local acceptance does not verify remote regression or publication.
