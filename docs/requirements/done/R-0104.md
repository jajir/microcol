## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to select a founder, earn their arrival and inspect lasting Congress membership.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The original sentiment requirement explicitly leaves Congress/founders open; construction metadata is not evidence of founder acquisition.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected and refined on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance refined on 2026-10-16.

1. After the agreed eligibility trigger, present candidates from the five founder categories and record the player’s chosen objective.

2. Accumulate the agreed bell progress once; reaching the threshold adds the founder once and reports arrival.

3. Congress shows membership and active effects. Reopening or Continue does not replay arrival or a one-time effect.

4. Cancel or invalid/stale choice preserves progress and membership. Each founder effect has a separately owned acceptance slice; acquisition alone does not complete UC-086–110.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [support, production and congress](../../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress)): Use the persistent five-category candidate offer, increasing bell cost, surplus carry-over and independent local/Congress accounts; John Paul Jones supplies the first real end-to-end effect.

6. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): apply this requirement within [national turn processing](../../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing). Resume the saved completed boundary without repeating production, arrivals or action replenishment; future pending choices must preserve earlier completed updates.

7. The Congress control is available from the map. Before the first colony it explains eligibility. Afterwards show the frozen offer, each candidate's category, current objective, bells/target and members. Closing without choosing changes nothing; a choice locks that objective until arrival.

8. At difficulty factor 1, choose Jones with 99 accumulated bells, produce one bell in an ordinary national update → he joins for 100 and one free frigate reaches home Europe. With 120 bells the first arrival leaves 20 toward the next target of 200. Join at most one per national update; while choice is pending, bells wait. Local bell storage is not spent. Reopening and Continue preserve the choice/offer and never replay the award.

9. Following independence, Jones's frigate uses the lowest-ID eligible reachable coastal colony; temporary obstruction retains one pending delivery. Membership and the delivery obligation persist separately. Other fathers can be selected and retained; their individual effects remain owned by the corresponding catalogue entries and dependent requirements, not claimed complete by this acquisition requirement.

10. Magellan integration selected on 2026-10-17 uses the accepted
    [founder details](../../gap-analysis.md#founder-details): all owned ship types
    have one additional map movement point. Other owners and land units are
    unchanged. Ships replenish through the existing owner-turn boundary;
    actual founder arrival precedes that replenishment. Newly created ships
    use the same effective allowance; saved spent movement is not replenished
    merely by Continue.

11. A new west-edge departure to Europe halves its already sampled ordinary
    duration, rounding up with minimum one. All other routes retain their
    ordinary duration. The ordinary formula uses base ship speed, excluding
    the founder movement bonus. Existing voyages keep their departure-time
    duration and remaining progress after recruitment and Continue.

After colonies, settle at most one selected founder, carry surplus, generate the next frozen offer and record arrival.
