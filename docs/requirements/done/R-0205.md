## 1. Summary

1. A player inspecting a known square or owned unit can understand terrain, work, movement and available orders without accidentally issuing one.
2. This requirement completes permitted local details and bounded foreign hold occupancy; R-0206 owns rules help.

## 2. Behavior

1. Inspecting a known tile shows terrain/forest/resource/improvement where actually discovered, legal move cost/range, present own units and orders, current date and treasury, and owned colony buildings/status plus cargo/stocks. Legal actions show prerequisites and unavailable reasons from the model; inspection and map navigation alone spend no movement or goods.
2. A visible foreign ship discloses occupied-hold count only, not cargo identities. Unknown terrain, hidden units and foreign private colony stocks remain undisclosed. A stale selection rechecks the live object and returns safely; switching viewpoint/Continue updates visible facts.
3. UC-114 checks known/unknown tile, own/foreign unit, order/moves, date/treasury/colony buildings/status/stock, foreign hold boundary, keyboard/mouse inspection and nonmutation.

4. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0130](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0130-refinement), [R-0131](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0131-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.

5. Disband confirmation consumes R-0131’s exact equipment retention/loss quantities, including tools and explicitly confirmed warehouse overflow; cancellation changes nothing.
