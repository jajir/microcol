## 1. Summary

1. A player at sea should be able to discard a chosen amount of ship goods deliberately, without accidentally losing passengers or another hold.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue source assessment at `ffae3396` credits a partial goods-removal operation but finds no player jettison control. That existing removal is reused.
3. Scope: validated ship/hold/quantity selection, confirmation and one goods decrease; ordinary selling and unloading remain separate.

## 2. Behavior

1. On an owned ship at sea, the player selects one goods hold and a quantity from 1 through its current amount, reviews the discard and confirms it. Only that hold loses the chosen amount; the ship stays in place and receives no gold.
2. Passengers, equipment and every other hold remain unchanged. Jettison cannot choose a passenger or empty hold. An unavailable ship, wrong owner, stale amount or changed hold rejects without any discard.
3. Cancel/Escape changes nothing. A repeated obsolete confirmation cannot discard twice. Save and Continue retain the reduced goods quantity and never repeat the command on load.

4. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0138-refinement)): Permit confirmed jettison of a selected hold quantity during an Atlantic crossing as well as on map sea tiles. Change only cargo, never crossing duration, direction or other holds/passengers.
