## 1. Summary

1. A player needs to send a unit to a destination beyond its current turn and regain manual control by explicitly canceling that order.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue source assessment at `f902be8a` reports a current-movement path limit and no stored destination. It is a source observation, not runtime acceptance.
3. This requirement owns saved go-to destination, multi-turn continuation and its cancellation. It reuses R-0125 path/cost and R-0054 legality; sentry [R-0073](R-0073.md), fortify [R-0106](R-0106.md), pioneer work R-0119–R-0121 and trade routes have their own activate/cancel acceptance.

## 2. Behavior

1. During the current player's turn, choose a reachable map square or named friendly destination even when the route needs at least three turns. The order shows its target and follows legal steps within each turn's available movement; it does not use a current-turn-only distance limit.
2. On later owner turns the unit continues toward the same target, using current shared terrain costs. Arrival ends the order and restores ordinary manual control. If a target or route becomes unavailable, the unit makes no illegal step and cannot hold the turn in an infinite loop; the player receives a reason and a way to cancel or repair the order.
3. Explicit Activate/cancel clears the go-to order without moving the unit or restoring spent movement. Selection or inspection alone does not cancel it. A stale/foreign order request or changed destination confirmation rejects without partial movement/order change; named destinations offered to the player must remain friendly and reachable.
4. Save and Continue preserve destination and progress mid-route. Loading never advances the unit, repeats arrival or changes its movement allowance. Whole UC-013 includes map and named-destination variants; UC-016 still needs other order owners and disband.

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0130-refinement)): Try another legal path to the same destination first. If none exists, pause the order, retain its destination and recheck on the next owner turn; allow explicit cancellation. A named destination that disappears or becomes hostile cancels the order. Report the pause/cancellation and its reason in persisted turn notifications, without restoring movement or advancing on load.

Continue once per owner turn with the shared legal step operation, never during loading or inspection; arrival clears the order and retains any legitimate unused movement.
