## 1. Summary

1. A player's heavy sales or purchases should move each nation's market price, with weaker pressure reaching other European ports and quiet goods recovering toward their starting price.
2. The 2026-10-17 source catalogue at `ffae3396` reports one fixed shared market, not national histories. R-0020 already owns the quote and tax arithmetic used to settle an individual transaction.
3. This requirement owns per-nation, per-good bid/ask and signed pressure; R-0144 supplies the Dutch factor. It does not change a transaction's already confirmed amount.

## 2. Behavior

1. Each nation's tradable commodity starts at its existing bid and spread; a missing tradable quote starts bid 2, ask 4. A confirmed sale increases signed pressure; purchase decreases it. Apply the full traded quantity to the actor's market and one quarter to each other European market, retaining fractional remainder.
2. Every +100 pressure lowers bid by 1; every −100 raises it by 1. Retain remainder, bound bid to 1–20 and set ask to bid plus its initial spread. At either bound discard outward pressure so it cannot bank a later reversal. On a market update with no trade affected that commodity since its previous update, bid moves one step toward its initial value.
3. Settle each trade at its confirmed quote, then update affected markets. A rejected/canceled trade has no pressure, money or cargo effect. Markets can diverge by nation; Save/Continue retains prices, fractional pressure and whether a quiet recovery update already ran.
4. [R-0144](R-0144.md) halves the pressure received by the Dutch market. Full Dutch UC-058 acceptance requires that integration; this requirement supplies the common market engine without duplicating the national rule.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Extend the existing Europe model owner with one persisted market per EuropeanNation and tradable GoodsType: current bid, initial bid/spread, exact signed pressure and a traded-since-update flag. Use integer eighths (own quantity ×8, cross-market ×2) so the later R-0144 half-receiver factor can preserve fractional effects without a second price store. R-0143 uses the normal factor for every nation; Dutch integration remains separately owned. Kings have no independent market.
2. Retain existing initial quotes, fallback bid/ask 2/4 only for a missing tradable quote. Apply thresholds at ±800 eighths, preserve the remainder, clamp bid to 1–20 and discard outward pressure at a bound; ask always uses original spread. Run quiet recovery once at the colonial nation's owner-update boundary, only when no own or cross-market trade affected that good since its prior update. Opening screens and Continue must not advance recovery.
3. Player.quoteEuropeTrade is the shared actor-national quote. Each accepted purchase/sale settles its complete confirmed amount first, then applies pressure once to all markets. Include existing equipment transactions, rush-tool imports and custom-house exports (full traded quantity, including existing reduced-price export settlement); no pressure for canceled, rejected, preview-only or stale-quote operations. Preserve existing boycott/tax/gold/cargo validation and atomicity; add model-side offered-quote checks where dialogs currently lose that information. No halfway repricing of a bulk transaction.
4. Audit all market consumers, including Europe display, trade dialogs, equipment, tax valuation, rush construction, custom house, AI and naval loot. Use the relevant actor's national quote; loot valuation belongs to the prospective recipient. Preserve working public trade entry points used by R-0091; do not change its objective/strategy or repair its tests. Isolate only R-0143 hunks in shared Player.java. Store market data in EuropePo, not the unrelated dirty PlayerPo fields.
5. Persist complete current market state, fractions and recovery flags with bounds/code validation. Update bundled maps, scenarios and test fixtures to the current schema directly; no save migration. Keep creation of new-game initial state separate from validation of saved state. A malformed present state must reject rather than silently repair history. Inspection and loading have no settlement side effects.
6. Add focused model arithmetic/transaction/round-trip tests: threshold and quarter remainders, both bounds/reversal, cross-market quiet flags, settled quote, rejected/canceled/stale trade, equipment/rush/export entry points and once-per-national-update recovery. Add TC_94_F85_national_market_pressure_test for UC-058 using actual Europe buy/sell controls, bid/ask feedback, stale confirmation and Continue. Preserve existing monetary expectations unless a real accepted market transition explains a changed value; do not weaken assertions.
7. Reuse architecture, functional/page-verification and shared Java formatter contracts. No new dependency or speculative route framework. Main reviews and tests targeted affected trade/production/UI paths, updates evidence and isolates the accepted commit; batch integration/publication remain the ten-requirement checkpoint.
