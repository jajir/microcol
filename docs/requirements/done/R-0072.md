## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to recall a ship before arrival without losing its passengers, goods or recorded coast.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Current transit supports departure and arrival but no reversal command or agreed remaining-time calculation for reversal.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected under the remaining-backlog authorization on 2026-10-16; refinement is complete; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Acceptance refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Select an in-transit ship → see its direction and remaining journey and an eligible reverse-course action.

2. Confirm reversal in either direction → change its destination and display the agreed remaining time while retaining cargo and origin.

3. Cancel, repeat an obsolete confirmation or act after arrival → leave transit and cargo unchanged.

4. Save and continue after reversal → keep the new direction/time and arrive once at the correct legal destination; blocked-map arrival retains the existing wait policy.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [exploration, work and transport](../../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport)): Reverse using the stored route position and duration, with no reroll or full-voyage restart; repeated reversals preserve actual progress.

6. With original duration four and one owner update already spent, reversal takes one update back to the source. Reversing again before another update restores three updates to the original destination. Reversal at departure returns immediately to the source without advancing the calendar. If that map square is occupied, the ship waits offshore at zero with unchanged cargo; it does not attack or choose another tile.

7. In Europe, each owned in-transit ship exposes a labeled Reverse course action next to its remaining time. Confirmation states the new destination and remaining updates. Cancel/Escape changes nothing; confirmation refreshes both transit lists or the destination port immediately. Controls and feedback are localized in English and Czech and usable by keyboard. The action is available only during the owner's active, fully prepared turn; a stale confirmation is rejected with feedback and refreshed state.

8. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): Apply the persisted voyage state at the next Europe preparation stage of [national turn processing](../../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing); reversing or loading does not also spend an extra owner update.
