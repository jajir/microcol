## 1. Summary

1. Every chart building must enforce its real hammer, tool, population, predecessor and founder limits and produce its stated effect.
2. This requirement owns the exhaustive table acceptance, not another construction queue or building rules engine.

## 2. Behavior

1. A project whose hammers or tools are short waits with nothing spent. Enough stock completes it once, spends exactly its chart cost and keeps any surplus.
2. A project below its population, predecessor or founder gate is refused without changing the colony, queue or stock. A completed building delivers its stated effect once.
3. Newspaper population is 4; the R-0025 corrections (carpenter, church, fort and newspaper population) remain in force.
4. Cancel, rush, upgrade and saved partial progress apply to each row where they can occur and are mapped to named checks in the ledger.
5. UC-031 and UC-042–050 have one result per chart row and representative player paths. A failed row is repaired in its existing owner.
6. The accepted 2026-10-20 rules in [R-0146](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0146-refinement) and [R-0177](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0177-refinement) do not change any chart gate; this requirement does not implement them again.
