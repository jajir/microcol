## 1. Summary

1. A European rival should defend a threatened colony and, when relations permit, pursue a feasible land offensive without illegal attacks or endless turns.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` describes first-colony delivery but no general military strategy; source observation is not a new AI journey test.
3. This requirement owns bounded land threat response and target selection/pursuit. Combat odds, capture rules and strategic transport are separate.

## 2. Behavior

1. With a known threat to its own colony, the rival can move legal armed defenders to a garrison/defensive position without vacating a food job or inventing equipment. When the threat leaves, it may resume other objectives under the agreed priority. A garrison uses ordinary fortification and combat rules.
2. With a known reachable opponent unit or colony and sufficient legal force, a rival at war chooses and advances toward a bounded land objective, then attacks only through shared movement/combat validation. A weaker target may be preferred when the agreed strategy is refined; AI cannot see unexplored targets or invent units/resources.
3. Peace/alliance forbids unauthorized attacks; relation changes or a target disappearing cause a safe replan/end-turn. Armed capture uses R-0026; R-0096's civilian-defense/whole-colony residual must be honored when applicable, rather than an AI-only shortcut.
4. If no valid threat or offensive target/path/force exists, finish the turn. Save/Continue retains or safely retires the objective; repeated turns do not duplicate movement or attacks. UC-068 observes a seeded threatened-colony response, purposeful land battle and no-target bounded case after a functioning economy.

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0170-refinement)): A known wartime enemy within two tiles of a colony gives defense priority. Move available armed defenders without removing a worker necessary for food. Leave at least one defender in each threatened colony before sending an offensive force; attack only at a computed win probability of at least 60%. Rank reachable known targets by distance, then higher win probability, then stable ID. This policy does not initiate war against peaceful rivals. Prepared reference scenarios require defense response within three turns and an offensive battle within ten, without inventing resources or targets.
