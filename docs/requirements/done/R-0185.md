## 1. Summary

1. Players should see a real prewar REF whose families and growth reflect Crown bell pressure before independence.
2. This requirement owns roster accounting and disclosure, not shipping or battlefield deployment; R-0186 owns those later actions.

## 2. Behavior

1. Each Crown begins with `4+d` royal regular infantry, `2+d` royal cavalry, two artillery and two men-of-war, using its nation's difficulty index. Before declaration, each accumulated `ceil(500/k)` bells produced by the colonial nation adds one ground unit cycling infantry, cavalry, artillery. Preserve the threshold remainder.
2. Whenever ground count exceeds six times ship count, add one man-of-war. After declaration, bell production no longer grows the roster. Destruction of a real REF unit does not recreate it; distinguish reserve, shipped and destroyed counts.
3. Congress displays actual family totals and state without exposing an invented force. Save/Continue preserves growth/remainder and does not double-add a unit at a threshold. UC-082 checks starting profiles, exact threshold, cycle, ship boundary and post-declaration freeze.

4. Accepted on 2026-10-18: defeated Continental and royal infantry/cavalry are destroyed together with their equipment. They do not downgrade, survive as colonists, or leave recoverable equipment. See the [shared defeat rule](../../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention).

5. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0186](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0186-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.
