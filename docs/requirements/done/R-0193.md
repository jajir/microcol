## 1. Summary

1. The game needs distinct last-colony and scored-play deadlines, with limited unscored continuation.
2. This requirement owns automatic date transitions; R-0194 owns the player's Exit/Retire choices.

## 2. Behavior

1. At entry to year 1600, a nation with zero owned colonies loses even if its last colony disappeared earlier; from then onward losing the last colony ends ordinary play. Before 1600, a zero-colony position does not end solely for that reason, and no fixed turn-count substitute applies. At entry to spring 1800, a nation without declared revolution records its score once and may continue **without scoring** until spring 1850.
2. A revolution already underway continues scored play past 1800 until victory/loss or spring 1850; if unresolved at 1850 it loses. Spring 1850 ends all play. A nation cannot declare after the accepted pre-1800 boundary.
3. The 1800 result is frozen: later unscored turns, retirement, loss or Save/Continue cannot replace its score. UC-111 checks differing start dates/turn counts, zero colonies already at 1600 entry, last colony lost just after 1600, revolution just before 1800, unresolved 1850, one score and resume.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Use the existing model calendar and game-end owners to distinguish scored play, frozen-score continuation and terminal play. Scope automatic date policy to free play; preserve explicit campaign mission deadlines and goals. Replace the fixed turn-count zero-colony rule with the agreed year boundary.
2. Freeze a single immutable per-game result identity and the R-0195/R-0196 component snapshot at the scored boundary. Persist it together with continuation state and validate consistency on load. Subsequent unscored turns, losses and repeated reads must not replace it. This is in-game history; cross-game ranking remains R-0197.
3. At the ordinary 1800 boundary show the frozen result and offer the permitted unscored continuation through an existing dialog pattern, with explicit EN/CS feedback. Do not permit further play at 1850 or change the pre-1800 declaration guard. R-0194 retains voluntary Exit/Retire choices.
4. Model tests cover 1599/1600, distinct start dates and turn counts, last-colony loss, 1799/1800 rebel and nonrebel paths, 1849/1850, one-time frozen components, and Save/Continue. Add TC162 for actual boundary feedback, continuation and reload, with assertions in page verify methods. Preserve stable test IDs and update authored scenarios for any required schema.
5. Main reviews and formats changed Java, runs all JUnit plus selected Docker/Xvfb UI scenarios, and performs full two-worker acceptance at the batch boundary.
