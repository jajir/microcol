## 1. Summary

1. A player needs visible small/large rivers that persist and give the documented yield and connected-travel benefit.
2. The 2026-10-17 source assessment at `f902be8a` reports no river course/size state or corresponding production and movement branch. This is historical source inspection, not a player test.
3. This requirement owns river segments, size, persistence and their accepted effects. It reuses R-0125 movement and R-0124 output; it does not own road construction, map generation policy or all production modifiers.

## 2. Behavior

1. A river tile shows and retains whether the river is small or large and which adjacent segments form its course. Inspecting or loading it does not change that state. A route only receives river travel benefit when its traversed segment is connected along the course.
2. A small river adds 1 and a large river adds 2 to an **already positive** tile output before job and percentage modifiers. A zero output remains zero. The colony's preview and next production update use the same river value; a river is not counted twice at a crossing or after Continue.
3. Moving along a connected river segment in either direction costs exactly one-third move under the [accepted rule](../../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport). Execution, path and highlighted range agree through [R-0125](R-0125.md). An adjacent tile without a connected course receives its ordinary terrain cost, and an invalid/stale move preserves location and points.
4. Save and Continue preserve river size, connections and movement/production effects, without replaying a move or producing goods on load. Whole UC-008 also includes special resources and remains separately open.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model/map own fixed-map river size and undirected connections. Store small/large size per land tile and explicit adjacent endpoint pairs for course segments; one shared segment gives reciprocal travel. Validate location bounds, land endpoints, known sizes, no duplicate/self/non-neighbor segments and river presence at both endpoints before constructing usable state. An absent river block means no river, following existing optional map improvements; malformed present data must reject. No save migration or generated placement policy is added.
2. Extend existing WorldMap/WorldMapPo and Terrain owners without a new package. Expose size and connected-step queries to the existing shared entry-cost method. A legal land step with a connected river segment costs one third in either direction; road and river discounts do not stack. Merely adjacent river tiles without a segment and crossing a course receive ordinary cost unless a connected road independently applies. Ships retain existing movement and land legality. Path search, range, execution and preview continue using the shared cost method.
3. Add small/large river +1/+2 in Terrain's flat production stage only when the selected commodity already has positive output; preserve zero, existing plow addition and later worker/job modifiers. Terrain queries used by field preview and actual production must share this result. No resource overlays, road-output bonus, depletion or whole production rewrite belongs here.
4. Draw known river courses and distinguish their sizes through existing map rendering, including connected endpoints; unseen tiles must not leak river state. Reuse the current graphics/UX brief and bilingual inspection feedback if adding text. Fixed authored fixture data supplies courses for acceptance; generator/editor authoring controls remain outside this change. Merely inspecting or continuing must not alter the map or grant production.
