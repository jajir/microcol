## 2. Behavior

1. Start with an empty tobacconist's house, four tobacco leaves, no cigars, fifty food and three indoor residents. The town commons provide four food and no tobacco. Before assignment, tobacco and cigars have no change and food has a two-unit deficit.
2. Assign a fourth ordinary colonist to the tobacconist's house through the colony UI. Without closing the colony, tobacco shows `4` and red `-3`, cigars show `0` and green `+3`, and food shows `50` and red `-4`. The actual stock and change text must be fully readable after scrolling to the goods row.
3. After one turn, food is 46, tobacco is 1 and cigars are 3. The next visible tobacco/cigar changes are red `-1` and green `+1`: the remaining input limits production.
4. After the second turn, food is 42, tobacco is 0 and cigars are 4. No positive cigar change or negative tobacco change remains. After a third turn, food is 38 and both tobacco and cigars remain at 0 and 4 despite the assigned worker.
5. Keep the original factory-rounding and other industrial scenarios, their expected stocks and stable IDs. Expected fixture amounts are independent constants, not values obtained from the production forecast being checked.
