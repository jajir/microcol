## 1. Summary

1. The player needs founder-restricted projects to become available only after the relevant founder joins Congress.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): R-0025 explicitly excludes founder acquisition and founder-dependent gates. Existing population/predecessor checks remain accepted.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected and refined on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance refined on 2026-10-16.

1. Without Adam Smith, deny the six factory projects listed by the manual; joining him unlocks only the otherwise eligible projects.

2. Without Peter Stuyvesant, deny the custom house; joining him unlocks it when other prerequisites hold.

3. The construction choice explains the missing founder. A stale confirmation rechecks membership without changing the queue or stocks.

4. Save/load preserves membership and eligibility; acquisition must not silently complete or enqueue a building.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [founder details](../../gap-analysis.md#founder-details), [economy, production and education](../../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Keep the catalogue's six Smith-gated factories and Stuyvesant custom house; capture preserves completed buildings while future/unfinished work checks the new owner's membership.

6. With the predecessor and eight residents present, ironworks, cigar factory, textile mill, rum factory, fur factory and arsenal remain unavailable without Smith. Recruiting him unlocks all six without changing other prerequisites or adding a project. Stuyvesant unlocks only the custom house.

7. A project admitted by an owner who has its founder pauses after transfer to an owner without that founder. Both normal completion and bought completion preserve the project's materials while blocked. Completed buildings survive transfer. Restoring eligibility lets the retained head complete once; Continue preserves the same condition and stock.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. `ConstructionType` exposes the required founder for the six factories and custom house. `ColonyBuildingQueue` shares a missing-founder reason between admission and completion, including rush quotes; the current owner's saved Congress membership is authoritative. Keep existing predecessor/population/repeat checks and existing completed structures.

2. The picker and current-project status translate the missing founder by name. Bind its confirmation snapshot to current membership as well as existing state. No separate unlock flag, save migration or duplicated UI gate is needed.

3. `FounderConstructionTest` covers all seven projects, unrelated members, remaining prerequisites, owner transfer, retained stocks, normal/rush completion and save/load. Preserve generic admission tests by explicitly establishing their founder prerequisites. `TC_57_F48_founder_construction_test` visits Congress, recruits Smith/Stuyvesant through ordinary production, verifies blocked/unlocked projects and completes a project through the actual queue. Regress TC32, TC50 and TC56 under the functional contract.
