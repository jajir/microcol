## 1. Summary

1. A missionary may pay to urge a tribe against a chosen European rival, with a real possibility of refusal and a real AI target on success.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` reports no paid-target diplomacy. This is source inspection, not runtime acceptance.
3. This requirement owns quote, confirmed attempt, result and target order. R-0156 chooses legal native attacks afterward.

## 2. Behavior

1. Quote the selected target and `ceil(500 × (1 + own hostility/100) / (1 + 0.1 × own missions in this tribe))` gold. A confirmed ordinary missionary succeeds 25% of the time, an effective expert 50%; Brebeuf grants the latter to ordinary missionaries through R-0163.
2. Charge only a confirmed affordable attempt, once. On success, set tribal anger T toward the selected European to at least 75 and give the tribe a persisted target that its legal native AI pursues. A failed confirmed attempt pays for that attempt but sets no new target; cancellation, insufficient gold or stale eligibility changes no money, target, relationship or random state.
3. Show the real price/risk before confirmation. Save/Continue preserves a pending attempt and a successful target without reroll or double debit. UC-067 checks target isolation, price directions, ordinary/expert/Brebeuf draws, acceptance/refusal and subsequent native action.

4. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0166-refinement)): A successful incitement target persists for 20 turns of that native nation, replacing the proposed ten-turn lifetime. Prefer legal known targets of the paid European power; if no eligible paid target exists, use ordinary hostile-target/patrol behavior. End the order when the target nation ceases to exist or relations fall below hostile; a new successful incitement replaces the old target. During those 20 native turns, pursue the nearest known reachable target of the paid nation beyond the ordinary two-tile raid/home-patrol radius, using only known legal paths. This grants no hidden-map knowledge; with no reachable known paid target, use ordinary behavior.
