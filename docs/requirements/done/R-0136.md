## 1. Summary

1. A player sailing into an owned coastal colony expects eligible passengers to step ashore without manually dragging each one from the hold.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue source assessment at `ffae3396` reports carrier movement without a colony-arrival passenger hook. R-0022 already owns explicit individual transfer, which stays available elsewhere.
3. Scope: one automatic own-colony arrival transition; ordinary shore landing choice and Atlantic-arrival waiting remain separately owned.

## 2. Behavior

1. When an owned ship legally enters its owner's coastal colony, each carried passenger becomes an outside-colony unit on that tile, retaining identity and equipment. Their holds become available. No passenger silently becomes a colony worker.
2. The arrival processes its passenger set once. Remaining goods stay aboard and ship location/movement follow the normal legal move. Merely opening the colony, selecting the ship or loading a save does not trigger unloading.
3. If arrival is rejected before movement, ship, passengers and holds remain unchanged. A stale/wrong-owner action cannot unload a passenger into a now-ineligible colony. Open-coast landfall retains the player's individual selection under CARGO-008.
4. Save/Continue preserves the post-arrival outside units and free holds without duplicating people; the pre-arrival ship retains its passengers until a real legal arrival.
