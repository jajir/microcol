## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a rival that sustains its first settlement and uses the same production and food rules as a human.

2. Current situation: R-0027 finds a known legal site and founds the first colony. It does not promise continuous workforce/economic management after founding.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected for development in the Claude batch on 2026-10-17; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Accepted behavior under the resolved workforce policy:

1. After founding, the rival assigns available workers to valid jobs and maintains food when the known local resources make this possible.

2. With a bounded reference scenario, advance ten turns after founding → observe a surviving colony and actual resource production, not only a placed colony marker.

3. If food or a legal job is unavailable, the rival chooses a defined fallback and ends its turn; it does not create supplies or bypass occupancy.

4. Save/load preserves workforce and progress, subject to the [shared-square rules](../../gap-analysis.md#shared-colony-working-squares): contradictory saved job references are rejected, not silently relocated. Decisions use the rival’s knowledge and the same assignment preconditions as a human, not hidden player information.

5. Accepted workforce policy (user decision, 2026-10-17; [rival colony workforce](../../gap-analysis.md#rival-colony-workforce)): Food comes first; assign spare workers to legal lumber jobs after sustaining the colony. If food cannot be sustained, use the best available legal food jobs and finish the turn without creating supplies. The ten-turn reference scenario must demonstrate survival and actual food/lumber production; this does not promise famine immunity or an optimal strategy.

Reuse its real robot-turn driver and deterministic expedition, then verify actual food/resource production and continuation over ten turns after founding, with spare workers producing lumber and a separate insufficient-food case that terminates legally without fabricated stocks. Never implement survival by editing stock or bypassing model assignments.

3. Required targeted verification: Extend FirstColonyJourneyTest and TC_34 beyond founding; verify stocks, legal jobs and bounded turn completion. Allocate any new scenario IDs during refinement; old unimplemented proposals do not reserve TC numbers. Follow the [functional test contract](../../functional-tests.md).

Do not alter founding, move distant units into the colony, override unavailable orders or read hidden foreign information. Actual production and consumption remain model-owned.
