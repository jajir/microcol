## 1. Summary

1. An eligible European learns the settlement's offered skill by staying with friendly natives.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `8f3ce118` has no native teaching handler. This is source assessment, not a tested player result.
3. This requirement owns the pupil's course and completion. A08 owns the settlement, offered skill and relationship; Europe availability is not inferred from a skill's native-teachable mark.

## 2. Behavior

1. At a settlement with content or uneasy relations, an unskilled free colonist or indentured servant may accept its offered card-marked skill. The offer shows the skill and four owner updates. A criminal or existing specialist cannot start; an equipped pioneer is judged by the underlying person and any equipment restriction established by the shared role rules.
2. One pupil at a time occupies the settlement's teaching place. After four owner updates there, the same person gains the offered specialty once. The result and course progress remain intact across Save/Continue; the school course is independent.
3. Declining leaves the person unchanged. Canceling an accepted course releases the person without skill and discards progress; hostility cancels it safely. Invalid or stale acceptance leaves the person and settlement state unchanged.
4. UC-053 checks both the complete card-marked teaching set and the separately approved Europe-excluded subset: a star alone does not bar a specialty from Europe. UC-063 checks eligible/ineligible roles, offer versus learned result, decline, cancel and completion.
