## 1. Summary

1. A Dutch player needs a more stable market under the same volume of trade, including pressure caused by other nations.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `ffae3396` reports fixed prices for all nations. R-0143 supplies dynamic national markets; this requirement owns only the Dutch multiplier.
3. It does not alter the selected nation's identity, its starting merchantman or the accepted quote/tax settlement.

## 2. Behavior

1. A Dutch market receives half the signed pressure per traded unit compared with an otherwise identical non-Dutch market, for both its own trade and quarter-strength pressure arriving from another European port. Retain fractional pressure so repeated one-unit trades accumulate correctly.
2. Under equal controlled trade volume, Dutch bid/ask moves half as rapidly once thresholds are crossed; the shared 1–20 bid bounds, spread and quiet recovery rule remain unchanged. A Dutch seller's confirmed transaction still settles at its pre-update quote.
3. Cancel/reject causes no pressure; Save/Continue retains the half-unit remainder from Dutch own trade and the one-eighth-unit remainder from another nation's trade (one quarter cross-port × one half Dutch factor), with the subsequent price trajectory. Other national markets keep their ordinary factor.
