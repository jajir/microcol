## 1. Summary

1. A trader may accept a native gold offer, try one haggle, give goods away or decline without silently losing cargo.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` finds no native sale/offer handler; no runtime result is claimed.
3. This requirement owns settlement buying from the European carrier and its gift path. Native goods offered back are R-0161.

## 2. Behavior

1. For offered loaded goods that the settlement can buy, the base native buy price is `max(1, floor(Europe bid × 0.75))` per piece; goods on its wanted list receive a further ×1.5, and ship receipts receive a final ×0.8. Floor once on the full quote after applicable multipliers. Available cargo and real settlement treasury cap the confirmed quantity; preview shows the final goods/gold change.
2. One haggle requests +10% receipts and succeeds with 50% chance when content, 25% when uneasy; otherwise it fails and the original offer remains. Retain the pending quote and draw so reopening or Save/Continue cannot reroll. Confirmed sale removes actual goods and transfers actual gold exactly once; the settlement's last bought good updates and R-0154 receives the trade/arms-sale event.
3. A gift removes confirmed goods without gold and applies R-0154's proportional reconciliation, including capital effect. Decline/cancel before any confirmed haggle or stale stock, cargo, mood or treasury changes no goods, gold or relations and draws no result. If a haggle was already confirmed, its saved draw remains consumed even when the player later declines the sale; no sale or gift is inferred from that draw. After a completed sale or gift, R-0161 may offer settlement goods.
4. UC-064 checks wanted/unwanted cargo, wagon versus ship, quantity/treasury cap, haggle outcomes, gift/cancel, 50-arms threshold and Save/Continue.

5. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0159](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0159-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.
