## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a negotiation in which peace takes effect only when the proposal is accepted.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): R-0026 persists symmetric peace/war and enforces hostility. The report intentionally does not offer a unilateral peace toggle.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected and refined on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Contact an eligible wartime rival → see the peace offer and any conditions before choosing.

2. Accepted peace updates both sides once and stops ordinary hostile AI actions; rejected/canceled negotiation leaves war in force.

3. A later confirmed treaty-breaking attack follows the existing R-0028 warning and revalidation.

4. Save/load preserves a pending/finished negotiation without applying its result twice.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [native and european relations](../../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations)): Use contact eligibility, the strength-based peace quote and explicit acceptance/cancel behavior, including Franklin's modifier.

6. With Franklin already recruited, the next ordinary owner-turn update creates a priced peace offer from each eligible contacted wartime rival. Existing pending offers are retained; creating or restoring an offer never pays or establishes peace. The turn report announces a newly created offer so the player can review it in European relations. Recruiting Franklin during an update takes effect for this automatic offer on the following update.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model supplies eligible visiting colonists and a quoted peace negotiation for the current player and a wartime AI rival. Contact needs a visible adjacent rival unit or colony; ships and wagons cannot act as visiting colonists. Sum existing base military strengths of living armed land units, excluding ships/civilians. Price is `ceil(200 × max(1, rival strength / max(1, actor strength)))`; Franklin halves the price, rounding up to whole gold.

2. Store one pending offer per diplomatic pair alongside the saved relation. Its actor/visitor identity, quoted cost, strengths, treasuries and Franklin membership bind confirmation to the reviewed state. Restore a pending offer without accepting or repricing it. An explicit cancel clears only the pending offer; postponing preserves it for Continue. Accept validates the live participants/contact/war, unchanged quote and both resulting treasuries before the single gold transfer and transition to peace. Repeated or obsolete acceptance is rejected without partial effects.

3. Extend the existing European relations report with a negotiation action for eligible wartime contacts, showing visitor, rival, cost and resulting treasury before confirmation. Keep basic relationship information visible without contact. Confirmation, cancel and postpone are distinct. Existing attack confirmation remains the only route to breaking the resulting peace; AI hostility uses the shared relation.

4. `PeaceNegotiationTest` covers contact visibility/adjacency/roles, strength and Franklin prices, cancellation, insufficient funds, stale contact/strength/gold/relationship, single transfer, saved pending and accepted state. F50/TC59 covers the report-to-offer journey, cancel, postponed Continue, confirmation and rejection. Regress TC33's existing relation report and treaty-breaking paths.
