## 1. Summary

1. Players must discover how to depart Europe after manually boarding passengers, without knowing another game or discovering a drag target by accident.
2. Implement the selected muted harbor design: warm ivory sky, neutral pale gray water, handwritten travel directions and a visible departure action beside the docked ships.

## 2. Behavior

1. Selecting a docked ship exposes its cargo and the action “Sail to New World” / “Odplout do Nového světa”. Activating the action starts the existing outbound voyage for that ship and its cargo, without configuring a trade route or opening another confirmation. Before activation the player can change the selected ship or leave the screen.
2. The departure action uses the same shared button styling, height, 32-pixel icon and text formatting as the upper toolbar. Keyboard activation works. Without a selected docked ship the action is disabled; pending decisions and existing model travel restrictions remain effective. A stale or rejected departure shows the existing unavailable-voyage message.
3. The two travel headings read “Sailing to Europe” and “Sailing to New World”, with Czech equivalents. They share the “Europe” page title's font and size, have no ribbons, shields or background panels, and omit ship counts. Dock and quay headings also omit counts.
4. Hand-drawn routes show arrival toward the actual docked-ship area and departure toward the New World. Do not add a separate Europe icon. Routes are decorative and cannot intercept input. Ships in both travel lists retain the docked ships' 45-pixel image size.
5. All ships remain reachable in independent horizontal lists. Keep selection, cargo, passengers, travel details, remaining turns, context menus, drag departure, immigration and taxes. A departed ship appears in the outbound list; remaining docked ships can still be selected.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Reuse `ActionButtons` and the shared toolbar size/style, and reuse the existing model departure operation through one UI handler for both drag and button. Keep rules in the model under the [architecture contract](../../architecture.md).
2. Update the Europe view, shared heading/button style selectors and both localizations. Preserve the existing ship sprites. Supply genuine editable SVG artwork and derived PNG exports for the muted harbor and route decorations under the [graphics contract](../../graphics.md#zdrojovy-format-herni-grafiky).

TC194 heading expectations were updated and `TC_04_depart_loaded_ship_with_shared_button` was added for keyboard/mouse departure, shared typography/control dimensions and preserved cargo. Completion uses this explicit exception, not normal tested acceptance.
