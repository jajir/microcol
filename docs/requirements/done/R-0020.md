Resolved for R-0020 on 2026-10-13 after consulting the [original manual](../../Sid_Meiers_Colonization_manual.pdf), printed pages 62–65 (PDF pages 68–71): equipment uses market bid/ask prices and the transaction monitor reports tax/net proceeds. The passage does not supply exact integer rounding or an unambiguous purchase-tax formula. Explicit MicroCol Q-10 policy: retain the current purchase surcharge and sale deduction, calculate purchase `floor(gross × (100 + tax) / 100)` and sale `floor(gross × (100 − tax) / 100)` with checked integer arithmetic, and share one model quote between cargo and equipment. Quotes include quantity, direction, unit price and tax so changed inputs require confirmation even when rounded totals coincide. This is implementation policy, not a claimed historical formula. King-treasury accounting is unchanged by this equipment slice.

2. Resolved implementation policy on 2026-10-13: require explicit sale of remaining tools before buying a fresh 100. Tools and weapons/horses cannot silently replace one another; sell incompatible equipment first. Horses and muskets may coexist. Printed page 65 (PDF page 71) specifies lots and buy/sell choices but does not specify partial-tool top-ups or automatic disposal. Preserve the remaining quantity and specialty; do not infer disposal from the manual.

3. Resolved trade prerequisite: printed page 87 (PDF page 93) closes home-country commerce after independence. This slice does not implement the manual's entire revolutionary Europe-screen/custom-house behavior. The old F06 cargo-trade fixture incorrectly declared independence while expecting purchases; its prerequisite is now pre-independence, with all original quantity/gold assertions retained. TC27/TC_08 separately checks rejected purchases and sales after independence.
