## 1. Summary

1. A player needs to assign a wagon or ship to a saved itinerary, choose its first stop and let it repeat until canceled.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `ffae3396` finds no saved route assignment or recurring navigation. This is source inspection, not a runtime check.
3. This requirement owns assignment, current stop, movement and repeated cycles; it reuses stop transactions and does not create a second goods-transfer rule.

## 2. Behavior

1. Assign an owned wagon to a legal land route or ship to a legal sea route and select a first stop. A non-carrier, wrong owner, incompatible route or unreachable first stop rejects without replacing the unit's existing state.
2. On later owner turns, the carrier follows legal steps within its movement, processes each reached stop once through [R-0140](R-0140.md), advances to the next ordered stop and repeats after the last. A full ship or empty warehouse does not stop the turn; a transfer may be a no-op. A single-stop or coincident-stop route cannot process its stop indefinitely within one owner update without movement. A lost/inaccessible stop pauses the carrier with a visible reason rather than substituting another destination or spinning.
3. Save/Continue mid-leg, at a stop and between two cycles retains assignment, current stop and travel progress without replaying movement or cargo. Cancel/repair behavior is [R-0142](R-0142.md); two complete circuits demonstrate the recurring order.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Keep one model-owned persisted assignment per carrier: stable route ID, selected/current stop, progress and a visible pause reason. Validate ownership, transport kind, first-stop reachability and exclusive movement orders before mutation. Route assignment does not invent a second itinerary or cargo rule.
2. Reuse existing owner-turn preparation, shared movement legality/costs and real Atlantic departure/countdown/arrival operations. Use the actual carrier's movement and Europe return location rather than the route-preview surrogate. Advance only on eligible owner updates, never on view/load; do not run both go-to and route navigation for one unit.
3. Process arrivals through R-0140, retain its exactly-once visit boundary, advance ordered stops and repeat. Bound each update even for one stop, coincident destinations or zero transfers. Unreachable, lost, no-longer-owned or otherwise unusable destinations pause with a model-provided reason; do not substitute a destination, teleport or expose hidden map knowledge. Assignment cancellation and editing remain R-0142.
4. Expose assignment, first-stop choice, current destination and pause feedback through existing carrier/route controls, with EN/CS labels and keyboard operation. Canceling the assignment dialog changes nothing. Preserve existing manual cargo and go-to paths.
5. Model checks cover wrong owner/kind/non-carrier, first-stop choice, movement budget and costs, owner-turn isolation, two circuits, actual Europe round trip, full/empty no-op, finite single/coincident stops, blocked/lost stop and strict save/Continue mid-leg/at-stop. Add named `TC_118_F109_trade_route_assignment_test` player scenarios using the existing fixture/page/verification contract and real end-turn progression.
6. Main reviews worker changes, applies the shared formatter and runs all JUnits plus affected Docker/Xvfb scenarios after each coherent change. Preserve test counts and failures in the evidence page. Complete second-batch acceptance is required before marking done and committing/pushing; no remote CI work.
