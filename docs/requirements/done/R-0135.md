## 1. Summary

1. A newly arrived immigrant should be willing to board an outgoing ship, while the player can opt that person out or bring them forward in the dock queue.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `ffae3396` credits R-0073's actual stable-ID sentry boarding. It does not credit immigrant defaults or player priority controls.
3. Scope: saved immigrant boarding preference and player queue control, feeding the existing departure transfer. Ordinary sentry eligibility, capacity, costs and identity remain R-0073/R-0022.

## 2. Behavior

1. A new immigrant reaching the European dock starts willing to board. The player can turn that willingness off without moving or deleting the person, and can bring a willing immigrant forward among dock candidates; the current visible order reflects the choice.
2. On a valid departure, only willing eligible people board up to actual free holds. Equipment/identity and [R-0022](R-0022.md) movement costs are preserved; excess people remain at the dock. After the explicitly prioritized willing immigrant, all remaining eligible immigrants and sentries share stable-ID order, as agreed below.
3. Canceling a preference change or an obsolete/wrong-owner choice changes neither queue nor cargo. Save/Continue retains each immigrant's opt-out and ordering state and cannot board a person again merely by loading.

4. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0135-refinement)): A player-explicitly prioritized willing immigrant boards before other eligible waiting passengers. Then use stable-ID order for the remaining eligible waiting groups together, retaining normal movement and hold limits; an opted-out immigrant does not board automatically.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Selected and refined on 2026-10-20. Add minimal model-owned immigrant boarding preference and explicit priority state, persisted through the existing unit/player save owners. Mark paid recruitment and earned immigration arrivals at their actual creation paths in `Player`; do not silently classify every purchased specialist, ship or returning passenger as a new immigrant. Reuse existing unit identity and dock location.
2. Extend the existing `AbstractUnit` departure boarding collection and transfer; do not create a second cargo mutation path. Check willingness, eligible place, live owner, normal movement and actual free slots before assigning anyone. A player-promoted willing immigrant goes first, followed by all other eligible waiting people in stable-ID order. Use one current front-of-queue choice per owner; promoting another replaces that choice, and opting out or boarding the selected person clears it. Ordinary map sentries retain their existing behavior.
3. Provide guarded current-player preference and priority commands and a model-provided ordered dock view. Reject stale, foreign, moved or ineligible choices before changing queue/cargo. Persist willingness and priority without boarding on load; validate present malformed state rather than guessing identities. Cancellation of an unconfirmed choice leaves state unchanged. Opt-out is authoritative even if another waiting-order flag would otherwise include the immigrant.
4. Extend the existing Europe pier unit controls with visible willingness and a front-of-queue action/feedback, using house controls and English/Czech text. The displayed order must match the model's departure order; keep existing manual boarding, sentry activation, recruitment and cargo interactions intact. Handle stale confirmation visibly without changing another unit.
