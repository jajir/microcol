## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a clear restriction when trying to remove the third resident from a fortified colony, while remaining free to rearrange work inside it.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The existing empty-colony cleanup is not the manual’s minimum-three-residents rule for stockades and higher defenses.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected and refined on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance refined on 2026-10-16.

1. With a stockade or higher defense and three internal residents, an action removing one from the colony is rejected before equipment or stocks change.

2. With four residents, allow one to leave when other conditions hold; show the resulting three.

3. Moving a resident between internal jobs does not reduce population and remains allowed.

4. Famine/combat outcomes follow an explicit forced-loss policy rather than reusing a voluntary-removal rejection. Save/load retains the real population and fortification. Reuse the [shared-square persistence rule](../../gap-analysis.md#shared-colony-working-squares): reject contradictory worker/job references before exposing the loaded game, without relocating workers or rewriting the file.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes), [economy, production and education](../../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Allow forced famine/combat losses below three without removing fortification, but retain the voluntary-removal restriction.

All voluntary placement paths out of the current colony validate before mutation, including transfers to another colony; moves between its fields and indoor jobs remain allowed. 2. Test stockade, fort and fortress at populations 2/3/4, unchanged full state on rejection, internal reassignment, departure followed by equipment/boarding, forced famine and current-schema save/load. Existing contradictory worker-reference validation remains authoritative. Outside troops do not count as residents.

Retain checks for unfortified last-resident abandonment and existing passenger restrictions. Follow the [functional test contract](../../functional-tests.md).
