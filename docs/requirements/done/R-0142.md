## 1. Summary

1. A player needs to change a running itinerary, cancel one carrier's route or delete a route while retaining control of every affected carrier.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `ffae3396` finds no route editor, assignment or cancellation. R-0139–R-0141 establish the prerequisite state.
3. This requirement owns route edit/delete timing, per-carrier cancel and repair of a paused lost stop; it does not recalculate past transactions.

## 2. Behavior

1. An edit to stops or configured cargo takes effect at the carrier's **next** stop, preserving its current location and voyage progress. Canceling an individual assignment returns only that carrier to manual orders immediately; other assigned carriers continue.
2. Deleting a route immediately returns all its assigned carriers to manual control, without moving them or resetting an Atlantic crossing. An inaccessible or lost stop pauses the affected carrier with a reason; the player may repair the route or cancel rather than spin or silently choose another port.
3. Canceling an edit changes nothing. Wrong owner, stale route or obsolete assignment rejects without partial edits, goods transfer or movement. Save/Continue retains pending edits or a paused reason without repeating a stop or resurrecting a deleted route.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Extend the existing model-owned route store and assignment boundary; preserve stable route identity. Stage validated stop/cargo edits for each assigned carrier's next-stop boundary without changing its current location, movement balance, current leg or Atlantic countdown. Validate the complete edit, owner and expected current route before mutation; route creation rules and normalized names remain shared.
2. Canceling one assignment removes only that standing order. Deleting a route atomically removes its assignments and pending references from all carriers while preserving cargo, treasury, placement and voyage progress. A crossing continues through the existing voyage mechanism with manual control available on arrival; never teleport or restart its countdown. Clean up saved visit references without enabling replay of already settled transfers.
3. Let the player repair an invalid/lost-stop route and resume through the same next-stop boundary, or cancel it. Preserve a visible reason while blocked. Save/Continue retains current-leg and pending-edit state; loading performs no movement, transfer or resurrection of deleted routes. Invalid/stale operations reject without partial effects.
4. Extend existing route controls for edit, per-carrier cancel and route deletion, including explicit affected-carrier feedback, EN/CS and keyboard paths. Closing/canceling an edit leaves the model unchanged. Reuse the existing goods ordering controls and model refusal messages.
5. Add model checks for two carriers at different stages, edits en route/at a stop, next-stop application, one-carrier cancel, deletion during Atlantic crossing, lost-stop repair, stale/wrong owner rejection, exact cargo/money invariants and pending-edit Save/Continue. Add `TC_119_F110_trade_route_edit_test` with real player actions and dedicated page verification methods, including cancel/delete/repair and disk Continue.
6. Main formats, reviews and runs all JUnits plus affected Docker/Xvfb UI tests after each coherent change. Record actual evidence; full second-batch acceptance precedes done/commit/push. CI remains outside this task.
