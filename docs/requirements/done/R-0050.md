## 2. Behavior

1. A reader can start from the [current backlog](../backlog.md), find a small outcome and understand its purpose and player examples without Java knowledge.
2. Every old requirement has one row in the coverage map, an explicit disposition and links to all its captured successors or a reason no unfinished outcome remains. Every successor states its origin and whether it is a remainder, acceptance gap or specification follow-up.
3. Completed work remains credited. Superseded old assignments are canceled with successors; unresolved new work starts as `new`, not automatically ready or done.
4. Existing decisions and historical chapter/item/test references remain accessible. Unspecified numeric rules stay open; accepted behavior is not silently replaced by a proposal.
5. Further game development requires selecting and refining one requirement. R-0041–R-0049 remain unused; this migration does not claim complete decomposition of the entire manual.
