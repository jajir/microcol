1. Resolved on 2026-10-14: initial Dutch/French relations in the two reference maps are explicitly `PEACE`. The [original manual](../../Sid_Meiers_Colonization_manual.pdf), printed page 81 (PDF 87), defines attacks as acts of war and says war persists until peace is obtained. It does not prescribe every custom scenario's initial relationship; the reference-map initialization is MicroCol policy. Combat fixtures may explicitly start at war; missing data is not inferred as peace.

2. Resolved on 2026-10-14: implement and test the accepted-peace transition in the model, without pretending that an offer is acceptance or providing unilateral peace UI. Printed page 82 (PDF 88) describes bilateral cessation of hostilities after a treaty and negotiations dependent on the rival's response. Negotiation, tribute and alliance journeys remain outside this bounded state slice. Keep their UC-069 gaps open.

3. Resolved on 2026-10-14: broad political relations can be visible from the start. The Foreign Affairs Advisor on printed page 79 (PDF 85) initially reports who is at war with whom; detailed population/colony/force comparisons come after Jan de Witt. This supports the basic relation readout without inventing contact flags or revealing detailed world information.

4. Resolved on 2026-10-14: use `Player.name` as the existing immutable internal key and `displayName` only as a label. The inspected constructor, save/load references and duplicate-key check already establish this separation; changing the display name must not rekey pairs. This is a MicroCol identity decision, not a claim about the historical save format.


5. Resolved on 2026-10-14: the [original manual](../../Sid_Meiers_Colonization_manual.pdf), printed page 32 (PDF 38), distinguishes the forces entering a captured settlement from its inhabitants, who change allegiance and continue working. Printed page 46 (PDF 52) places soldier/dragoon roles on the map rather than in productive jobs. Keeping the capturer equipped on the colony tile is the implementation interpretation used here; no automatic workforce assignment is implied. Source inspection found that the previous capture path called field placement before changing colony ownership, which conflicts with authoritative equipment-return validation. This does not implement the separate civilian-defense combat described on page 32.
