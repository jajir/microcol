## 1. Summary

1. Help the player compare buildings and decide which improvement to construct without leaving the game.
2. Source inspection at `97793b40` found illustrated terrain, bonus and unit references, and artwork for all 41 construction types, but no buildings topic.
3. Add one localized reference page using existing artwork and rules. Construction, production, saved games and artwork remain owned by their existing components.

## 2. Behavior

1. From a running game, open help with its button or H and select Buildings (Budovy in Czech). Every building level has its name, current artwork, short description and benefit. Related levels appear together in upgrade order.
2. Each constructible level shows its full hammer/tool cost, minimum population, predecessor and required founder where applicable. Starting buildings are identified as present from founding. For example, the lumber mill follows the carpenter's shop and costs 52 hammers, 0 tools, with a minimum population of 3.
3. Productive buildings show worker capacity, base input/output per free colonist per turn and any unattended output. Explain that actual output depends on workers, supplies and bonuses. Passive buildings describe their actual benefit, including the unchanged breeding effect of large stables.
4. Search finds localized building names without requiring accents or matching case. Long content scrolls within the help frame in narrow and wide windows. Back and Escape return to the map without changing the game. This reference does not offer purchases or construction actions; rush completion prices remain in the construction dialog.
