## 1. Summary

1. Earning intervention must bring controlled ships and Continental troops into an eligible port exactly once.
2. R-0178 owns the combat-role definitions; this requirement owns real delivery and pending placement, not a second role implementation.

## 2. Behavior

1. One earned intervention supplies two men-of-war, four Continental infantry and two Continental cavalry under rebel control at the lowest-ID owned reachable coastal colony. Their role strengths and Tory-colony +50% bombardment are R-0178's rule.
2. If that port is temporarily obstructed, keep delivery pending; revalidate at each opportunity and never displace another unit, create partial duplicate gifts or silently discard the event. With no surviving coastal colony, evaluate R-0192 loss first.
3. Save/Continue retains pending/delivered state, each unit identity and control. UC-083 checks exactly eight units, legal port selection, blockage/retry, no port/loss and no repeat after reload.

4. Accepted on 2026-10-18: defeated Continental and royal infantry/cavalry are destroyed together with their equipment. They do not downgrade, survive as colonists, or leave recoverable equipment. See the [shared defeat rule](../../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention).

5. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0196](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0196-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model consumes R-0189's durable earned event once, plans all unit placement against current occupancy/capacity and commits atomically or leaves it pending. Use shared R-0178/R-0097 roles; GUI reports actual arrival.
2. Proposed model/UC-083 checks cover lowest-ID port, blocked/lost port, full role/control set, no duplicate and Continue under the [functional contract](../../functional-tests.md).
3. Selected and refined 2026-10-23. Consume `Player.getIntervention().isEarned()` from R-0189. Persist delivered state separately from the earned target and preserve exact existing bell accounting. Use the model's unit creation, placement and military roles; validate all eight placements and IDs before mutation. Reload itself must not deliver units.
4. Add TC156 for actual arrival, player control, blocked delivery and Save/Continue. Model tests cover lowest-ID reachable owned port, full composition, ownership, role provenance, no partial mutation, blocked retry, loss precedence and no duplication. Main runs all JUnit plus selected Docker/Xvfb scenarios and complete batch acceptance; no remote CI work.
5. Coordinate the no-port/loss precedence with R-0192 before full acceptance. Until that integration is complete, an absent port must preserve pending state and must never create or displace units.

6. Targeted results are recorded in [delivery evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0190-targeted-intervention-delivery-verification-2026-10-23). R-0192 integration and full batch acceptance remain pending.

6. Complete local acceptance on 2026-10-23 is recorded in [sixth-batch evidence](../../functional-tests.md#sixth-batch-full-local-acceptance-2026-10-23).
