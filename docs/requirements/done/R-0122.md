## 1. Summary

1. A road should improve documented resource work on its tile without creating output on barren land or deposit-free mountains.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `f10e3f49` records terrain production modifiers for trees and plowed fields, but no road/resource branch. This is a documented source assessment, not a player test.
3. Scope: apply the accepted road addition to ore, furs, lumber and eligible mountain silver. Building/travel effects belong to [R-0121](R-0121.md); the broader baseline terrain and resource-reserve matrices retain their own ownership.

## 2. Behavior

1. A player inspecting a worked road tile sees the same road-influenced output that the colony receives on its next production update.
2. A road adds one to an already positive ore, fur or lumber tile output. Mountain silver gets that addition only when a silver deposit is present. Apply the [accepted calculation order](../../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education) before profession and percentage effects.
3. A zero-output resource, deposit-free mountain or tile without a road gets no road bonus; previews and production remain unchanged. Road eligibility on all non-sea terrain, including coastal colonies, comes from [R-0121](R-0121.md); eligibility alone cannot create a zero-base resource output. Removing or changing the worker does not add a second road bonus.
4. Save and Continue retain road/deposit identity and the same yield rule; loading does not produce goods by itself.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Reuse the shared terrain-output calculation for both preview and actual production, reading persisted road state and [R-0127](R-0127.md) explicit silver-deposit identity. Do not infer a deposit from nonzero current production.
2. Selected and refined on 2026-10-20. R-0121, R-0124 and R-0127 are accepted prerequisites. Inspect the existing shared terrain calculation and its callers; apply the road addition once before profession and percentage modifiers. Keep the model authoritative and reuse its result for previews and production.
3. Required checks: model before/after road for all four resources, zero-base and deposit-free mountain cases, matching specialist versus ordinary worker, preview versus one real production update, and Save/Continue without load-time production. Extend the existing UC-021 `TC_88_F79_road_construction_test` and its page/fixture where practical; preserve stable scenario IDs and the functional source contract.
