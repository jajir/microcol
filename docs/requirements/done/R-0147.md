## 1. Summary

1. A player can remove a colonist's civilian expertise so that the same person may learn another specialty.
2. The 2026-10-17 EDU-004 source assessment at `8f3ce118` credits R-0107 with persisted skills and training but finds no player action to clear an existing specialty; no runtime result is claimed.
3. This requirement owns the clear action. R-0107 remains the owner of teaching, work experience and graduation; military veteran status remains independent.

## 2. Behavior

1. For a living specialist under the player's control, the colony/unit controls offer a clear-specialty action with the identity and resulting free-colonist role visible before confirmation.
2. Confirming turns that same person into a free colonist, clears civilian expertise and its relevant training progress, and preserves identity, owner, location, equipment and any currently legal work. A later eligible school/work path can teach another specialty under R-0107.
3. Canceling or attempting the action on an invalid/stale target leaves role, work, progress and equipment unchanged. If an existing assignment cannot legally survive the new role, resolve or reject it before mutation and explain the outcome rather than leaving invalid staffing.
4. Save/Continue retains the cleared role and identity without reviving the former skill. Clearing civilian specialty does not silently clear separate military veteran status.
