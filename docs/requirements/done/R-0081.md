## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a printing press/newspaper to improve the colony’s existing bell output without replacing its statesmen.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The catalogue flags printing structures modeled as separate bell producers instead of multipliers of town-hall output; admission fixes alone do not establish the effect.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected and refined on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance refined on 2026-10-16.

1. Keep the same bell-producing workers → a printing press adds 50% and a newspaper 100% to the agreed bell base.

2. The upgrade replaces the previous printing benefit; it does not add two competing bell-producing buildings or duplicate staff.

3. Preview and actual next-turn bells agree for even and odd base quantities under the explicit rounding rule.

4. Save/load accepts the valid town-hall/printing combination and retains output and workers.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Modify existing bell output with the press/newspaper replacement bonuses and shared rounding; preserve Newspaper minimum population 4.

6. The accepted economy base is one unattended town-hall bell plus three per ordinary statesman, with expertise/laborer adjustments before printing. Sum the town hall first, then apply the replacement printing percentage and floor once. No workers: 1 → 1 → 2; one ordinary: 4 → 6 → 8; two ordinary or one expert: 7 → 10 → 14. Completing a printing upgrade affects the following production update.
