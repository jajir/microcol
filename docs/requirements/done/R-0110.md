## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance, refined 2026-10-17 from the resolved production rules.

1. With equal staffing, compare chapel, church and cathedral → apply the agreed increasing religious benefits, retaining one automatic colony base.

2. Complete an upgrade with no preacher → apply its agreed immediate production benefit, without inventing a worker.

3. Add an eligible expert preacher → combine the skill and facility benefits in the documented order; removing the worker removes only the worker-dependent part.

4. Save/load and reopening preserve the same calculation and do not duplicate base/upgrade benefits.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Use the explicit unattended religious totals and staffed/expert production, including the shared Penn/rounding examples.

6. Unattended totals replace the earlier tier: chapel 1, church 2, cathedral 3. Each ordinary church/cathedral preacher adds 3/6 and a firebrand preacher adds 6/12. Preserve the current chapel staffing behavior and staffing limits. Removing a worker preserves the unattended contribution. Other skills do not gain the preacher multiplier.

7. William Penn increases the colony's total crosses by 50%, flooring once after combining unattended and staffed output. One ordinary preacher gives church 5 → 7 and cathedral 9 → 13; an unattended chapel remains 1. Persisted membership applies to every owned colony without changing stored crosses merely by opening the game or preview.

8. A completed upgrade immediately changes the production preview. The completion turn retains the existing production-before-construction ordering; the next colony update commits the new tier's output exactly once. National immigration receives the same actual production once, including after Continue.
