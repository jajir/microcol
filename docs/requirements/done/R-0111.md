## 1. Summary

1. The player needs military experience to produce a real veteran whose identity, equipment and stronger role survive further play.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The accepted ordinary-role attack flow does not establish the full veteran acquisition and promotion lifecycle.

3. Scope: this military role outcome. Ordinary attack eligibility remains delivered; this does not complete all combat or the whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Refined on 2026-10-17 under the continuing authorized implementation run.

1. An eligible ordinary soldier’s agreed victory can promote that person to veteran; preserve identity, owner and remaining equipment.

2. Use the manual reference bases 3 for a veteran soldier and 4.5 for a veteran dragoon without applying their included experience bonus twice.

3. Cancel or reject an attack → no promotion and no experience draw. A qualifying result promotes at most once and reports the change.

4. Save and continue → retain the rank and its actual combat behavior. Other acquisition routes, such as purchased or taught veterans, must create the same supported role; their own availability rules remain with those features.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Promote eligible winners one step at the agreed chance, guaranteed with Washington; preserve specialty and veteran status on equipment removal.

6. An armed land winner below veteran status is eligible, as attacker or defender. A normal eligible victory consumes one additional saved combat draw and promotes exactly when it is below 0.25. Washington guarantees the same one-step promotion without an unnecessary promotion draw. Criminal → servant → free colonist → veteran; an expert gains veteran military status while retaining the civilian specialty. No promotion on defeat, cancel, rejection, naval/artillery winners, or an already-veteran victory.

7. Veteran foot strength is 3 and mounted strength 4.5 before ordinary modifiers; disarming or losing equipment preserves veteran status. A purchased veteran uses exactly the same military rule, and future taught veterans must use it too. These outcomes do not implement continental/revolutionary promotion. Report the actual step once after combat; Continue preserves status, identity, equipment and consumed RNG without replay.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Extend the existing colonist and current-schema unit state with persistent military veteran status; preserve specialist type and stable unit ID/object references. Purchased `VETERAN_SOLDIER` is inherently veteran. Use the existing laborer type for criminal/servant social advancement without replacing a registered unit during combat. Avoid a general conversion framework or save migration; update fixtures only where required by the new schema.

2. Apply promotion only after a valid ordinary-land battle outcome and only to its eligible armed winner. Reuse Model's saved combat RNG; ordinary winner draw precedes promotion, Washington uses the membership owned by R-0104. Bind veteran eligibility/status and relevant founder membership to reviewed strength/outcome inputs so a changed offer is rejected before draws/effects. Preserve both participants' movement consumption and equipment-loss behavior.

3. CombatStrengths reads veteran base once and exposes it through the existing UI. Freeze enough promotion data in UnitAttackedEvent to report the exact new status after animation; do not recalculate the promotion or mutate state while rendering. Keep the existing loser message as well. Display persistent veteran status in the unit information where applicable.

4. Model tests cover both winners, 25% boundary and saved draw count, Washington, each social step, expert specialty, purchased veteran, disarm/remount and repeated victory, stale rejection and save/load. Regress ordinary land/naval/artillery attack suites. Add `TC_66_F57_military_promotion_test` for real review/confirm, visible English/Czech promotion, subsequent effective strength and Continue. Main applies the formatter and performs full model, relevant Docker, documentation and publication acceptance.
