## 1. Summary

1. After selling or gifting, the trader may choose one of up to three locally available goods to buy from the settlement.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` finds no native purchase stage; this is a source observation only.
3. This requirement owns the second-stage offer and actual purchase, using R-0159 stock rather than creating free goods.

## 2. Behavior

1. Only after a confirmed sale or gift, show up to three available goods and unavailable empty slots, ordered by local surplus then ID, their real available quantity and price at the Europe ask. The player may choose, negotiate where offered, or reject. A pending offer survives Save/Continue without becoming a new sale/gift or rerolling.
2. Confirmed purchase is bounded by settlement stock, carrier capacity and buyer gold; transfer the actual quantity and gold once. A partial purchase uses actual confirmed quantity. Empty stock, full carrier, insufficient gold, changed state, reject and cancel do not mutate either side.
3. The purchase closes the current second-stage choice without repeating the preceding sale/gift; the settlement stock and European cargo remain conserved across a turn and Continue. UC-064 checks all three offers when available and unavailable-slot cases, one successful/partial purchase and refusal.

4. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0161-refinement)): Allow one request for a 10% discount on the complete purchase quote: acceptance 50% at content and 25% at uneasy relations. Failure preserves the original offer. Round a positive final total upward to whole gold and persist the quote and draw so reopening cannot reroll. Keep ordinary admission, stock, capacity and gold checks.
