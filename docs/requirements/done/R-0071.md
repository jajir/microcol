## 1. Summary

1. The player needs meaningful crossing times while keeping the known departure coast and safe arrival behavior.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Accepted departure/return rules use provisional durations: eastward outbound three turns, westward four, return three. Weather, speed and navigator do not yet determine duration.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected under the remaining-backlog authorization on 2026-10-16; refinement is complete; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Acceptance refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Begin a valid voyage → determine and show its duration using the agreed ship, direction and navigation/weather factors.

2. Ordinary crossings respect the manual’s one-to-four-turn range; any founder-specific exception must be separately justified.

3. Loading a voyage preserves its already determined duration and cargo; opening the Europe screen does not reroll weather.

4. An occupied return location still waits at zero with the existing reason and retry policy. Cancellation before departure changes nothing.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [exploration, work and transport](../../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport)): Choose and persist the bounded voyage duration once at departure, including weather, ship speed and departure edge; apply Magellan separately.

6. Ordinary examples: east-edge caravel to Europe takes one/two/three turns; fast ships take one/one/two. West-edge caravel to Europe and caravel from Europe take two/three/four. Fast means base speed at least six. Weather, original duration and remaining duration survive save/Continue unchanged.

7. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): Advance voyage time and retry eligible arrivals once in the Europe preparation stage of [national turn processing](../../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing); Continue never advances or rerolls a voyage.
