## 1. Summary

1. A player selects a square in the colony surroundings and compares all products available to its worker without opening a dropdown or another dialog.
2. Source inspection at `b17302c0` on 2026-10-30 found an enlarged preview, a product dropdown and a separate yield line. The map already supports mouse and keyboard selection.
3. Replace that detail with a compact tile and visible product choices. Existing production rules, worker assignment, save schema and other work destinations remain unchanged.

## 2. Behavior

1. Selecting a square highlights it and shows its terrain and worker, if present, at exactly the same scale as one square in the surrounding map. Selection alone does not change the game.
2. An occupied square shows every supported product with its actual yield per turn for that worker, including a supported product whose current yield is zero. The current product is selected. Clicking another row or activating it by keyboard changes that square's production immediately and refreshes the selected row, map and colony totals.
3. An empty square shows terrain and the existing no-worker explanation, with no production choices. The center retains its town commons explanation and automatic production. Invalid edge squares show no stale preview or product choices.
4. Enter/Space on an occupied map square moves focus into the choices. Mouse selection, map arrows, drag-and-drop and the context-menu work dialog remain available. A stale offer is rejected without partial effects and the detail refreshes with a status explanation. English/Czech text and a narrower window remain readable.
5. Changing selection does not advance a turn or consume a resource deposit. Chosen production uses the existing save/load state. Example quantities in the proposal are illustrative, not new balance values.
