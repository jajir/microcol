## 1. Summary
1. Players configure a reproducible generated world, then their identity and difficulty, without a long mixed form.
2. Source inspection at `f4eb0218` found a single setup form, numeric seeds and a fixed 50 × 30 generated world. This is source evidence, not a player observation.
3. Deliver the wizard and selectable world shapes/sizes. Authored scenarios remain available through the map choice. No new artwork, climate controls or map-revealing preview is needed.

## 2. Behavior
1. Opening new free play defaults to a generated world. Step 1 provides Small 90 × 60, Medium 120 × 80, Large 150 × 100 and Huge 180 × 120, with width listed first; all have height-to-width ratio 2:3. Small is the initial choice.
2. Land layout offers One landmass, Big islands and Archipelago. Big islands is the initial choice. One landmass is connected; big islands contains several separated large land groups; archipelago contains more smaller groups. Ocean routes, usable founding sites, terrain, rivers, resources and native settlements remain required.
3. Seed is a fresh editable random string on opening setup, with a New seed button. It accepts 1–64 Unicode code points after surrounding Unicode whitespace trimming. Internal spaces and punctuation remain significant. Equal seed, world settings and generator version reproduce the same world. Player name and difficulty do not alter geography.
4. Continue opens step 2 with difficulty, selectable nation and independent player name (initially blank), plus world summary. Back returns to step 1 without losing values; Back/Escape from step 1 returns to menu. Nation changes never rewrite player name. Existing player-name validation remains authoritative.
5. Creating a world displays busy feedback without blocking the window; duplicate submission is prevented. Failure keeps settings for correction/retry and leaves the previous game unchanged. Only a complete validated world starts a game. Continue loads the saved world without regeneration; saved metadata includes text seed, size, layout and generator version.
6. Fixed authored scenarios retain their existing nation restrictions. Selecting one hides generated-world fields; the two-step navigation and name rules remain the same.
