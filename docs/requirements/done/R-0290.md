## 1. Summary

1. Keep river courses readable and useful for nearby colonies without stacking
   forest, plowing, settlements and unrelated resource symbols on one square.
2. Source inspection at `05301270` found land rivers adding +1/+2 to existing
   production, with forest, crops and settlements permitted. The screenshot
   showed the resulting forest/river overlap. This is historical context,
   not the new acceptance result.
3. The selected outcome replaces ordinary river-square production with fishing
   and finite silver or ore. Existing land movement and road construction are
   reused; no new base terrain, commodity, bridge project or ship route is added.

## 2. Behavior

1. Both river sizes remain traversable land. A river square cannot contain a
   forest, plowed field, colony or native settlement. Colonies can stand beside
   a river under ordinary founding rules. Roads remain legal and appear as a
   compact bridge at the river crossing. Founding/plowing rejection explains
   the restriction without spending tools, movement, land payments or orders.
2. A colony may assign one worker to an available river among its eight
   neighboring squares. Fishing needs no dock and yields ordinary food:
   small river 3, large river 4 before other modifiers, only where bare terrain
   supports food. Mountain/arctic river sources give no food. Fishermen double
   fishing output; farmers receive no fishing expertise. Other worker and
   political modifiers retain their rules. Merely adjoining a river gives no
   passive food or irrigation; existing shared-field ownership rules apply.
3. Only silver and ore deposits may overlay rivers. Their base output is 2
   silver or 1 ore before expertise/politics, with initial reserves of 100
   silver or 200 ore. The selected job produces food or one mineral, never both.
   Existing depletion caps the final extraction, and exhausted deposits yield
   zero mineral while fishing remains available. There is no ordinary mountain
   silver or hill ore baseline on a river. Roads and the retired +1/+2 river
   modifier do not increase river production.
4. Generated river squares independently receive silver with probability 15%,
   ore with 5%, and no deposit with 80%. Non-river squares keep the existing
   approximately 10% resource allocation and resource families. Fixed maps
   approximate the river shares with deliberate placement. Keep usable founding
   sites, timber access, native land rules, every existing resource kind and
   fixed-map arrival/Continue journeys.
5. Connected river travel remains one third of a movement point in either
   direction and does not stack with roads. Unconnected crossing uses ordinary
   movement; ships cannot sail through river land. Underlying terrain defense
   and movement remain; forest contributions disappear with the forest.
6. Colony job previews show the selected food/mineral output and expertise;
   help explains fishing and restrictions. For example, an ordinary colonist
   on a large food-capable river yields 4 food before political effects.
   Invalid/stale choices reject before mutation; canceling does nothing.
   Save/Continue retains jobs, roads and mineral reserves without producing
   goods or replenishing a deposit. Invalid authored/saved combinations reject;
   opening a player's existing save never silently repairs it.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Reuse `Terrain`, `WorldMap`, `ResourceOverlay`, `UnitType`, founding/pioneer
   operations and colony job queries under the [architecture](../../architecture.md).
   GUI, AI and native terrain/teaching queries consume the same restrictions
   and production. No package
   ownership changes. Keep river/road movement and existing depletion machinery.
2. Remove river forests in generation before placing resources; exclude river
   settlement sites. Update every authored map with rivers directly. Original
   World had 28 river squares, 10 river forests, one river ore and two other
   river bonuses. Relocate forests and incompatible bonuses nearby; place four
   silver and one ore deposit on its river squares. Preserve the resource/forest
   inventory, coastal landing and native/European sites.
3. Extend model coverage for all terrain/output cells, expertise, finite output,
   invalid combinations, atomic founding/plowing, map generation and fixed-map
   counts. Update affected political-production test callers to the shared
   terrain-aware expertise query.
4. Keep `TC_90_F81_river_effects_test` course/travel, preview/production and fog IDs. Its fishing path
   uses a fisherman without docks; new silver/ore paths verify actual production,
   reserve consumption, bridge pixels and Continue. Verify rejected founding and
   plowing through controls. Run affected road, resource/depletion, pioneer,
   terrain/help and Original World scenarios, plus the source contract and
   mandatory [local acceptance](../../publishing.md#local-acceptance-policy).
5. Implementation and required local acceptance are complete. Actual selections,
   counts and visual evidence are in the [acceptance record](../../functional-tests.md#r-0290-river-corridors-2026-10-26).
   Remote publication requires separate verification.
