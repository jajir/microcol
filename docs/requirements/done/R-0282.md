## 1. Summary

1. Give unexplored map a few stationary question marks resembling the supplied reference.
2. Source inspection at `3feabbec` on 2026-10-25 found flat gray unknown squares and seed-based stable terrain decoration.
3. This is decorative map presentation; it reveals no terrain, units, resources or discoveries and adds no control or saved field.

## 2. Behavior

1. Opening a map automatically shows brown question marks on roughly 4% of unknown squares, independently of their hidden contents.
2. Marks stay attached to the same map squares through repainting, camera movement, resizing and Save/Continue. They do not animate.
3. Exploring a marked square removes its question mark. Leaving current sight does not restore it, because the square remains explored.
4. Grid on/off preserves marks and the opaque unknown background. This automatic decoration has no action/cancel path and changes no exploration or gameplay outcomes.
