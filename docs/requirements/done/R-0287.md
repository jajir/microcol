## 1. Summary

1. Choosing Original World should offer forests and useful resources throughout the continent, several rivers and freshwater lakes with outlets to the sea.
2. Source inspection at `ab874f83` on 2026-10-25 finds 17 forest squares, 12 bonuses (one of each kind), one ten-square river and two lake squares. The inventory is complete but sparse.
3. Enrich this existing fixed world while preserving its starting expedition, French colonies, polar geography and normal founding route. Other maps and existing saved games are unaffected.

## 2. Behavior

1. Starting Original World consistently produces forest regions in the north, temperate interior and south, repeated terrain-appropriate bonuses, several connected rivers and inland exploration rumors away from the initial landing.
2. The eastern approach still reveals the river mouth and permits landing and founding. Nearby forest, food and fishing bonuses make the landscape useful on arrival.
3. A freshwater lake keeps its identity when connected to the sea, including direct water contact; an authored river also drains it to the coastal river. Ships enter connected lake water under the existing ocean movement rules. A river on land does not become a navigable sea channel. See the [lake rule](../../gap-analysis.md#terrain-reference-data-audit).
4. Save/Continue retains forests, bonuses, rumors, river courses, lake identity and the ship's position. Canceling setup retains its current behavior. A newly enriched map is used only for a new game.
