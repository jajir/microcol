## 1. Summary

1. De Witt lets a loaded wagon or ship trade with a peaceful rival colony without attacking or capturing it.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` reports no rival-colony transaction path; this is source assessment, not runtime acceptance.
3. This requirement owns the trade permission and actual bilateral barter/sale, including its use after home Europe closes. De Witt's information benefit is R-0168.

## 2. Behavior

1. A loaded owned wagon or ship visiting a foreign colony can offer goods only while its nation owns de Witt and has peace or alliance with the host. War, absent founder, empty cargo or invalid access refuses trade without movement, attack, capture or stock change.
2. Price the exchange against the host's Europe prices without the visitor's home tax. A confirmed sale transfers real cargo to the host's actual stock and real gold from its treasury; barter transfers real goods on both sides. Both stock, capacity and treasury limits apply before mutation; cancel or stale confirmation changes neither side.
3. During rebellion, closing home Europe does not revoke this foreign-colony permission; the same founder/relation/stock checks still apply. Save/Continue preserves the agreed transaction exactly once and does not convert a peaceful visit into colony capture.
4. UC-071/081/100/117 cover founder absent/present, peace/alliance/war, loaded wagon/ship, sale/barter, insufficient host stock/gold, cancellation and Continue.

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0167-refinement)): One barter transaction exchanges one incoming commodity for one outgoing commodity at player-chosen quantities. Value incoming goods at the host’s Europe bid and outgoing goods at its Europe ask, settling the difference in actual gold paid by the appropriate side without the visitor’s home tax. Show both goods and the gold balance before confirmation; validate both stocks, capacities and treasuries before changing either side.
