## 1. Summary

1. Players need eight distinct native nations, recognizable settlement classes and a stronger, wealthier capital for each nation.
2. The dated catalogue reports European colonies but no native-settlement or capital state. This is source assessment, not player acceptance.
3. This requirement owns stable nation and settlement identity, class/capital attributes and persistence. Braves, homeland transactions, trade, combat resolution and native AI have their own owners.

## 2. Behavior

1. Arawak, Aztec, Inca, Tupi, Cherokee, Iroquois, Sioux and Apache exist as native nations. Aztec/Inca use cities; Cherokee/Iroquois/Arawak villages; Tupi/Sioux/Apache camps. Each nation has an identifiable capital distinct from ordinary settlements.
2. A capital is stronger and wealthier than an otherwise comparable ordinary settlement of its nation. The player can distinguish nation, class and capital on the map and in settlement information without relying on color alone. Different classes retain their documented defense, wealth and available-goods distinctions.
3. Save/Continue preserves stable nation/settlement IDs, class, capital and separate ownership. Inspection does not create a settlement or reveal undiscovered private trade stock. Invalid or duplicate native identity in a required current-schema save is rejected rather than guessed.
4. UC-060 checks all eight mappings and one ordinary/capital pair for each supported nation; trade/defense checks remain with their domain owners.

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0150-refinement)): Apply an additive settlement defense bonus to actual defenders: camp 0%, village 25%, city 50%, plus 25 percentage points for a capital. A capital city therefore gives 75%. This does not create nonexistent defenders; explain the settlement bonus separately in battle preview.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Implement model-owned native nation and settlement identity separately from European players/colonies. Preserve the eight agreed tribe/class mappings, stable unique settlement IDs, valid distinct land locations and capital identity. Validate invalid or duplicate identity before accepting saved state. A nation with settlements has one capital; empty worlds remain valid. Native settlements must not enter European-colony operations such as Coronado's survey.
2. Expose the agreed additive defensive attribute: camp 0%, village 25%, city 50%, plus 25 percentage points for capitals. This is a settlement attribute for real defenders, not a fabricated combatant. Reuse the agreed ordinary/capital initial treasury distinction of 1,000/2,000 in [R-0159](../R-0159.md); stock, periodic treasury updates, market access and transactions remain there. Do not introduce a second treasury system.
3. Persist identities through the current schema, updating bundled scenarios and fixtures directly where required. No backward-compatibility migrations. Use a deterministic prepared scenario for all eight nations and capital/ordinary variants; world-generation choices remain [R-0065](../R-0065.md). Existing worlds need not gain arbitrary new settlements.
4. Map rendering and read-only settlement information show the legitimately known nation's name, settlement class and capital status, with English/Czech labels and normal fog boundaries. Unknown settlements must not leak. Reuse existing map rendering and selection/inspection conventions, preserve action/cancel behavior and do not route native sites through European colony workforce dialogs. Scope excludes braves, land purchase, diplomacy, markets and conquest.
5. Add model checks for all eight mappings, capital defense/wealth distinction, unique IDs/locations, invalid saves, European separation and save/load. Add `TC_112_F103_native_settlement_identity_test` with real map discovery/inspection, capital versus ordinary identity, fog boundaries and disk Continue. Use existing fixture and page-object conventions and dedicated `verify...` methods. Main reviews architecture, applies the shared formatter, and runs all JUnits plus affected Docker/Xvfb UI classes. Complete second-batch acceptance remains required before done/commit/push.
