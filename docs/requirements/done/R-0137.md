## 1. Summary

1. A player needs a repeatable quick way to load the best sale cargo and unload holds without manually dragging each stack.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue source assessment at `ffae3396` records manual drag transfers but no ranked quick-load or sequential unload command. This is not a runtime test.
3. Scope: one quick-load choice per action and one ordered hold unload per action, reusing current transactions and warehouse limits; route automation remains separate.

## 2. Behavior

1. At an eligible owned colony dock, each Quick load action chooses the available commodity with the highest **current tax-net sale value per unit**. Exclude horses, tools and muskets. Break ties by goods ID. Fill the first compatible partial hold, or the first empty hold by index, using only actual source stock and hold capacity; repeating the action may fill another hold. Do not predict later prices.
2. Quick unload acts on the first nonempty goods hold in index order; repeating it proceeds through the carrier's holds. Each action transfers only the amount the destination can accept under its actual capacity. It does not discard excess, sell goods or empty a passenger hold.
3. If there is no eligible stock, no capacity or a changed state, explain why and leave all goods/treasury unchanged. Cancel does nothing. Quick commands use the same legal transfer and rejection boundaries as manual drag.
4. Save/Continue retains source and hold balances and their order; loading alone neither runs a shortcut nor repeats a transfer.

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0137-refinement)): Initially offer quick load/unload only between an owned colony and a ship or wagon. Europe continues to use explicit purchase/sale, and carrier-to-carrier transfers remain manual. Quick loading therefore never implicitly spends gold; retain the accepted tax-net ranking and partial-hold order.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Selected and refined on 2026-10-20. Model owns quick-transfer eligibility, the ranked load/unload offer and confirmation. Restrict it to a live current-player ship or wagon at the actual owned colony transfer location, with completed turn preparation and the same operational/cargo rules as manual transfer. Europe and carrier-to-carrier contexts are ineligible.
2. Reuse the current owner-specific European sale quote and tax contract for one-unit net value. Among available nonexcluded goods with a compatible partial or empty hold, choose highest value then goods ID. For that commodity choose the lowest-index compatible partial hold before any empty hold, then the lowest empty index. Transfer only the actual stock/capacity limit. Unload selects the first nonempty goods hold; a full destination for that hold produces a reason and no transfer, rather than skipping ahead or discarding goods. Passenger holds are never unloaded by this command.
3. Reuse `CargoSlot.storeFromColonyWarehouse` and `ColonyWarehouse` transfer validation/mutation. An offer captures the selected colony/carrier/hold/goods/amount and relevant quote and stock/capacity state; confirmation recomputes it and rejects changed state before mutation. Cancel leaves all balances unchanged. No gold transaction, automatic sale, new inventory owner or parallel transfer path.
4. Extend existing colony dock/carrier controls with Quick load and Quick unload and a concise preview/confirmation of the affected hold, goods and amount. Use model reasons for unavailable actions and stale offers, existing house controls and English/Czech text. Refresh the warehouse and holds after success. Preserve manual drag and explicit Europe trade.
