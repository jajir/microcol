## 1. Summary

1. Rivers must follow the game's ballpoint drawing style: a flat water fill bounded by dark ink of the same optical thickness as the sea coastline.
2. The selected narrow outlet joins both river banks to the coast with small rounded corners, without a decorative island or a broad funnel.

## 2. Behavior

1. On an explored river, both banks have a continuous dark outline. Tile joins, bends and tributary junctions have open water without internal ink barriers. Small and large rivers remain distinguishable.
2. A known terminal river opens toward a known ocean edge using the existing direction rule. Its narrow mouth connects to the sea; no transverse coastline, land strip, island or rounded dark cap closes the outlet. Lakes, hidden water and corner-only sea contacts do not gain an outlet.
3. Main map and selected-tile preview show the same drawing at normal and doubled scale. Resources and bridges remain above water. Panning and Save/Continue preserve the appearance; no save or world-generation data changes.
4. Original World at (25, 13) opens east into (26, 13). The player's approach, landing, colony founding and Continue remain unchanged.
