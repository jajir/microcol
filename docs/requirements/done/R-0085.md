## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to select goods for automatic export and see the stock and treasury effects each turn.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The catalogue identifies an inert custom-house type without persisted export selections or a turn export transaction.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected and refined on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. In a completed custom house, select the goods to export; leave all unselected goods untouched.

2. At the agreed phase, export the eligible quantity once using the shared price/tax/boycott rules and report the actual proceeds.

3. Cancel selection changes → retain previous settings. An unavailable market or invalid export causes no partial stock removal or credit.

4. Save/load preserves selections and processed state. Any revolution-time export at 50% proceeds is a separately identified variant requiring its political-state dependency.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Export selected tradable stock above the agreed reserve after production and spending; apply the specified boycott and revolution proceeds rules.

6. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): apply this requirement within [national turn processing](../../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing). Resume the saved completed boundary without repeating production, arrivals or action replenishment; future pending choices must preserve earlier completed updates.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Persist a colony's selected tradable goods in `ColonyPo`; new colonies explicitly start empty. Require valid current-schema data. `Colony` validates configuration against the live current owner, completed custom house and registered colony before mutation. UI snapshots model/owner/selection and confirms explicitly; cancel does not change settings.

2. Reuse the shared European sale quote. After consumption, production, breeding and construction, sell each selected commodity's amount above 100; retain unselected stock subject to normal capacity. Apply ordinary net-of-tax proceeds, or half that integer net during revolution. Export precedes final warehouse clamping so same-turn overflow is eligible. Plan and validate all debits/credits before the export batch; failed export accounting must not remove stock or credit part of the batch. Existing turn preparation/save boundaries prevent replay on Continue.

3. A persisted turn report identifies colony, commodity, quantity and actual income. The colony exposes an export-settings action and checks completed-house availability; selection is not itself a sale. Capture preserves the structure, but confirmation rechecks owner and future proceeds go to the current owner.

4. `CustomHouseExportTest` covers reserve boundaries, taxed and revolution arithmetic, new production before overflow, construction-before-sale, invalid/stale selection, absent house, round trips, and exact stock/treasury/event accounting. F49/TC58 covers cancel, confirm, next-turn report, Continue without a duplicate sale and revolution. Regress warehouse, production, construction and Congress player paths under the functional contract.

5. R-0102 now provides the actual boycott lifecycle and ordinary export blocking. Complete the remaining revolution variant using arrears produced by a real protest, preserving exact half-net accounting and Continue. A controlled saved revolution flag isolates this export rule; it does not verify R-0105 declaration/muster. Targeted completion passed on 2026-10-18 ([evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0085-real-boycott-completion-2026-10-18)); full batch acceptance remains open. Do not substitute an always-allowed flag or claim this variant from a fixture without a real boycott lifecycle. Ordinary and revolution export behavior is implemented; [execution evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0085-custom-house-export-2026-10-16) records the actual model/UI checks. Publication remains independently verified.
