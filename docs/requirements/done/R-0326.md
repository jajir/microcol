## 1. Summary

1. Move the change of water colour inside the river mouth so it no longer coincides with the coastline. Follow the [selected reference](../../graphics-assets/river-mixing-2026-10-27/selected.png).
2. Retain the narrow outlined banks from R-0320 while showing river water, a middle tone and sea water as three flat fills with wavy interfaces.

## 2. Behavior

1. A known coastal river endpoint shows two curved colour interfaces upstream of the coast. The middle tone lies between the existing river and adjacent sea colours. No gradient or transverse ink line separates the water.
2. Both black banks retain their thickness and connection to the coastline; mixing remains inside the water silhouette. Small and large rivers and all cardinal outlet directions use the same treatment.
3. The map and selected-tile preview agree at normal and doubled scale, before and after panning and Save/Continue. The player discovers the change by exploring and selecting the existing mouth; it adds no control, confirmation or cancellation path.
4. Inland courses, fog, placement, resources, bridges, movement, production and saved data retain their existing behavior. Hidden sea and lakes do not gain a coastal outlet.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Extend the existing mouth fill in `gui.util.PaintService`, shared by map and preview under the [architecture map](../../architecture.md). Clip two flat wavy bands to the existing mouth and draw the bank ink afterward; reuse the adjacent sea palette and existing rotation.
2. Update the mouth and preview verification in `RiverPage` and TC24/F15/12, retaining landing, founding, Save/Continue and pan checks. Verify three tones, curved interfaces and intact banks; retain river/resource and coastal rendering scenarios.
3. Apply the shared formatter, visually inspect actual renderer output, run model/JUnit, targeted Docker/Xvfb, fixed smoke/support, documentation checks and strict MkDocs. Record actual acceptance in [functional evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0326-river-mouth-mixing-2026-10-27).

4. Implemented and accepted locally on 2026-10-27 after model/JUnit, targeted Docker/Xvfb, smoke and visual review. The [functional evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0326-river-mouth-mixing-2026-10-27) records selections, counts and screenshots. Remote CI and publication remain unverified.
