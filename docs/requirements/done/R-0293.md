## 1. Summary

1. The water mark should visibly fall forward after forming an overhang, spread along the surface and dissolve before the next wave.
2. At `16da91a4`, the implementation still uses R-0289's four shallow rolling frames over two seconds. The approved six-second study contains a distinct impact and fading wash.
3. Replace ocean and High Seas wave motion with the approved sequence. Preserve terrain colors, High Seas direction marker, placement probabilities, meaningful overlays, fog, lake artwork and gameplay.

## 2. Behavior

1. Opening or continuing a game automatically shows a small swell, a brief crest, a forward fall, impact and a long fading wash. There is no new setting, action or confirmation; normal map navigation and exit still apply.
2. The full cycle lasts six seconds. The crest is brief; low water and dissolution occupy most of the cycle. Neighboring locations retain different deterministic phases after panning and Continue.
3. Every eligible High Seas tile participates in the animation. Full disappearance of the wave is an intentional part of the approved dissolve, not a random absence; the direction marker stays visible. Static previews always show the recognizable crest.
4. Ocean retains its 5% decoration placement, selected for a complete six-second cycle so the dissolve is not interrupted. Lakes retain their existing drawing and four-second placement interval. Attributes retain priority, and unexplored terrain remains hidden. No model state or save format changes.
