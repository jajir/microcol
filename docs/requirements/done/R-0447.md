## 1. Summary
1. Players need complete dividing lines above and below the scrolling content on Settings and Keyboard shortcuts.
2. Inspection of `0a5b5b81` on 2026-11-10 found that shared bar border images extend three pixels into the opaque scrolling sibling. These parts can be covered by its background.
3. Scope is the two affected screens, reusing existing border artwork and controls.

## 2. Behavior
1. Open Settings from a running game, then Keyboard shortcuts: both horizontal dividing contours remain visible across the content width.
2. Scroll to the beginning or end and resize between 900 × 700 and 1280 × 900: neither contour is cut off by the scrolling content.
3. Cancel the shortcut editor: return to Settings with complete contours and unchanged preferences. Existing caption, language, keyboard and save behavior remain intact.
4. Menu-hosted Settings retains its existing sea layout. This change has no save-data or game-rule effects.
