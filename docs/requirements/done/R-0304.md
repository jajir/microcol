## 1. Summary

1. The implemented river bends are too short to reproduce the chosen proposal. The desired course has long, flowing bends spanning several squares, with restrained width variation and readable resource bonuses.
2. A river reaching the sea should join the coast through a narrow outlined opening. A dark river stroke pasted over an intact coastline does not communicate an outlet.
3. This capture supplies four visual examples for refinement. It does not report an implemented river mouth or replace the completed R-0300 evidence.

## 2. Behavior

1. Proposed river appearance follows the supplied reference: long smooth bends, clean tributary joins, distinguishable small/large watercourses and gentle width variation. The square grid should not dictate a repeated small wave.
2. Coastal composition follows the [current narrow-mouth agreement](../../graphics.md#uzke-usti-a-obrys-reky-27-rijna-2026): continuous ink banks join the coastline with small rounded corners and no transverse closing line.
3. The final selection on 2026-10-27 is variant 1: narrow outlined banks without a decorative island. [R-0320](R-0320.md) replaces the earlier two-arm choice. There is one shared appearance, with no stored variants or selection UI; small and large rivers retain their widths and flat fills. The selected follow-up [water mixing](../../graphics.md#prolinani-vody-v-usti-27-rijna-2026) places two wavy colour interfaces inside the mouth.
4. Resource icons remain above river and bridge artwork. Fog, unexplored neighbours, bridges, selection, movement and production retain their existing meaning. Panning and Continue preserve the shape; loading must not rewrite a player's map.
5. Existing R-0301/R-0302 geography excludes river squares touching water, including diagonally, except the single Original World endpoint at (25, 13) explicitly authorized in [R-0313](R-0313.md) on 2026-10-27. [R-0320](R-0320.md) renders existing known coastal endpoints only; it does not introduce further placement exceptions or change generation, authored geography or saves.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Source inspection at `0ad22e8c` finds `RiverShape` building individual tile bands with a 2.2-pixel local bend and tile-centre controls. It receives river size/known links but no water/coast context. This explains the short-wave appearance and missing outlet treatment; it is source analysis, not a new runtime test.
2. For implementation, derive smooth tangents across a known connected run while keeping its water inside the agreed logical river corridor. Handle tributaries, ends and junctions explicitly, preserve shared banks and stable world-coordinate geometry, and place bridges on the actual local course. Keep presentation in `gui.util` and map composition in `gui.screen.game.gamepanel` under the [architecture map](../../architecture.md).
3. The current selected single mouth is implemented separately in [R-0320](R-0320.md); no new map-format field or variant registry is needed. Resolve the coastal-rule dependency before changing generation or fixtures. The model owns any terminal connection/placement exception; the GUI uses only known coastline and river data to compose the mouth. Do not invent hidden ocean connections or new playable islands from decorative geometry.
4. Retain TC90/F81 and TC91/F82 gameplay and overlay checks; add coverage for long-bend continuity, actual coastal openings, size transitions, hidden coast, diagonal approaches, bridges, panning and Continue. Any placement change also updates the relevant generated/fixed-map scenarios. Apply the shared formatter and the model, targeted UI, smoke and documentation checks from [local acceptance](../../publishing.md#local-acceptance-policy).
5. This requirement retains the broader inland-curve proposal and the selected mouth design. Current mouth delivery and acceptance are tracked in [R-0320](R-0320.md); [R-0315](../canceled/R-0315.md) preserves the replaced island choice. Four SVG proposals were rendered and visually inspected, and browser inspection confirmed the first view and carousel transition to the delta. These checks validate the examples, not game implementation.

6. The follow-up [R-0326](R-0326.md) applies the selected three flat water tones with wavy interfaces inside the narrow mouth, under the [current visual contract](../../graphics.md#prolinani-vody-v-usti-27-rijna-2026). Preserve this treatment when extending inland curves.
