## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to issue and cancel fortification and see when its defensive benefit becomes active.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The existing order set lacks a persisted fortifying/fortified state; combat modifiers need a real activation boundary rather than a permanent type flag.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected and refined on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance, refined from the resolved movement and national-turn rules on 2026-10-16.

1. A soldier or dragoon on the map with positive movement and no other order can issue Fortify. It spends all remaining movement and records fortifying, without an immediate defense bonus. Civilian, ship, wagon, cargo and indoor workers cannot issue it. Artillery consumes the same contract once R-0095 supplies that unit role.

2. On the next fresh owner-turn unit update, mark the order fortified. Other players’ turns do not advance it. Both fortifying and fortified suppress routine next-unit activation while ordered; the map/status distinguishes them.

3. Explicitly cancel/activate clears either stage and its effective defense state, restoring no spent movement. Selecting for inspection, opening menus and Continue leave the order intact. Movement, attack, equipment or work changes require explicit cancellation first.

4. Save before and after the boundary → retain the correct effective state without advancing it on load.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Retain next-owner-turn fortification activation and use the shared combat modifier without granting it immediately.

6. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): Make the order effective during the unit activation stage of [national turn processing](../../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing), after colony updates; loading that completed boundary never activates it a second time.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Extend the existing `UnitAction` lifecycle with persisted fortify state, using the next `Unit.startTurn` to make it effective. Do not advance state in converters or load. `Model` owns availability and issue/cancel commands; validate current live registered owner, role, placement, movement and existing order before mutation. Expose `canFortify`, `fortify`, `canCancelFortify`, `cancelFortify` and `isFortified` to callers.

2. Reuse the shared unit-order toolbar/presenter and localized status; preserve sentry and plowing. Keep selection independent of cancellation and guard alternate command paths so an order cannot be silently replaced. AI uses existing no-action selection. No GUI dependencies enter the model.

3. Add `FortifyOrderTest` for timing, other-owner updates, same-turn cancel at zero movement, fresh-turn cancel retaining restored allowance, malformed persistence, stopped/wrong-owner/stale units and invalid roles/placements. Verify unchanged saved state on rejection and continuation before/after activation. F52/TC61 covers player issue/inspect/Continue/next-turn/cancel journeys; regress sentry, plowing and toolbar fit under the functional contract.

4. Implemented and locally verified on 2026-10-16: the combined model suite passed 1653 tests and both TC61 player journeys passed after correcting a test hover setup. See [combined evidence](../../functional-tests.md#combined-peace-tribute-and-fortification-verification-2026-10-16); full CI and publication remain pending. The effective state is delivered here; the actual +50% defense calculation and resolver remain the jointly delivered R-0093/R-0094 combat slice. R-0095 now connects full and damaged artillery to the same fortify command, including model persistence checks; [artillery evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0095-artillery-lifecycle-2026-10-17) records the integrated acceptance and pending publication.

5. Final acceptance on 2026-10-17 supersedes earlier pending-CI notes: the whole selected outcome passed the combined 1,823-model/289-player-support regression and is published in `dev-149-57e16d911029`. See [combined acceptance and delivery](../../functional-tests.md#r-0069r-0111-combined-acceptance-2026-10-17) for exact scope, prior repairs and the unchanged automatic channel.
