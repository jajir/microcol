## 1. Summary

1. The player needs coastal facilities to unlock fishing and ship construction at the correct levels.

2. Current situation: Building eligibility exists, but water-field work and ship production have separate effect gates. The catalogue flags missing dock checks and ship construction tied to the wrong upgrade tier.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected and refined on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance refined on 2026-10-16.

1. Without a dock, a water fishing job is unavailable; a dock or its higher upgrades enables eligible ocean/lake fishing.

2. A dock or drydock alone does not enable the manual’s shipyard-only ship projects. A completed shipyard retains lower-tier capabilities and enables the existing galleon and frigate projects, retaining their current costs (100 hammers/100 tools each). R-0108 explicitly preserved these construction sources; caravel, merchantman, privateer and man-of-war do not gain a new construction source here.

3. Cancel or fail a job/project change → keep workers, queue and stocks unchanged. Explain the missing facility in the existing control.

4. Save/load preserves the upgraded facility and its capabilities; do not rely only on exact equality with the removed predecessor type. Fishing jobs also follow the [shared-square assignment and persistence rules](../../gap-analysis.md#shared-colony-working-squares); a dock does not allow a second worker on an already worked world square.

5. Coastal admission uses the shared eight-neighbor water rule from R-0054, including lakes, without adding ocean connectivity. Dock/drydock/shipyard projects and actual ship construction require a coastal colony. Inland saved facilities do not bypass this gate.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. `ColonyField` owns dock-aware production and acceptance; `AbstractUnit` reuses that validation before relocation or equipment changes. Existing field save/load validation rejects a water worker without the required facility. Keep shared-square checks intact.

2. `UnitType` and `ColonyBuildingQueue` share shipyard/coastal admission and completion checks. Reuse `Model.isOwnedCoastalPort`, with model reasons displayed by the existing construction picker. Higher facilities retain fishing capability. No new ship types or repair mechanics.

3. `TC_51_F42_coastal_facilities_test` covers rejected/canceled fishing before a dock, real dock completion and water assignment, drydock-to-shipyard upgrade, denied/allowed ship projects, actual completed ships and Continue. Model tests cover both water terrains, facility tiers, inland rejection, shared squares and no partial mutation. Recheck affected construction, job and field scenarios under the [functional contract](../../functional-tests.md).
