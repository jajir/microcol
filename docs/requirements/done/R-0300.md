# R-0300 — Curved rivers with readable resource icons

1. Status: done
2. Updated: 2026-10-26
3. Related: P04 map/exploration; UC-008 and UC-012, Hra, existing river and resource journeys in the [functional test contract](../../functional-tests.md).
4. Dependencies: [R-0290](R-0290.md) river corridors and deposits; [R-0126](R-0126.md) connected river travel; [R-0127](R-0127.md) resource identity; the [current visual brief](../../graphics.md#soucasne-zadani-vzhledu).
5. Primary area: [A03](../areas.md#a03), map appearance and readable overlays.
6. Related areas: A05 supplies river topology, sizes, visibility and resource identity; A07 supplies unchanged deposit and fishing outcomes.
7. Origin: user screenshot and request on 2026-10-26 for five alternatives to straight river segments, with bonus icons above rivers.

## 1. Summary

1. A player inspecting the map should see natural-looking connected rivers and clearly recognize any resource bonus on their squares.
2. Source inspection at `688e4ca7` on 2026-10-26 finds that terrain painting includes resource icons and the map then paints rivers over it. This explains the overlap in the supplied screenshot; it is not a new runtime verification.
3. The user selected proposal 2, **Ručně kreslené koryto**, for implementation on 2026-10-26. Its gently curved, slightly variable-width water and the required overlay correction form this change; the other proposals remain design history.

## 2. Behavior

1. When a known river enters the map view, its course follows smooth bends instead of a chain of straight segments and sharp corners. Small and large rivers remain distinguishable, with continuous tributaries, junctions and mouths.
2. A silver or ore icon on a known river square is drawn above the river and stays readable. It keeps its existing identity and placement; the river does not cut across it. This ordering is required for every proposed style, not an open design choice.
3. Scrolling, redrawing and Continue preserve the course's visual shape. Hidden terrain and river connections remain hidden. Bridges, settlements, units and selection remain legible; rendering changes no river links, movement, fishing, deposits or saved game state.
4. Five alternatives were proposed; **Ručně kreslené koryto** is selected: **Jemné oblouky**, restrained smooth bends with constant width; **Ručně kreslené koryto**, smooth bends with gently irregular width; **Výrazné meandry**, broader alternating bends; **Tenká inkoustová kontura**, curved water with a thin dark bank outline; **Světlé břehy**, curved water bordered by a narrow light bank. All follow the existing flat palette and hand-drawn brief. The selected appearance is agreed; runtime acceptance remains separate.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Keep the selected appearance within the existing GUI painting responsibility in the [architecture map](../../architecture.md). `RiverShape` constructs tile-local curved bands from known model links, with matching positions, tangents and widths at neighbouring boundaries. World coordinates determine the subtle variation, so scrolling and reloading cannot change it. Degree-two courses bend through the tile; sources and junctions retain rounded connections. Bridges follow the local curve.
2. Split the main map into terrain background, rivers, bridges and terrain overlays, followed by existing colonies/units/fog. Keep resource icons above water and bridges, with the existing movement tint and rumor rules. Existing single-tile and colony previews continue to include their resource overlays. Use `PaintService` for painting and `GamePaintService` for visible-world selection; no model or saved-data changes are needed.
3. Extend `TC_90_F81_river_effects_test` and `TC_91_F82_resource_overlays_test` as appropriate: small/large courses, diagonal links, junctions, mouths, river deposits, bridge visibility, fog boundaries and Save/Continue. Keep current gameplay assertions, fixture conventions and dedicated page verification methods. Add focused geometry or ordering checks where needed and inspect screenshots at normal tile size.
4. Apply the shared [Java formatter](../../code-formatting.md), [functional contract](../../functional-tests.md) and [local acceptance policy](../../publishing.md#local-acceptance-policy). The [dated acceptance record](../../functional-tests.md#r-0300-hand-drawn-rivers-2026-10-26) records successful targeted and mandatory local checks, actual screenshots and retained failed attempts. Remote regression and publication remain independently verified.
