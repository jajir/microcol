## 1. Summary

1. The player needs political support and Tory burden to affect colony productivity at the documented boundaries.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): R-0031’s proposed sentiment state explicitly excludes production modifiers. Current production does not establish the complete political bonus/penalty rules.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected for technical refinement as requirement 15 of the Claude batch; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Accepted behavior; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Compare equivalent colonies at 49/50/99/100% local support → apply the manual’s +1 at 50% and another +1 at 100% to the agreed eligible output.

2. At the relevant Tory-count threshold, apply the difficulty-dependent penalty; show its contribution separately from the support bonus.

3. Population or support changes update preview and next-turn output consistently; do not use rounded display percentages to trigger rules.

4. Save/load preserves the inputs to the calculation, without adding modifiers again on each opening.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [difficulty and national effects](../../gap-analysis.md#difficulty-and-national-effects), [support, production and congress](../../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress)): Use the agreed intermediate Tory thresholds and exact 50%/100% bonus boundaries with the shared production order.

6. Accepted capture rule (user decision, 2026-10-17): Production uses support under the [shared affiliation rule](../../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress), including the European conqueror's Bolivar bonus or the last colonial nation's bonus under Crown occupation.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Source inspection (2026-10-18): consume R-0100's model-owned `RebelSupport`, including the last colonial affiliation and Bolivar. `Colony.startTurn` computes production both before and after famine, and its food/birth helper also computes raw production. Capture exact support and resident count once at the update start and use the same snapshot throughout those calculations; fresh read-only previews use current inputs. Newly credited bells affect the next snapshot only. Keep the snapshot transient and avoid a mutable global production mode.

2. Observation basis: the 2026-10-14 [migration baseline](../migration-2026-10-14.md#verification-baseline). Source inspection and catalogue findings are not fresh acceptance of these examples; recheck the selected paths before choosing a design.

3. Verification to prepare: Add boundary tables with independently calculated output and a visible support/productivity journey, including input-limited manufacturing. Allocate any new scenario IDs during refinement; old unimplemented proposals do not reserve TC numbers. Follow the [functional test contract](../../functional-tests.md).

4. `ColonyField.getPotentialProduction` and `ConstructionSlot` own worker production, while `Construction` aggregates unstaffed BELL/CROSS base output, applies existing printing/Penn percentages and limits manufacturing by real input. Add political changes only to positive-base staffed jobs after existing flat/profession effects and before percentage effects; never give a bonus to unstaffed output, horse breeding or zero-yield resources. Combine bonus and penalty once, clamp at zero, then preserve existing input/output accounting.
