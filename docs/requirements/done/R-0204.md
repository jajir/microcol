## 1. Summary

1. A player can understand and act on important turn outcomes, then inspect the final result, even when routine summaries are off.
2. This requirement owns shared notice delivery/accessibility across event families, not their underlying gameplay effects.

## 2. Behavior

1. Queue food shortage/birth, missing production input/tools, storage overflow/discarded goods (including R-0120 cleared lumber), project completion, arrival, diplomacy and revolution events until the human turn; never interrupt an attack/equipment confirmation. Preserve R-0120's next-turn lumber notice timing. The turn summary defaults on; a Europe arrival adds an Open Europe action, but automatic Europe opening defaults off.
2. Each actionable notice names the affected object/reason and offers Open location and Close without spending moves. R-0086 continues to emit exactly one current missing-tools reminder per owner update; reopening, dismissing and Continue do not create a duplicate for that update.
3. Critical rejection, famine, lost colony and endgame results remain visible with routine summaries off. The final result shows R-0195–0197's actual breakdown and one terminal outcome. Save/Continue does not replay an event; UC-114 checks all named families, mute/no-color reading, summary on/off, Open/Close and reload.

4. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0130](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0130-refinement), [R-0174](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0174-refinement), [R-0175](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0175-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Reuse saved typed turn events, `TeService` and the fresh-human-turn route. `GamePreferences` stores routine-summary (default on) and automatic-Europe (default off) settings. Critical famine and colony loss bypass summary suppression; existing rejection and final-result paths remain independent.
2. Keep the report first when automatic Europe is requested; consume the request on Close/Escape and clear it when leaving the report or starting a human turn. A saved-game continuation does not create a fresh request. Resolve Open actions against current model identity and ownership without spending moves.
3. `ColonyBuildingQueue` emits one saved project-completion event after completion. Existing birth and overflow notices provide the affected colony action. Localized texts include the actual colony, project or goods and quantity. Preserve existing event producers and their timing, including waiting tools and cleared lumber.
4. Model/presenter tests and TC177–180 verify preferences, routing, completion, overflow, stale actions, reopening, Continue and final results; existing family scenarios remain authoritative. Follow the [functional contract](../../functional-tests.md) and complete two-worker local batch acceptance before marking done.
5. Targeted verification and review passed; complete eighth-batch acceptance remains pending. Execution history and actual counts are in [turn-notice evidence](../../functional-tests.md#r-0204-targeted-turn-notice-verification-2026-10-23), including the limits of model/presenter-only coverage.
