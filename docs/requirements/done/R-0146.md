## 1. Summary

1. A colony produces food and a terrain-dependent second commodity from its center square without assigning a resident there.
2. The 2026-10-17 source catalogue at `8f3ce118` reports surrounding-field and building totals but no center-square production; this is a source observation, not a runtime acceptance result.
3. This requirement owns unattended commons output and its display/accounting, not colony founding, staffed field jobs, or the terrain yield matrix.

## 2. Behavior

1. After founding a colony on eligible land, the player can inspect food and the local commodity attributed to its center before ending a turn. No worker can be assigned to obtain this base output.
2. On each owner production update, the commons adds its food and terrain-dependent commodity once to the same production/food and storage accounting as other colony output. An otherwise unworked colony still receives it; a save and Continue do not repeat an update.
3. Apply the center's eligible special-resource effect to the appropriate output, except Prime Timber does not enhance commons production. A resource cannot conjure an unrelated output contrary to the shared terrain/resource rules.
4. Compare at least two contrasting center terrains and a resource/Prime Timber pair. The colony view's forecast, settled goods and food agree. Founding failure or merely opening the view produces nothing.

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../../gap-analysis.md#r-0146-refinement)): Explicitly reuse the ordinary-worker baseline of the actual center terrain as the commons rule: food plus its highest positive nonfood raw-resource output, without profession bonuses. Select and persist that second commodity at founding from terrain baseline, using a fixed commodity order for ties; if no positive second output exists, produce none. Then apply the matching eligible resource effect, retaining the Prime Timber exception; a resource does not create an unrelated output. Derive and publish the complete commons table from the shared baseline instead of introducing an independent formula. The exact tie order and complete derived baseline table are documented in the linked shared rule.
