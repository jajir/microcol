## 1. Summary

1. A colonist may become a missionary in Europe or a colony with a church/cathedral, and Brebeuf makes ordinary missionaries act as experts.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` finds no ordination action; R-0074's delivered expert roster does not prove native mission effects.
3. This requirement owns missionary role admission and shared effective-expertise contract, used by mission, denunciation, incitement and conversion owners.

## 2. Behavior

1. Ordination is free and movement-neutral, including for servants and criminals. It is available on the European pier or outside an owned colony; assigned workers must first leave their job. Any eligible colonist may be ordained in Europe or in an owned colony with a church or cathedral. A colony without either cannot ordain. Confirmation preserves the person's identity and legally retained equipment/status; cancel or stale building/ownership changes nothing.
2. An existing Jesuit/expert missionary remains expert. Before Brebeuf, an ordinary missionary uses ordinary mission values. Recruiting Brebeuf makes existing and future ordinary missionaries use expert values, without doubling an already expert effect or erasing their identities. Save/Continue preserves membership/roles.
3. The effective-expert result applies consistently to mission alarm effect, conversion, denunciation and incitement when those actions run; it does not itself found a mission or produce a convert.
4. UC-066/101 check Europe/church/cathedral/no-church paths and one existing/new ordinary missionary plus an already expert missionary before/after Brebeuf.
