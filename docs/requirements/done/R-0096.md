## 1. Summary

1. The player needs conquest to resolve every defender and transfer the entire colony coherently.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): R-0026 supports armed capture after existing military defenses and safe ship escape. It does not establish the complete civilian-defense and outside-field capture rules.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected for refinement on 2026-10-16 after shared combat integration at `0d027e62`; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Accepted behavior; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Defeat armed defenders first. A colony with only civilians still resolves one defense by its inhabitants as a group (base defense 1), with the shared terrain/highest-fortification modifiers. Select the strongest eligible representative then stable unit ID; winning that civilian defense transfers the whole colony, not one indoor job at a time. A mere movement command cannot bypass this battle.

2. On accepted successful capture, transfer the colony and every affected worker, including workers in all eight fields, exactly once.

3. Reuse the accepted attacking-unit placement and safe escape policy for ships; no ship, cargo or unrelated peaceful occupant is silently deleted.

4. If no valid atomic outcome exists, reject before changing owners. Save/load retains the captured colony identity, workers and diplomatic state. Captured worker references must agree with the new colony owner and remain globally unique under the [shared-square persistence rule](../../gap-analysis.md#shared-colony-working-squares); reject contradictory saved data without rewriting it.

5. Paul Revere uses the agreed 50-muskets cost only when no armed defender exists; preview/cancel never consumes muskets. Defeat of this emergency militia still permits colony capture. Accepted placement (user decision, 2026-10-17; [founder details](../../gap-analysis.md#founder-details)): after victory, the armed worker keeps its muskets and joins the outside garrison on the colony tile, vacating its job. The last resident instead stays inside with its muskets so a defensive victory cannot erase the colony. This is a compulsory combat outcome, not a voluntary exit: the fortified-colony minimum adds no further exception, while ordinary voluntary exits retain their restriction.

6. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Use strongest eligible defender then stable identity, civilian defense and the retained colony-capture/worker-placement decisions.

7. Accepted political-state rule (user decision, 2026-10-17; [support, production and congress](../../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress)): Capture preserves accumulated liberty. European colonial conquest transfers its national contribution to the new nation and applies that nation's Bolivar bonus; Crown occupation retains the last colonial affiliation. [R-0100](R-0100.md) owns that political state.

8. Preserve [R-0089](R-0089.md)'s accepted retained-position rule (2026-10-18): ending an alliance does not relocate already co-located units. Existing occupants may leave, new non-allied shared entry is forbidden, and combat requires ordinary adjacent tiles after separation. Colony defense/capture must not create a same-tile attack or grant workforce access to former allies.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Source mapping at `0d027e62` (2026-10-16): `AbstractUnit.findAttackTarget` and `Colony.planCapture` currently query map occupants only; fields/buildings are absent. Add a colony-specific defender roster, keeping general `getEnemyUnitsAt` map-only for movement/ship occupancy. Use actual armed capability rather than the type-level `canAttack` flag to distinguish garrison soldiers from unarmed colonists. Armed resident edge cases use the armed tier; civilians use a group representative. An independently attacked European field worker is captured individually; attacking the colony center transfers its complete working population on victory.

2. Bind `AttackOrder` to the target colony tile and the selected defender's actual job/place, owner and complete live defense roster. A resident's battle position is the colony center, not a fabricated relocation to the map. Extend the shared `CombatStrengths` for civilian defense 1 and conditional Revere strength; GUI displays that same quote. The outcome explanation must distinguish colony capture, worker capture and equipment loss. Animation needs an explicit battlefield location because `GamePanelPainter.addFightAnimation` currently calls the defender's map-only `getLocation`. After victory, use the shared model placement operation to move the equipped survivor into the outside garrison only if another resident remains; preserve the last resident's job and equipment. Treat this as a completed resident exit for R-0100 accounting, not transient job reassignment.

3. Close both movement and command bypasses: `isPossibleToMoveAt`, capture preflight and `moveOneStep` must not place/spend before validating remaining defenders. For a quote that can capture the colony, validate all ship escape destinations and peaceful occupants before RNG, muskets, diplomacy or ownership changes. Preserve the existing safe-port policy and reject unavailable atomic outcomes. A successful captured colony keeps its ID, warehouse and all field/building jobs; the capturer remains on the colony map tile outside jobs. No persistent intermediate civilian-battle flag is needed for an immediately resolved command.

4. Reuse and audit `Colony.captureColony`'s existing field/building transfers. Reuse R-0100's model-owned affiliation transition at the completed capture boundary; do not infer it from the Crown owner or duplicate the support calculation. `Model.validateSavedColonyFields` already checks duplicate worker identities/jobs, owner agreement and shared-square conflicts; extend roundtrip and malformed-captured-state tests without guessing repairs. Cover eight occupied fields plus indoor jobs, controlled win/loss, no free Revere equipment, victorious Revere placement with multiple residents and with the last resident, retained muskets and save/Continue, stale quote and blocked ship escape with unchanged saved state/RNG. Extend `EuropeanHostilityTest`, `CombatOutcomeTest`, colony identity/shared-field tests and actual TC04/TC33/TC35 paths; retain previous diplomatic/cancel and cargo assertions.

5. Preserve lawful retained mixed-owner placements and their saved validation when changing capture/ownership and unit removal; use R-0089's model owner rather than bypassing it.

6. Selected refinement (2026-10-18): the model owns one frozen colony-defense quote containing the armed/civilian roster, actual defender place, explicit battlefield, Revere inputs and safe-capture plan. Killing the last armed defender must not skip a remaining civilian-group battle. All preview and cancellation paths are read-only; battle outcomes apply ordinary shared capture/placement operations once. No persistent intermediate battle-authorization flag is needed. Worker assignment uses the existing callers listed above and TC76/F68 for new civilian/Revere journeys, retaining TC04/33/35 and related fog/save regression. Implementation follows R-0100 targeted acceptance; subsequent verification is linked below.
