## 1. Summary

1. The player needs two discoverable choices: postpone a unit within this turn, or finish its actions for this turn.

2. Current situation: Tab already cycles active on-map units and wraps back; there is no distinct player command to spend the selected unit’s remaining opportunity.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. With three available units, Wait selects another without spending the waiting unit’s movement; the waiting unit can return after the others.

2. Skip turn spends the selected unit’s remaining opportunity and excludes it from normal activation until its next turn.

3. Skipping creates no persistent sentry or fortification order. The next ordinary turn restores availability.

4. With zero or one eligible unit, neither command loops indefinitely or selects an enemy. Save/load preserves a skipped unit’s spent movement.
