# R-0226 — Play event effects without disrupting music or gameplay

1. Status: canceled
2. Updated: 2026-10-31
3. Related: P30; [audio integration rules](../../audio.md#technicky-navrh-pro-budouci-efekty), [current playback limits](../../audio.md#ukonceni-a-omezeni-dulezita-pro-rozsireni).
4. Dependencies: existing background-music player and visual event paths; player settings [R-0227](../R-0227.md) and a first selected cue [R-0228](../R-0228.md) consume the service.
5. Primary area: [A03](../areas.md#a03).
6. Related areas: A01 app lifecycle; A04 turn/event delivery.
7. Origin: bounded audio-event infrastructure; the existing music stream is credited, not treated as an effects service.

## 1. Summary

1. A short confirmed event cue can play while music continues, without blocking an action or changing its outcome.
2. The effect service handles overlap, cleanup and missing audio independently of game rules.

## 2. Behavior

1. Play a selected effect at most for its actual result and intended human recipient; preview, canceled action, old message reopening and Save/Continue do not replay it. Simultaneous and repeated events follow the cue's recorded priority/coalescing contract.
2. Music and effects coexist. Start/stop/restart, quit, mute, device absence, unsupported or missing asset and volume change leave gameplay responsive and resource cleanup safe. Failure is logged/visible where actionable but never reverses a completed model action.
3. Automated checks cover event count, routing and failure isolation; actual listening on supported platforms checks timing and mix. An audible effect is never the sole explanation of an outcome.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Establish a GUI-side audio service/event boundary, reuse the model's committed-result events, and test lifecycle/overlap without UI-thread waits. Keep model and AI independent of GUI/audio under the [architecture map](../../architecture.md).
2. Current result: captured service outcome only. The 2026-10-11 [audio audit](../../audio.md) is historical source observation, not a current runtime failure claim.
3. Canceled by the user's decision on 2026-10-31. The scope above is retained as history; separately owned requirements remain unchanged. Cancellation does not claim implementation or verification. [Status-change record](../../publishing.md#requirement-status-decisions-2026-10-31).
