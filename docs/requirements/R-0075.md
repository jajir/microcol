## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to choose and pay passage for one of three visible candidates, with the selected place replenished once.

2. Current situation: Generic purchasing is implemented; it is not a saved recruitment pool with three candidate identities and passage offers.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Open recruitment → show exactly three current candidates and the applicable passage quote for each.

2. Confirm an affordable candidate → debit once, put that person on the pier and replace that pool position once.

3. Cancel or fail affordability/current-candidate validation → keep treasury and all three candidates unchanged.

4. Reopen or save/load → retain candidates and quotes without rerolling them. University purchases remain independent of this pool.
