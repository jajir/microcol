## 2. Behavior

Acceptance refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Open recruitment → show exactly three current candidates and the applicable passage quote for each.

2. Confirm an affordable candidate → debit once, put that person on the pier and replace that pool position once.

3. Cancel or fail affordability/current-candidate validation → keep treasury and all three candidates unchanged.

4. Reopen or save/load → retain candidates and quotes without rerolling them. University purchases remain independent of this pool.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Keep three persistent weighted candidates; paid passage has its own price/count and replaces only the selected candidate.

6. Persist three candidate tokens and types, the next token, paid-passage count,
   cross progress and earned-immigrant count per colonial player. Each replacement
   draws 60% free colonist, 20% indentured servant, 10% petty criminal and 10%
   uniformly selected eligible European expert. Servants/criminals retain their
   class through equipment, transport and save; their indoor output is ordinary
   output minus one/two (minimum zero), with ordinary outdoor output.

7. Passage price is independent of type: `ceil((200+20p)*k)`, discounted to
   `max(50, ceil(base*(target-min(crosses,target))/target))`. Use the agreed
   difficulty factors and England's threshold. Paid passage increases only p,
   does not spend crosses and does not increase earned arrivals. Validate
   arithmetic before charging. Quotes bind candidate identity, live player/game
   and price inputs; stale, duplicate, foreign-turn and stopped-game attempts
   leave all state unchanged. Settling earned arrivals belongs to R-0076;
   pending earned arrivals must not permit a stale paid quote.

8. Opening/reopening the dialog never draws candidates. New-game construction
   initializes the pool; current-schema load requires complete valid data.
   The European screen exposes a labeled recruitment action, three named and
   priced entries, visible insufficient-funds feedback, keyboard confirmation
   and Escape cancellation in English/Czech. Hiring a university specialist
   neither replaces candidates nor changes passage counts.

9. Brewster's pool filtering and earned-choice integration remain required
   when R-0104 supplies real founder membership; a disconnected flag is not
   proof of that outcome. R-0076 owns cross accumulation/earned arrivals.
   Full acceptance must distinguish verified base recruitment from those
   dependent integrations.

10. Brewster integration selected on 2026-10-17: his real Congress arrival
    replaces only candidate servants/criminals once, assigning fresh candidate
    identities. Existing materialized people, paid-passage count, crosses and
    earned count remain unchanged. Future replacement draws use 90% free
    colonist and 10% eligible expert; paid passage retains its normal price and
    accounting. Invalid arithmetic or exhausted identities reject before any
    membership/pool mutation. Existing stale offers cannot select a replaced
    identity. R-0076 owns the sequential earned-choice boundary and resumption.
