# R-0182 — Free existing native converts when Las Casas joins

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-20
3. Related: P19/P23; [FATHER-018](../gap-analysis.md#founding-father-effects); [UC-103](../use-cases.md#vsech-25-otcu-zakladatelu) (Hra).
4. Dependencies: convert identity/work [R-0149](R-0149.md), founder join [R-0104](done/R-0104.md), ordinary free-colonist role [R-0074](R-0074.md).
5. Primary area: [A10](areas.md#a10).
6. Related areas: A07 worker identity/jobs; A08 native affiliation; A04 once-only conversion.
7. Origin: FATHER-018 one-time existing-population effect; future mission conversions remain R-0149.

## 1. Summary

1. Las Casas immediately frees the nation's converts who already exist when he joins.
2. R-0104 already recruits him; R-0149 owns acquiring and working converts. This is the one-time transformation only.

## 2. Behavior

1. On joining, every currently owned living native convert becomes a free colonist once, retaining stable identity, location and legal assignment where possible. Its convert work/expiry restrictions cease; no extra person, equipment or resource appears.
2. A convert acquired after the join remains a convert under R-0149. Other nations' converts and people already free are unchanged. Canceled/unearned recruitment changes no one.
3. Save/Continue neither replays the change nor erases its resulting role. UC-103 compares existing map/colony/cargo converts, a later arrival, other owner and maintained legal jobs.

4. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0149](../gap-analysis.md#r-0149-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model founder-join transaction enumerates currently owned converts and changes role atomically, preserving placement and assignment links. Resolve any job incompatible with the resulting free role before mutation, rather than leaving an invalid worker reference.
2. Proposed model/UC-103 checks cover once-only population conservation, future convert exclusion, jobs, save/load and canceled recruitment under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
3. Current result: capture only; no gameplay or runtime acceptance is claimed.
4. Align the existing checks and shared model/AI/UI integration with the linked 2026-10-20 decisions; retain unresolved questions only with their rule owners and run UI regression in CI under the current user instruction.
