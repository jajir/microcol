## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a negotiation in which peace takes effect only when the proposal is accepted.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): R-0026 persists symmetric peace/war and enforces hostility. The report intentionally does not offer a unilateral peace toggle.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Contact an eligible wartime rival → see the peace offer and any conditions before choosing.

2. Accepted peace updates both sides once and stops ordinary hostile AI actions; rejected/canceled negotiation leaves war in force.

3. A later confirmed treaty-breaking attack follows the existing R-0028 warning and revalidation.

4. Save/load preserves a pending/finished negotiation without applying its result twice.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [native and european relations](../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations)): Use contact eligibility, the strength-based peace quote and explicit acceptance/cancel behavior, including Franklin's modifier.
