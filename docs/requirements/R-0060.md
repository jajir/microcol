## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a usable game after a colony’s last resident starves, with no phantom colony or vanished unrelated unit.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The model already removes an empty colony and R-0027 preserves it during internal reassignment. Complete famine cleanup of selection, queues, ships and saved continuation still needs an explicit acceptance slice.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected and refined on 2026-10-16; R-0059 focused acceptance has passed. This does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance, refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Lose the last resident to famine → remove the colony once and close or refresh any view that referred to it.

2. Keep unrelated outside units and carried passengers intact. Resolve port ships and stored goods under an explicit destruction policy, without leaving them attached to a nonexistent colony.

3. Continue to another unit and the next turn without a stale selection or duplicate loss report.

4. Save after removal and reload → no colony, queue or worker reference reappears. Internal movement of the last resident between jobs still preserves the colony.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Last-resident famine removes the colony and its warehouse/buildings while retaining outside troops; seaworthy ships relocate to legal adjacent water or are lost with an explicit report if none exists. Repairing ships revalidate their destination.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Reuse `Model.destroyColony` and the existing last-resident guard; inspect every caller so internal reassignment still preserves the colony. Destruction must be idempotent: remove the colony and its queue/buildings/warehouse from persisted state once, remove the dead worker, preserve unrelated outside troops and wagon cargo, and leave no live selection or open view pointing to removed objects.

2. Before final removal, resolve each docked `Ship` through the same model-owned cleanup: choose a valid adjacent water square ordered by x then y, excluding enemy occupancy and allowing friendly ships to share. Move the ship with its existing cargo without using combat escape randomness or spending player movement. If no destination exists, destroy the ship and cargo through the existing unit destruction path and persist one explicit loss report. Check map edges, enemy blockade, multiple ships, passengers and goods. Do not move unrelated outside troops.

3. Source inspection on 2026-10-16 found no implemented repairing-ship destination or colony trade-route state. The accepted revalidation rule remains a required dependency of [R-0098](R-0098.md), which owns ship repair; do not invent a repair subsystem in this cleanup slice. Verify currently stored worker places, building queues and continuing actions, and make the supported cleanup owner reusable when repair is implemented.

4. Extend `TC_10` for last-resident famine, removed colony/view, preserved outside unit, selection and subsequent turn. Add focused model tests for ship relocation/destruction, one report, save/load and absent colony/queue references. Preserve founding and last-resident indoor/outdoor job tests. No new TC identifier is allocated. Shared formatter, focused Docker UI/model/page-contract checks and full integrated regression are required; focused implementation acceptance passed on 2026-10-16: 19 model checks and Docker TC09/TC10 plus the page contract. Full integrated regression and publication remain pending; see [execution evidence](../functional-tests.md#r-0057r-0061-cloud-sol-batch-2026-10-16).
