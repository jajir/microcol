# R-0183 — Reveal current colonies when Coronado joins

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P04/P23; [FATHER-021](../gap-analysis.md#founding-father-effects); [UC-106](../use-cases.md#vsech-25-otcu-zakladatelu) (Hra).
4. Dependencies: visibility and discoveries [R-0069](done/R-0069.md)/[R-0070](done/R-0070.md), founder join [R-0104](done/R-0104.md). Native settlement identity [R-0150](R-0150.md) is an explicit exclusion from this European-colony reveal.
5. Primary area: [A10](areas.md#a10).
6. Related areas: A05 map knowledge; A08 European rival colonies; A04 one-time discovery.
7. Origin: FATHER-021 one-time current-colony revelation, distinct from delivered ordinary sight and future colony visibility.

## 1. Summary

1. Coronado shows the player every colony that exists when he joins and the immediate surroundings of each.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue describes no join-time reveal; existing fog/sight operations are the reusable foundation.

## 2. Behavior

1. On recruitment, reveal every then-existing European colony and the area immediately surrounding it to the founder owner's map knowledge, including foreign colonies. Preserve ordinary known/visible distinction: discovering a place does not grant live private inventory. The exact reveal radius needs refinement.
2. A colony founded after joining is not revealed by this past event. Native settlements are not silently treated as European colonies. Other players' visibility is unchanged; canceling recruitment reveals nothing.
3. Save/Continue retains the discovered cells and does not reveal additional later colonies through a replay. UC-106 checks hidden current foreign colonies, nearest surroundings, later new colony, other player and resume.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model founder-join event snapshots current colony locations and calls the existing visibility/discovery owner once; GUI renders legitimately known map state. Do not add a permanent all-colony sight override.
2. Proposed model/UC-106 checks cover current versus future, foreign versus native, map boundaries, other viewpoint and Continue under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
3. Current result: capture only; no gameplay or runtime acceptance is claimed.

## 4. Open questions

1. What exact radius/shape implements the manual's “area immediately surrounding” each colony? The source names the neighboring area but does not specify a numeric radius; one-square adjacency is a proposal, not an accepted value.
