## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to see colonial and national support change predictably with bells and population.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Bell production/storage exists, but the inspected political state has no local progress formula or weighted national support calculation. The accepted formulas below now define the intended behavior.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected for refinement on 2026-10-16 as the support dependency of R-0102; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Accepted behavior; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Each colony shows its population, political progress and local support; the national view clearly labels the national value.

2. Apply bell progress once per agreed phase. Growth, famine and transfers change population without creating duplicate progress.

3. Combine unequal-size colonies by the agreed population-weighted rule, not an unweighted average of displayed percentages.

4. Zero colonies/population and rounding produce valid values. Save/load preserves progress; opening reports never creates bells.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [support, production and congress](../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress)): Use colony-held liberty, population-weighted national support and the agreed transfer/capture examples, without passive decay.

6. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): apply this requirement within [national turn processing](../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing). Resume the saved completed boundary without repeating production, arrivals or action replenishment; future pending choices must preserve earlier completed updates.

7. Accepted capture rule (user decision, 2026-10-17; [support, production and congress](../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress)): A European colonial conqueror keeps the colony's accumulated liberty, which now contributes to its national support and uses its Bolivar bonus. Crown occupation instead retains the last colonial affiliation and that nation's bonus. An English colony captured by France therefore contributes to France; later Crown occupation retains its French affiliation. Save/load preserves that distinction.

8. Accepted resident-exit consequence (2026-10-17; [Revere placement](../gap-analysis.md#founder-details)): A victorious emergency defender moved to the outside garrison no longer counts as a working resident; apply the existing population/liberty cap rule. The last-resident exception keeps that worker inside and leaves resident population unchanged.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Source inspection on 2026-10-16 at main `92c5b806`: `Colony.startTurn` already computes actual post-famine bell production and separately credits Congress. Add local liberty at that same actual-production boundary, without subtracting Congress credit or interpreting stored warehouse bells as new production. `ColonyPo` needs explicit required liberty data; new colonies initialize it explicitly and bundled current-schema fixtures must be updated. Read-only getters/reports must never mutate or clamp state.

2. Population changes need a completed-operation boundary. `AbstractUnit.placeToLocation`, field/building assignment and destruction currently change places in steps; do not clamp liberty during transient detachment for a same-colony job change. The existing `PlaceColonyField.destroy` / `ColonyField.empty(true)` and `PlaceConstructionSlot.destroy` / `ConstructionSlot.clear(true)` callbacks distinguish real removal from `destroySimple` job reassignment. Clamp after a completed resident exit, famine or cross-colony transfer, preserving the agreed 2/8-resident example. `produceNewColonist` creates an outside unit: population increases only when it enters colony work. Outside garrisons, passengers and Europe remain excluded.

3. Provide exact model-owned local and population-weighted national values plus display-floor helpers. Apply Bolivar once as percentage points without minting bells. Preserve liberty on capture, subtract actual Crown-produced bells under occupation, and identify the rebel nation explicitly for Bolivar. `captureColony` changes workers and current owner; `ColonyPo.ownerName` alone cannot retain the previous colonial nation during occupation. `PlayerKing.getWhosKingThisPlayerIs` identifies the Crown's nation but does not replace the colony's last colonial affiliation. Persist that affiliation explicitly: initialize it for a new colony, replace it on European colonial capture, and retain it under Crown occupation, following the [accepted capture rule](../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress). Do not infer occupation data from display names.

4. Main supplies colony and national report labels for population, stored liberty and exact-derived displayed support. Tests must cover zero population, unequal populations, the agreed transfer example, cap loss, no loss on same-colony reassignment, victorious Revere garrison exit versus the last-resident exception, production/famine/birth boundaries, Bolivar changes on European colonial capture, subsequent Crown occupation/liberation with the last colonial affiliation retained, save/load and Continue with no repeated credit. Production bonuses and independence eligibility remain [R-0101](R-0101.md)/[R-0105](R-0105.md) consumers of this shared state.

5. Selected refinement (2026-10-18): model support uses an integer rebel-equivalent numerator `min(L + BolivarBonus × N, 200N)`, where the bonus is 40 per resident for Bolivar and zero otherwise. Sum those numerators and resident populations for the affiliated nation's exact weighted support; compare integer quantities at thresholds and floor only display percentages. Zero residents produces zero support. Reuse the local calculation in national reporting and later R-0101/R-0105 consumers.

6. Extend the existing political report with own national support and colony rows for population, liberty and local support; expose the same model values in the colony view. Label national/local values in English and Czech, preserve keyboard access and rename behavior, and do not reveal foreign colonies. These reports are read-only. Use TC75/F67 for actual population transfer, production, report/reopen and Continue journeys, plus focused model boundaries. Actual Revere victory placement will be implemented and jointly verified in R-0096; this requirement verifies the completed resident-exit accounting it consumes.

7. Fixture inventory (2026-10-18): 68 bundled/test `.microcol` files contain colonies. Explicitly add zero liberty and the stated colonial affiliation to these current-schema sources; F04 and F06's occupied New Amsterdam retain Dutch affiliation. Update programmatic fixtures too. The main agent may apply this repetitive data edit after the worker establishes field names; runtime code must reject absent/invalid required fields, not infer a migration. Start the bounded implementation on Sonnet medium after R-0089 targeted acceptance; no runtime acceptance is claimed by this refinement.
