# R-0150 — Represent eight native nations and their settlement classes

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P18; [NAT-001/002](../gap-analysis.md#native-nations-settlements-and-relations); [UC-060](../use-cases.md#domorode-narody) (Hra).
4. Dependencies: world/terrain identity [R-0123](done/R-0123.md); existing current-schema save rules. Brave actors are [R-0151](R-0151.md), land claims [R-0152](R-0152.md).
5. Primary area: [A08](areas.md#a08).
6. Related areas: A05 map occupancy; A04 settlement persistence; A03 map identification; A09 settlement defense.
7. Origin: native nation/settlement identity missing in the 2026-10-17 source catalogue at `8f3ce118`; European colony and AI requirements do not represent native settlements.

## 1. Summary

1. Players need eight distinct native nations, recognizable settlement classes and a stronger, wealthier capital for each nation.
2. The dated catalogue reports European colonies but no native-settlement or capital state. This is source assessment, not player acceptance.
3. This requirement owns stable nation and settlement identity, class/capital attributes and persistence. Braves, homeland transactions, trade, combat resolution and native AI have their own owners.

## 2. Behavior

1. Arawak, Aztec, Inca, Tupi, Cherokee, Iroquois, Sioux and Apache exist as native nations. Aztec/Inca use cities; Cherokee/Iroquois/Arawak villages; Tupi/Sioux/Apache camps. Each nation has an identifiable capital distinct from ordinary settlements.
2. A capital is stronger and wealthier than an otherwise comparable ordinary settlement of its nation. The player can distinguish nation, class and capital on the map and in settlement information without relying on color alone. Different classes retain their documented defense, wealth and available-goods distinctions.
3. Save/Continue preserves stable nation/settlement IDs, class, capital and separate ownership. Inspection does not create a settlement or reveal undiscovered private trade stock. Invalid or duplicate native identity in a required current-schema save is rejected rather than guessed.
4. UC-060 checks all eight mappings and one ordinary/capital pair for each supported nation; trade/defense checks remain with their domain owners.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model owns native nation and settlement records with explicit class/capital and current-schema persistence. Keep them separate from European colony workforce/ownership; GUI reads model-provided public identity. [R-0159](R-0159.md) owns treasury/stock and [R-0173](R-0173.md)/[R-0174](R-0174.md) apply defense in combat.
2. On selection, connect settlement identities to the map and later R-0151 brave homes, R-0152 claims and R-0153 relation keys. Do not implement native market formulas or conquest in this foundation.
3. Proposed model/UC-060 checks cover every tribe/class pairing, one capital, capital attributes, discovery display, unique IDs, Save/Continue and invalid state. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no gameplay change or runtime acceptance is claimed.
