## 1. Summary

1. The player needs military experience to produce a real veteran whose identity, equipment and stronger role survive further play.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The accepted ordinary-role attack flow does not establish the full veteran acquisition and promotion lifecycle.

3. Scope: this military role outcome. Ordinary attack eligibility remains delivered; this does not complete all combat or the whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. An eligible ordinary soldier’s agreed victory can promote that person to veteran; preserve identity, owner and remaining equipment.

2. Use the manual reference bases 3 for a veteran soldier and 4.5 for a veteran dragoon without applying their included experience bonus twice.

3. Cancel or reject an attack → no promotion and no experience draw. A qualifying result promotes at most once and reports the change.

4. Save and continue → retain the rank and its actual combat behavior. Other acquisition routes, such as purchased or taught veterans, must create the same supported role; their own availability rules remain with those features.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Promote eligible winners one step at the agreed chance, guaranteed with Washington; preserve specialty and veteran status on equipment removal.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Preparation awaits selection. Inspect shared model role/outcome operations under the [responsibility map](../architecture.md); GUI and AI must use the same rules.

2. Observation basis: the 2026-10-14 [migration baseline](migration-2026-10-14.md#verification-baseline). Recheck these source/catalogue findings during refinement; no new gameplay acceptance was run for this capture.

3. Verification to prepare: Add controlled promotion/nonpromotion and role-strength checks and a battle-to-veteran/Continue player journey; retain ordinary attack validation. Allocate new scenario IDs against the actual catalogue and follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).

4. Current result: captured, not implemented by this migration.
