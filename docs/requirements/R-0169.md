## 1. Summary

1. A European power may offer a veteran mercenary group; the player accepts the entire priced group or declines without cost.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` reports no mercenary offer path. This is source assessment, not runtime acceptance.
3. This requirement owns offer timing, choice, debit and once-only delivery. Ordinary immigrant/purchased veteran recruitment remains with its existing owners.

## 2. Behavior

1. Before rebellion, a foreign power may offer a veteran package when the player has a substantial treasury; the manual's roughly 5000 gold is not an exact universal trigger. The player sees composition, full price and destination before accepting. Rejection spends nothing and does not bar future eligible offers.
2. After rebellion starts, every tenth rebel update offers two veteran soldiers for `ceil(1000 × k)` gold from the lowest-ID contacted European nation at peace; if none qualifies, skip that interval. The offer expires at the end of that rebel turn. Acceptance requires all funds and a valid owned delivery port; accept atomically and deliver once, with an obstructed port held pending under the accepted intervention-delivery rule.
3. Reject/cancel, insufficient funds or stale issuer/port changes no money or units. Save/Continue preserves the offered decision, paid pending delivery and completed delivery without reroll, duplicate debit or duplicate soldiers.
4. UC-070 checks peacetime/rebel offer, full acceptance, refusal, insufficient funds, later offer after rejection, tenth-update boundary and delayed delivery.

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0169-refinement)): Every tenth own turn before rebellion, if treasury is at least 5000 gold, make one offer draw with probability 25%. Offer two veteran soldiers for ceil(1000 × difficulty coefficient), from the lowest-ID contacted European at peace. The offer expires at turn end; the player chooses an owned delivery port, and blocked delivery follows the accepted pending intervention-delivery policy. Keep the already accepted rebel offers unchanged.
