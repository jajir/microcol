## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to issue and cancel fortification and see when its defensive benefit becomes active.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The existing order set lacks a persisted fortifying/fortified state; combat modifiers need a real activation boundary rather than a permanent type flag.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Issue Fortify to an eligible unit → record the order, but do not grant its defensive bonus immediately.

2. On the next agreed owner-turn boundary, make fortification effective and suppress routine activation while it remains ordered.

3. Explicitly activate the unit → clear fortification under the agreed movement rule; selecting it for inspection alone does not cancel the order.

4. Save before and after the boundary → retain the correct effective state without advancing it on load.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Retain next-owner-turn fortification activation and use the shared combat modifier without granting it immediately.
