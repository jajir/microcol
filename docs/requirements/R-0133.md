# R-0133 — Reject ships and wagons as ordinary passengers

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P08; CARGO-003 and CARGO-005 carrier restriction; UC-024 (Hra). See the [cargo rules](../gap-analysis.md#transport-ships-and-atlantic-travel) and [boarding use case](../use-cases.md#lode-naklad-a-obchodni-trasy).
4. Dependencies: delivered wagon passenger exclusion [R-0024](done/R-0024.md) and ordinary ship boarding [R-0022](done/R-0022.md); artillery role [R-0095](R-0095.md) stays separate.
5. Primary area: [A06](areas.md#a06).
6. Related areas: A05 unit/carrier role identity; A03 boarding options and rejection.
7. Origin: remaining nested-carrier path under CARGO-003, excluding the wagon-passenger behavior already delivered by R-0024.

## 1. Summary

1. Boarding and direct cargo operations must not turn a ship or wagon into a passenger of another carrier.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `ffae3396` describes permissive lower-level slot and embark paths. [R-0024](done/R-0024.md) already owns wagon UI/passenger exclusion; that delivered slice is not reopened.
3. Scope: close the nested-carrier rule at every model entry path and matching player offer while preserving legal goods-to-wagon and person-to-ship transfers.

## 2. Behavior

1. A ship or wagon is never offered or accepted as an ordinary passenger in a ship or wagon hold. Direct transfer and adjacent embark reject these roles before any location, hold or movement change.
2. A colonist still boards an eligible owned ship under [R-0022](done/R-0022.md), and goods still enter a wagon under [R-0024](done/R-0024.md). A wagon still cannot carry colonists or artillery. One rejected carrier does not displace or erase existing passengers or goods.
3. Cancel, wrong ownership, full hold or stale choice leaves both carriers and contents unchanged. Save/Continue cannot restore a nested carrier as a valid state.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. The model's cargo/embark admission checks the unit's actual carrier role, independent of which UI path calls it; UI filters using the same eligibility. Preserve the existing passenger and wagon model/UI flows.
2. Not prepared for development. On selection, inspect direct slot, adjacent embark and persisted cargo validation; verify R-0024 wagon behavior rather than rebuilding it.
3. Proposed checks: ship→ship, wagon→ship, ship→wagon and wagon→wagon rejection through direct and player paths; ordinary person→ship and goods→wagon regression; unchanged state, save/Continue and named UC-024 player checks under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: captured only; no gameplay change or runtime test is claimed.
