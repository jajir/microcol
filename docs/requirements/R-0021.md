## 1. Summary

1. Make the Atlantic departure trigger and return location predictable on both sides of the map.

2. The catalogue records immediate transit on entering HIGH_SEA, and random eastern return rather than the original departure square. Existing carrier cargo is preserved during transit.

3. Outward departure, stored origin and safe return. Variable weather/speed durations (CARGO-012), reversal (CARGO-014) and Magellan remain separate; retain current timing visibly as provisional until Q-11 settles it.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for individual refinement; capture does not approve implementation.

1. Entering or moving along a sea-lane cell keeps the ship on the map. Moving outward at the corresponding east/west edge starts exactly one voyage.

2. Record the departure sea-lane square and direction. The Europe screen displays transit state and remaining turns without allowing simultaneous map actions.

3. A return from Europe uses that recorded coast and square, subject to the agreed occupied-arrival policy. Never silently send a western departure to a random eastern location.

4. Goods and passengers keep their identity and quantities. Each turn reduces remaining time once; loading mid-voyage does not restart the crossing.

5. Invalid outward movement or cancelled departure leaves location, cargo and action points unchanged. First departure from Europe with no recorded origin follows an explicit profile rule.
