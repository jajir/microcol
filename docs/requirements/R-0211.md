## 1. Summary

1. All printed land/naval roles must have the right strength, eligibility, movement and transport identity, including every ship family.
2. This is exhaustive chart acceptance, not one new unit implementation.

## 2. Behavior

1. Check unarmed/wagon/treasure/scout/brave variants, ordinary/veteran/Continental/royal soldiers and cavalry, full/damaged artillery, and caravel, merchantman, galleon, privateer, frigate and man-o-war. For each, compare card strength, may-attack/target domain, legal terrain, movement and hold/passenger limits to independent expected values.
2. Check colony equipment at 49/50 horses or muskets, soldier→dragoon and scout→dragoon combinations, correct stock decrement and preserved identity. Check role transition on defeat/promotion/equipment, ship damage/repair, treasure six holds and saved identity. No unsupported role is counted as passing merely because a nominal type enum exists.
3. UC-022/024/072–077/082–083 matrix needs model cells plus player paths for role-changing and transport outcomes; failures return to the relevant listed feature owner.
4. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0150](../gap-analysis.md#r-0150-refinement), [R-0173](../gap-analysis.md#r-0173-refinement), [R-0186](../gap-analysis.md#r-0186-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.
5. An armed land attacker fights an unarmed civilian or wagon in the open at the civilian base 1 on the card, the same base native battles already use. A defeated armed attacker loses its muskets or horses as in ordinary battles. A defeated civilian or wagon is currently destroyed as retained behavior only; its fate is open (chapter 4).
6. Scouts are three accepted contexts, not one rule. In the open field a scout attacks only with muskets and then fights as an ordinary soldier or dragoon (base 2 or 3), because [R-0028](done/R-0028.md) decided that horses alone are no exception and that no seasoned-scout specialty or unit type is added. A purchased Seasoned Scout follows the same rule. [R-0174](done/R-0174.md) alone lets an unarmed scout attack a native settlement at the mounted base 3. The card's strength 1 for an unarmed scout has no open-field cell.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Keep one per-role/per-attribute ledger from the aid card and accepted rules, with each cell naming its owner check and player path. Record the ledger, covered cells and blocked cells in the [functional evidence](../functional-tests.md#r-0211-unit-role-ledger-2026-10-23).
2. Reuse the existing UnitType, ShipRoster, combat, equipment, promotion, repair and treasure owner tests. Add only missing boundary checks, the open-field civilian fight and the scout-to-dragoon player path (TC183). Native and royal outcomes keep their accepted owner rules.
3. Route each unresolved cell to its rule owner (chapter 4) instead of inventing a value or a fate. Run all JUnit and the affected UI families locally before any claim of acceptance; do not watch remote CI.

Fate of a standalone civilian or wagon defeated by a European in the open field: the accepted [combat rule](../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes) covers only a colony field worker, which is captured, and gives no rule for the open field. The question is with the user and is unanswered; destruction is only the existing behavior. Owner: [R-0093](done/R-0093.md)/[R-0094](done/R-0094.md).
