# R-0020 — Buy and sell colonist equipment on the European pier

1. Status: Draft
2. Updated: 2026-10-13
3. Related: P05, P15; UNIT-002, Q-10; UC-018 (Hra). See the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md),
   [use cases](../use-cases.md) and [work packages](../implementation-plan.md#seznam-pracovnich-baliku).
4. Dependencies: Existing colony equipment operations and Europe goods pricing; [R-0022](R-0022.md) passenger transfer for the return journey.
5. Primary area: [A05](areas.md#a05).
6. Related areas: A06 prices, tax and trade restrictions; A03 transaction feedback.

## 1. Summary

1. Equip a colonist waiting in Europe and bring that same person back to the colony with the purchased equipment.

2. UNIT-002 is recorded as missing: colony equipment methods require an owned colony and the European pier currently supports dragging units, not buying/selling their equipment.

3. European-pier horses, muskets and tools transactions for supported colonists. Preserve existing colony equipment behavior; do not add new specialist professions or ship types.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for individual refinement; capture does not approve implementation.

1. For an owned colonist on the pier, offer horses/muskets in lots of 50, tools purchase of 100, and sale of the actual remaining tools. Display the model quote before confirmation.

2. Accepted purchase changes treasury and equipment once while retaining identity, specialty and unrelated equipment. Selling credits exactly the quoted net proceeds and removes only that equipment.

3. Insufficient gold, wrong owner/location, existing incompatible equipment or a trade restriction rejects before mutation. Cancel preserves treasury and the colonist.

4. If prices or location change while the offer is open, do not charge a stale quote silently; refresh and require confirmation of the new offer.

5. Embark the equipped colonist, return to the New World and save/load; equipment quantities and identity remain the same throughout.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Add model-owned equipment transactions reusing unit equipment and market pricing; inspect `Player.buyGoods/sellGoods` and `UnitFreeColonist` before choosing the shared operation.

2. Add Europe-pier controls and translations using R-0017 feedback. Apply the same trade eligibility as the market; resolve purchase-tax/rounding under Q-10.

3. Proposed `TC_europe_equipment_trade_test`: exact funds/one short, buy/sell each equipment type, partially used tools, cancel/stale quote and return trip. Model tests prove atomicity and preserved specialty.
