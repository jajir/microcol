# R-0020 — Buy and sell colonist equipment on the European pier

1. Status: In progress
2. Updated: 2026-10-13
3. Related: P05, P15; UNIT-002, Q-10; UC-018 (Hra). See the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md),
   [use cases](../use-cases.md) and [work packages](../implementation-plan.md#seznam-pracovnich-baliku).
4. Dependencies: Existing colony equipment operations and Europe goods pricing; [R-0022](R-0022.md) passenger transfer for the return journey.
5. Primary area: [A05](areas.md#a05).
6. Related areas: A06 prices, tax and trade restrictions; A03 transaction feedback.

## 1. Summary

1. Equip a colonist waiting in Europe and bring that same person back to the colony with the purchased equipment.

2. Baseline before this change: the pier and passenger dragging worked, but TC22/TC_01 observed no equipment menu after primary/context clicks. Current TC27 verifies the new ordinary-colonist trades and retained identity; specialist coverage will follow R-0023. Colony equipment still uses its existing owned-colony operations.

3. European-pier horses, muskets and tools transactions for supported colonists. Preserve existing colony equipment behavior; do not add new specialist professions or ship types.

## 2. Behavior

Implementation is authorized. The historical audit below records the baseline;
acceptance still requires the complete behavior and actual verification.

1. For an owned colonist on the pier, offer horses/muskets in lots of 50, tools purchase of 100, and sale of the actual remaining tools. Display the model quote before confirmation.

2. Accepted purchase changes treasury and equipment once while retaining identity, specialty and unrelated equipment. Selling credits exactly the quoted net proceeds and removes only that equipment.

3. Insufficient gold, wrong owner/location, existing incompatible equipment or a trade restriction rejects before mutation. Cancel preserves treasury and the colonist.

4. If prices or location change while the offer is open, do not charge a stale quote silently; refresh and require confirmation of the new offer.

5. Embark the equipped colonist, return to the New World and save/load; equipment quantities and identity remain the same throughout.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. In progress: shared `EuropeTradeQuote` and `Player.quoteEuropeTrade` now define quantity, bid/ask, tax and checked totals; cargo buying/selling and affordability use them. `UnitFreeColonist` owns pier-equipment validation, quotes and atomic transactions. Local Docker and published CI regressions passed 737 game tests and 90 functional/control tests, including 22 equipment model cases and nine TC27 player scenarios with save/reload during the return voyage. This slice is published as `dev-48-b989413bb606` ([evidence](../functional-tests.md#europe-equipment-implementation-2026-10-13)); colony disembarkation and specialist integration remain pending.

2. In progress: Europe-pier controls and translations use R-0017 feedback. Click, right-click or Enter on the focused colonist opens equipment choices; a translated confirmation shows quantity, unit price, tax and total. Confirm applies the model transaction and redraws equipment/treasury; cancel/Escape preserves state. Rejections retain a specific reason, and a changed offer requires another confirmation. Apply the same trade eligibility as the market. Inspection found that the market sale panel displayed the ask price and affordability ignored tax; these shared callers now use the model quote and have regression assertions for the displayed prices. Q-10 policy is recorded below.

3. Implemented `TC_27_F19_europe_equipment_test` (nine scenarios passed in the full regression): exact funds/one short, buy/sell each equipment type, partially used tools, cancel/stale quote and return trip. Model tests prove atomicity and preservation of the ordinary colonist type; the new specialist journey remains part of the R-0023 integration.

4. Verification: current TC22/TC_01 requires both primary and context clicks to show all three equipment choices, with cancellation preserving the colonist and gold. Its stable TC22/TC_01 ID is retained; the old absence assertion was a baseline gap observation. Current commands, counts, remaining coverage and publication evidence belong to the [functional test record](../functional-tests.md#europe-equipment-implementation-2026-10-13).

## 4. Open questions

1. Resolved for R-0020 on 2026-10-13 after consulting the [original manual](../Sid_Meiers_Colonization_manual.pdf), printed pages 62–65 (PDF pages 68–71): equipment uses market bid/ask prices and the transaction monitor reports tax/net proceeds. The passage does not supply exact integer rounding or an unambiguous purchase-tax formula. Explicit MicroCol Q-10 policy: retain the current purchase surcharge and sale deduction, calculate purchase `floor(gross × (100 + tax) / 100)` and sale `floor(gross × (100 − tax) / 100)` with checked integer arithmetic, and share one model quote between cargo and equipment. Quotes include quantity, direction, unit price and tax so changed inputs require confirmation even when rounded totals coincide. This is implementation policy, not a claimed historical formula. King-treasury accounting is unchanged by this equipment slice.

2. Resolved implementation policy on 2026-10-13: require explicit sale of remaining tools before buying a fresh 100. Tools and weapons/horses cannot silently replace one another; sell incompatible equipment first. Horses and muskets may coexist. Printed page 65 (PDF page 71) specifies lots and buy/sell choices but does not specify partial-tool top-ups or automatic disposal. Preserve the remaining quantity and specialty; do not infer disposal from the manual.

3. Resolved trade prerequisite: printed page 87 (PDF page 93) closes home-country commerce after independence. Shared goods/equipment trade therefore checks `Player.canTradeInEurope`; the goods panel explains its disabled state. This slice does not implement the manual's entire revolutionary Europe-screen/custom-house behavior. The old F06 cargo-trade fixture incorrectly declared independence while expecting purchases; its prerequisite is now pre-independence, with all original quantity/gold assertions retained. TC27/TC_08 separately checks rejected purchases and sales after independence.
