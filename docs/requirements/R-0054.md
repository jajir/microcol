## 1. Summary

1. The player must be able to enter an own coastal colony while an inland colony never provides a shortcut across land. This is primarily missing acceptance evidence; fix behavior only if the checks expose a defect.

2. Current situation: R-0022 now supplies the eight-neighbor coastal predicate to ship entry; Existing passenger checks reject an inland port. The old R-0004 claim that every own colony qualifies is stale. Its full adjacent-colony movement journey is not established by those passenger checks.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Water → own coastal colony → return to water succeeds with normal movement charges and identical cargo/passengers.

2. From a coastal colony next to an inland colony in a current-schema fixture, attempting to sail inland is rejected without changing location, movement or cargo.

3. Eight-direction water adjacency, including diagonal water or an isolated lake, qualifies as coastal under the existing R-0022 decision; do not add an ocean-connectivity condition.

4. Preserve passenger landing as a distinct action. Empty-ship movement to ordinary land and moves beyond the map are rejected; save/load preserves a legal port location.
