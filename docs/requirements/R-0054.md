## 1. Summary

1. The player can sail into an own coastal colony and back to water, while an inland colony cannot provide a shortcut across land. Preserve cargo and the separate passenger-landing action.

2. Source inspection on 2026-10-15 at `77dd824a` confirms that R-0022 already supplies `Model.isOwnedCoastalPort` to ship movement. MOVE-002 still describes the retired predicate; the missing work is explicit movement, preview, AI and persistence acceptance.

3. This selected A05 slice verifies the [coastal-port rule](../gap-analysis.md#coastal-port-movement). It does not reopen founding spacing, automatic passenger unloading (CARGO-007), terrain costs, high-seas travel or general path planning.

## 2. Behavior

1. During the player's prepared turn, select a ship on water with six movement points and loaded goods/equipped passengers. Preview the adjacent own port on the map, cancel with Escape without changes, then use the normal map drag: arrive in the port with five points and identical holds, passengers and equipment. Save/Continue retains that state; returning to adjacent water leaves four points. Escape also cancels a subsequent preview when the Colonies menu retains keyboard focus; outside a preview it still closes that menu normally.

2. A valid water neighbor in any of eight directions makes the colony coastal, including diagonal water and an isolated lake. Every accepted one-square entry/exit costs one ship point. Ground units retain ordinary eight-direction land movement and cannot move onto water without a separate transport action.

3. In a deliberately scripted current-schema fixture, the coastal colony is adjacent to an inland colony. Once the ship is in port, an attempted inland sailing step has no sailing path and is rejected without changing its place, movement, cargo or passengers. A ship without eligible passengers also offers no landing preview there. An empty ship cannot enter ordinary land; map borders cannot be crossed. These fixtures do not change ordinary founding spacing.

4. Passenger landing remains an explicit, separate action. An eligible passenger may still be offered a landing preview on adjacent land; that is not evidence that the ship can sail there. Canceling the choice preserves the complete load. AI uses the same coastal eligibility: it can enter a legal port and cannot take an inland route when its water exit is blocked.
