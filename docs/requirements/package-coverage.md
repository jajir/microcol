## Continuing contracts

1. Every selected change retains [model ownership and preconditions](../architecture.md), [functional regression](../functional-tests.md), [UX](../ux.md), [graphics](../graphics.md) and [audio](../audio.md). Current-schema saves include the new state; previews, stale commands, cancellation and Continue cannot duplicate outcomes.
2. P01 reference expectations come from accepted source tables and examples, independently of runtime constants. Rule decisions remain in the catalogue and each affected active requirement; the [open-point index](open-points.md) is a navigation snapshot.
3. UC-119 remains ongoing regression for each accepted change. A past full suite proves that revision, not all future changes. Publication is independently verified under [development delivery](../development-builds.md).

## Explicit boundaries

1. Setup UC-001/002 remains **Nastavení**, separate from **Hra** acceptance; it is captured, not waived. All nine mandatory matrices, nine report families and four integrated journeys remain required under the [UC catalogue](../use-cases.md#povinne-matice-a-obsah-prehledu).
2. DOS hardware/installer parity, exact historical shortcuts/artwork, epitaph prose, the historical appendix and completion of the optional campaign are outside the ordinary-game rules acceptance. R-0207 updates existing tutorial guidance without commissioning a new campaign.
3. Current development explicitly requires no historical-save migrations. Stable update/rollback preserves user files; any public-version compatibility promise is a distinct decision in R-0240, not authorization for development migrations.
4. Windows, RPM, Steam achievements/Cloud/Deck certification, new translations, full screen-reader certification, additional music and a predetermined effects count are not made mandatory by illustrative estimates. They require explicit selection. Existing Mac ARM and Intel development packages are credited, not recaptured as new architectures.
5. Art direction follows the current visual brief. Missing assets follow feature needs; old asset counts and source observations are not current defects. Human-study samples, performance budgets, balance targets and supported release platforms remain open where not decided.
6. Steam account, commercial, review and launch activities are captured as publisher work. This preparation does not submit a store, spend fees, launch a product, recruit participants or promise indefinite support. Historical budget/support estimates are planning scenarios rather than approved feature quantities.
