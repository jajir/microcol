## 1. Summary

1. Native relations need to react consistently when Europeans provoke a tribe or reconcile with a settlement.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `8f3ce118` reports no native relation consumers for these events. This is a source assessment, not a runtime test.
3. This requirement owns exact T/L event deltas and one-time application; the originating actions remain with their combat, tribute, trade, mission and rumor owners.

## 2. Behavior

1. Attacking a native unit adds T+10 and its home's L+10; attacking a settlement adds +20 to both, and destroying it adds another +20. A capital doubles its settlement increments. Disturbing a burial site adds T+30 to the nearest nation. Tribute adds +10 to T and L even when refused.
2. A completed trade reduces that settlement's L by 2. A gift reduces L by 5 per 100 goods, proportionally. At a capital, double the reduction and apply it to T too. Each complete 50 muskets/horses sold to natives adds +5 T; retain partial sold-quantity counters across trades and Save/Continue. These effects reach only the relevant European/nation/settlement pair, preserve fractions and clamp 0..100.
3. Canceled, rejected or merely previewed actions do not apply an event. A completed attack/transaction applies its event once, including after Save/Continue; a second distinct action can apply another event. The player receives intelligible feedback without revealing hidden opponent state.
4. UC-061 checks unit, ordinary/capital settlement, burial, tribute refusal, trade, gift and peaceful aftermath. UC-065 checks the real attack/tribute owner also emits the appropriate relation event; this requirement alone does not prove combat or treasury settlement.

5. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0129](../gap-analysis.md#r-0129-refinement), [R-0152](../gap-analysis.md#r-0152-refinement), [R-0166](../gap-analysis.md#r-0166-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.
