# R-0197 — Keep and display the top ten Hall-of-Fame results

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P25/P27; [SCORE-009](../gap-analysis.md#end-conditions-and-scoring); [UC-112](../use-cases.md#konec-hry-ulozeni-a-informace) (Hra).
4. Dependencies: final score [R-0195](R-0195.md)/[R-0196](R-0196.md), scored result identity from [R-0191](done/R-0191.md)/[R-0192](done/R-0192.md)/[R-0193](R-0193.md), current save/storage rules. [R-0194](R-0194.md) also records a confirmed Retire.
5. Primary area: [A10](areas.md#a10).
6. Related areas: A03 result and leaderboard display; A04 cross-game persistence; A01 access/navigation.
7. Origin: cross-game ranked results residual; existing within-game statistics do not keep a Hall of Fame.

## 1. Summary

1. Players can see the finished result breakdown and a persistent top-ten ranking across games.
2. This requirement owns cross-game record/ranking/display, not score arithmetic or the choice to retire.

## 2. Behavior

1. Store at most ten scored game results ordered by higher final score first, then earlier completion record. Keep one entry per game-result identity; finishing, reloading or opening the list again does not duplicate it.
2. Automatically scored victory, loss and date-end results, plus a confirmed scored Retire, enter the same ranking path. Always show the just-finished result and its component breakdown even if it does not enter the top ten. A qualifying result survives app restart with score, difficulty and identifying nation/player data. An Exit or canceled Retire records nothing; later unscored play cannot replace the frozen result.
3. UC-112 checks 11+ results, score/tie ordering, boundary admission, nonqualifying breakdown, restart and duplicate prevention.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model/result storage records one immutable result identity and applies stable top-ten ordering; GUI shows the model-calculated R-0195/0196 breakdown and ranking. Keep this record separate from opening/modifying an existing save.
2. Proposed model/UI/UC-112 checks cover capacity/order/ties, restart persistence, duplicate result identity and unscored exclusion under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
3. Current result: capture only; no Hall-of-Fame implementation or runtime acceptance is claimed.
