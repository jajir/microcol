# R-0143 — Apply national and cross-port market pressure

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P15; TRADE-006/007; UC-058 (Hra). See the [market rules](../gap-analysis.md#trade-and-the-european-market), [accepted pressure formula](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education) and [market use case](../use-cases.md#imigrace-a-evropsky-obchod).
4. Dependencies: existing R-0020 shared transaction quote and [R-0052](done/R-0052.md) national update boundary. Dutch half-pressure is [R-0144](R-0144.md), not an independent price store.
5. Primary area: [A06](areas.md#a06).
6. Related areas: A04 saved market/update state; A02 chosen nation; A03 visible bid/ask; A10 tax enters transaction quote, not pressure.
7. Origin: TRADE-006/007 gaps in the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md#trade-and-the-european-market).

## 1. Summary

1. A player's heavy sales or purchases should move each nation's market price, with weaker pressure reaching other European ports and quiet goods recovering toward their starting price.
2. The 2026-10-17 source catalogue at `ffae3396` reports one fixed shared market, not national histories. R-0020 already owns the quote and tax arithmetic used to settle an individual transaction.
3. This requirement owns per-nation, per-good bid/ask and signed pressure; R-0144 supplies the Dutch factor. It does not change a transaction's already confirmed amount.

## 2. Behavior

1. Each nation's tradable commodity starts at its existing bid and spread; a missing tradable quote starts bid 2, ask 4. A confirmed sale increases signed pressure; purchase decreases it. Apply the full traded quantity to the actor's market and one quarter to each other European market, retaining fractional remainder.
2. Every +100 pressure lowers bid by 1; every −100 raises it by 1. Retain remainder, bound bid to 1–20 and set ask to bid plus its initial spread. At either bound discard outward pressure so it cannot bank a later reversal. On a market update with no trade in that commodity since its previous update, bid moves one step toward its initial value.
3. Settle each trade at its confirmed quote, then update affected markets. A rejected/canceled trade has no pressure, money or cargo effect. Markets can diverge by nation; Save/Continue retains prices, fractional pressure and whether a quiet recovery update already ran.
4. [R-0144](R-0144.md) halves the pressure received by the Dutch market. Full Dutch UC-058 acceptance requires that integration; this requirement supplies the common market engine without duplicating the national rule.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model owns persisted per-nation market quotes, spread, pressure fractions and recent-trade flags. Feed every accepted buy/sell through the existing settled quote, then update market states once; UI and AI read the same model quote.
2. Not prepared for development. On selection, inspect all trade entry points and the market update stage; preserve fractional one-quarter effects across single-item trades and save/Continue.
3. Proposed checks: one and repeated sales/purchases, own versus other port, +/−100 with fractional remainder, 1/20 bounds, quiet recovery, confirmed-price settlement, canceled/stale trade and disk Continue. Add a named UC-058 player path under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: captured only; no gameplay change or runtime test is claimed.
