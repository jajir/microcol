## 1. Summary

1. The player needs artillery to be a buildable unit with a real damaged state and the appropriate combat capabilities.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Currently accepted attack roles omit the artillery lifecycle. The catalogue records absent artillery construction and no complete damage outcome.

3. Scope: this military role outcome. Ordinary attack eligibility remains delivered; this does not complete all combat or the whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Refined on 2026-10-16 under the authorized continuing implementation run.

1. An artillery project requires the armory or higher eligible facility. The produced unit has the chart’s capabilities and supported purchase/transport behavior. European artillery purchases use the agreed base 500 and increase only the artillery type’s next price by 50 per purchase, using R-0077 history and stale-quote validation.

2. Full artillery uses the agreed 7/5 attack/defense and damaged artillery 5/3; a qualifying loss changes or removes it according to the explicitly chosen outcome rules.

3. Cancel or reject an attack causes no damage. Save/load retains the artillery type, damage and equipment.

4. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Use the full-to-damaged-to-destroyed artillery progression and the explicit outdoor/native-raid modifiers.

5. Provisional construction/movement choice under the run's authorization: a new gun costs 200 hammers and 100 tools and has one movement point per owner turn. These values are a project balance choice, not a claim about the manual. It occupies one ship hold, cannot work in a colony or carry goods, and can use the shared fortify order. Damage changes strength, not identity, movement allowance or hold size; it is not repaired by Continue or a new turn.

6. The player discovers artillery in the European purchase list and eligible colony projects. The battle preview shows its current strength and outdoor penalty. A first defeat leaves a visibly damaged gun on its tile, with both participants out of movement; a second defeat removes it. Cancellation and changed quotes leave damage, resources, ownership, location and combat random state unchanged.

7. Apply the outdoor penalty to each artillery participant outside its own fortified colony, using its current tile even when the attacker targets an adjacent tile. A unit's personal fortify order does not replace a colony stockade/fort/fortress. Native-raid acceptance remains pending actual native combat integration; European AI is not a native opponent.
