## 1. Summary

1. The player needs artillery to be a buildable unit with a real damaged state and the appropriate combat capabilities.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Currently accepted attack roles omit the artillery lifecycle. The catalogue records absent artillery construction and no complete damage outcome.

3. Scope: this military role outcome. Ordinary attack eligibility remains delivered; this does not complete all combat or the whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. An artillery project requires the armory or higher eligible facility. The produced unit has the chart’s capabilities and supported purchase/transport behavior.

2. Full artillery uses the agreed 7/5 attack/defense and damaged artillery 5/3; a qualifying loss changes or removes it according to the explicitly chosen outcome rules.

3. Cancel or reject an attack causes no damage. Save/load retains the artillery type, damage and equipment.

4. Shared behavior accepted on 2026-10-15 ([Q-08](../gap-analysis.md#q-08-combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Use the full-to-damaged-to-destroyed artillery progression and the explicit outdoor/native-raid modifiers. These decisions unblock policy; individual design, dependencies and acceptance checks still require refinement.
