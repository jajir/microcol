## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a damaged ship to reach an eligible repair location and become usable again according to a clear schedule.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The catalogue identifies missing ship damage/repair behavior; a completed drydock alone is not evidence of operational repair.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. When a ship requires repair, select the agreed eligible destination, including Europe when appropriate, and show it to the player.

2. While damaged or repairing, movement, combat and cargo actions follow explicit restrictions; the ship cannot act as fully operational.

3. Complete the agreed repair interval once → restore the allowed capability and report completion.

4. If a destination becomes unavailable, including last-resident colony destruction under R-0060, revalidate the nearest eligible repair destination and apply the agreed fallback without losing ship/cargo. Save/load retains damage, destination and remaining repair progress.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Select and revalidate the nearest eligible repair destination, retain inaccessible cargo and apply explicit transfer/repair duration.

6. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): Run repair progress once at the owning turn boundary under [national turn processing](../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing); Continue preserves completed and remaining repair work.
