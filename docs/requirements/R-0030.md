## 1. Summary

1. Make a tax demand a persistent player decision with visible economic consequences rather than an unexplained rate change.

2. The catalogue records a persisted king tax, initially zero in free play, but no increase/protest event or boycott state.

3. One repeatable tax-event lifecycle with accept/protest. Back-tax payment, Fugger and the full political event generator remain separate GOV-003/founder work.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for individual refinement; capture does not approve implementation.

1. Present the proposed new rate and consequences before action. Accept changes the rate once; protest follows the agreed goods-loss, sentiment and boycott policy.

2. Protest identifies the affected owned stock and commodity before confirmation. It destroys only the specified amount, applies its accepted sentiment effect once and boycotts that commodity.

3. Boycott blocks both ordinary Europe purchases and sales of the commodity through shared model eligibility, including equipment paths where applicable; other goods remain tradable.

4. An unresolved demand survives save/load without a second event. Closing its presentation does not silently accept or protest; the agreed turn policy determines when a choice is required.

5. Invalid/stale choice leaves treasury, stock, tax and relations unchanged. Report the actual outcome and ensure repeated confirmation cannot apply it twice.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model the pending decision, outcome and commodity restriction through player/king state and persistence. Use shared trade validation and R-0031 sentiment operations.

2. Implement a controlled, documented event trigger for the reference game and its UI decision/report; do not claim a final Crown strategy from a test trigger.

3. Proposed `TC_tax_demand_test`: accept, protest, unavailable selected stock, pending reload, repeated confirm and buy/sell boycott. Model tests assert atomic resolution and unchanged unrelated goods.
