## 1. Summary

1. A conquered native settlement may leave one valuable treasure train; Cortes guarantees a larger one without paying the same reward twice.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue reports no treasure role or native-conquest producer. This is source assessment only.
3. This requirement owns the treasure identity, conquest draw/value and eventual claim; R-0134 owns the six-hold ship reservation.

## 2. Behavior

1. For one valid native conquest without Cortes, one saved 50% draw determines whether a treasure train worth 500 gold appears, or 1000 for a capital. With Cortes, exactly one train always appears worth double the matching base: 1000 or 2000. Failed/canceled conquest makes no draw or reward.
2. The train retains stable owner, location, value and nonattacking zero-strength role through movement, loading, loss and Save/Continue. It cannot initiate combat; R-0134 requires six free ship holds to board it. No gold is also credited at creation if the train is the value carrier.
3. The player may carry the six-hold train by galleon to home Europe for redemption, or leave it in an owned coastal colony for the King's galleon to collect for a price. Each legal route settles the retained face value at most once and removes the train once, applying only that route's agreed deductions. Invalid/canceled collection leaves train and treasury unchanged; loss before collection pays nothing. Home Europe's closure at independence must not be bypassed by pretending the own-ship route stays open.
4. UC-024/065/086 check ordinary/capital and father absent/present, both controlled draws, six-hold own-galleon/Europe and royal coastal pickup, one net settlement and Continue.

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0175-refinement)): Own-galleon delivery to home Europe pays the normal current tax but no transport fee. Royal pickup first deducts half the face value as transport charge, then applies normal tax to the remainder. Show the exact net proceeds for explicit confirmation in an owned coastal colony; collect on the next owner turn only if the train remains eligible. Declaration closes both redemption routes; existing trains remain movable but cannot be redeemed through an invented new buyer, and declaration warns of this consequence. Preserve the ordinary whole-transaction rounding convention and exactly-once removal/payment.

At refinement, decide any own-ship Europe deduction, the royal fee/tax, pickup timing and confirmation, and what happens to both routes after home Europe closes. Keep this single value owner authoritative so conquest, transport and treasury cannot each award gold.
