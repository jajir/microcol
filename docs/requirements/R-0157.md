## 1. Summary

1. A visitor entering a native settlement needs to see only actions permitted by its role and current state, with a safe way to decline.
2. The dated catalogue reports no native interaction choice. This is source inspection, not a player test.
3. This requirement owns the action chooser and entry/cancel boundary, not the effects of chief visits, teaching, trade, tribute, mission or attack.

## 2. Behavior

1. At a known settlement, an eligible visitor sees role-appropriate scouting/chief, teaching, trade, tribute, missionary or attack choices, their prerequisites and an intelligible reason when unavailable. A teaching choice shows that settlement's persisted card-marked skill. The player can back out before accepting an action.
2. Selecting an action invokes its shared model operation; availability is advisory, and a state change before confirmation is revalidated. An ineligible role cannot trigger the action through another UI path.
3. Canceling the choice does not consume a visit, move/kill the visitor, change relations, cargo or treasury, or begin a mission. Save/Continue does not replay a pending unconfirmed choice. A confirmed action follows its own requirement's cost and effect.
4. UC-062–067 each checks one admitted and one refused role, including scout, free/servant/criminal, loaded carrier, military and missionary paths.

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0157-refinement)): At map creation prefer native-teachable skills matching locally productive resources and select uniformly among those candidates with the map seed. If no such candidate exists, choose uniformly from the complete accepted native-teachable list. Persist the selected skill; visits and reload never change it.
