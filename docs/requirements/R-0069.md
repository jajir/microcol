## 1. Summary

1. The player needs exploration range to follow the unit’s role and observation abilities rather than accidentally following remaining movement.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): R-0019 fixes presentation of unexplored terrain while retaining sight derived from movement speed. The accepted role-specific ranges below still need implementation and acceptance.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Move an ordinary unit and a longer-sighted role to the same square in equivalent worlds → reveal the agreed radius for each.

2. Spending movement without changing observation capability does not shrink explored knowledge.

3. Map edges clip sight safely; upgrades/equipment that do not alter sight do not change the radius accidentally.

4. Save/load retains explored terrain. Existing hidden overlays and tooltips stay hidden outside permitted knowledge.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [exploration, work and transport](../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport)): Use explicit one-square ordinary land sight and two-square scout/ship sight with eight-direction distance, independent of movement speed.
