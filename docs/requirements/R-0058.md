## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to know how much imported food can be accepted and when it creates residents, without losing the quantity that should trigger growth.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Food has a 200 warehouse limit and birth is evaluated once per colony update. Nonfood wagon unloading in R-0024 does not settle food import timing or a surplus of 400.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Import food taking the effective stock from below 200 to exactly/above 200 → show accepted quantity and the agreed timing of the birth before confirmation.

2. Cancel or reject a stale transfer → food remains with its original carrier/warehouse, without a birth or stock loss.

3. For effective surpluses 399, 400 and 401, apply the explicitly agreed single/multiple-birth policy and retain or report the exact remainder.

4. The same policy applies to ship and wagon imports and survives save/load before and after the update. Never clip stock to 199 to avoid handling the threshold.

5. Shared behavior accepted on 2026-10-15 ([Q-10](../gap-analysis.md#q-10-economy-production-and-education)): Food imports wait for the next colony update; each complete 200 of post-consumption food produces a birth, retaining the remainder. These decisions unblock policy; individual design, dependencies and acceptance checks still require refinement.
