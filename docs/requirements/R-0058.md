## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to know how much imported food can be accepted and when it creates residents, without losing the quantity that should trigger growth.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Food has a 200 warehouse limit and birth is evaluated once per colony update. Nonfood wagon unloading in R-0024 does not settle food import timing or a surplus of 400.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected on 2026-10-16; design is refined below, with R-0057 focused acceptance passed. This does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance, refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Import food taking the effective stock from below 200 to exactly/above 200 → show accepted quantity and the agreed timing of the birth before confirmation.

2. Cancel or reject a stale transfer → food remains with its original carrier/warehouse, without a birth or stock loss.

3. Effective post-consumption surpluses 399, 400 and 401 produce respectively 1, 2 and 2 newborns, retaining 199, 0 and 1 food.

4. The same policy applies to ship and wagon imports and survives save/load before and after the update. Never clip stock to 199 to avoid handling the threshold.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Food imports wait for the next colony update; each complete 200 of post-consumption food produces a birth, retaining the remainder.

6. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): apply this requirement within [national turn processing](../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing). Resume the saved completed boundary without repeating production, arrivals or action replenishment; future pending choices must preserve earlier completed updates.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Remove the food-only 200 storage ceiling in `ColonyWarehouse`, retaining nonfood limits and exact atomic carrier transfers. Keep representable nonnegative quantities and existing persistence validation. Food may accumulate between updates; in `Colony` create one newborn and report for each complete 200 after production and consumption, retain the remainder, and never charge newborns in that update. Reuse the [national update boundary](../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing).

2. Reuse `PanelColonyGoods` and `ChooseGoodsDialog` for both ship and wagon unloading. Before confirming food imports, show the accepted quantity and explain that growth waits for the next colony update, after consumption. Opening/canceling does not change stocks. Recheck the exact current source, location, owner and available amount on confirmation; reject stale transfers atomically and reuse the existing refresh path. Preserve nonfood overflow confirmation and limits.

3. Extend shared warehouse-transfer/model tests for ship and wagon imports, cancellation/read-only preview, stale rejection, persistence before/after growth, and the 399/400/401 matrix alongside R-0057.
