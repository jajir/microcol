## 1. Summary

1. Once a rival has multiple colonies, it needs to sequence food, production, transport and later expansion without dropping established colonies from its plan.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` records only the first-colony slice; no multi-colony scheduler is verified.
3. This requirement owns coordination and recovery across colony/economy/expansion capabilities, including the residual decision to repeat expansion beyond R-0092's second colony. It does not silently declare R-0092 to have implemented a third colony, nor invent worker, reserve or purchase formulas.

## 2. Behavior

1. Across at least two established colonies, the rival continues R-0090's food-first, spare-worker lumber policy at each and runs eligible R-0091 transport/trade objectives. After R-0092's bounded second-colony outcome, it may prepare a third or later colony through shared legal founding only under a separately agreed continuing-expansion trigger/reserve; that further expansion is owned here, not claimed as delivered by R-0092.
2. Each owner turn advances a finite set of legal objectives, with clear priority when food, shipment and founding compete. Failed stock, lost colony, occupied field, blocked carrier or unavailable founder leads to bounded retry/retirement; no resource, cargo or person is duplicated.
3. Save/Continue retains still-valid objective ownership and actual cargo/stocks. UC-068 checks a seeded multi-colony economy over a stated horizon, including one disruption/recovery and, once the trigger is agreed, one legal further colony; it does not require identical moves to the original game AI.

4. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0172-refinement)): Priority is famine prevention, immediate defense, completion of ongoing transport, supplies, export, then expansion. Prepare at most one new colony concurrently, only when every existing colony has nonnegative food balance, five turns of food-consumption reserve and its agreed tool supply target met; retain R-0092 founder/population safeguards. Verify a 40-turn prepared multi-colony scenario with two colonies, one transport disruption/recovery and founding a third.
