## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a naval battle to report a coherent fate for both ships, their passengers and their goods.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Frigate attack initiation works. It does not establish the full naval outcome choices, prize cargo rules or damaged-ship state.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Individually refined on 2026-10-17; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Refined on 2026-10-17 under the continuing authorized implementation run.

1. From a valid confirmed ship attack, apply the agreed naval strength/outcome rules and report whether the loser sinks or is damaged.

2. Sinking removes the ship and handles every passenger/cargo item under the explicit loss policy; no orphaned identity remains.

3. If goods are captured, transfer only the agreed eligible quantity within the victor’s free capacity and report the remainder.

4. Damaged ships enter a valid repair path owned by R-0098. Save/load preserves the resolved outcome without repeating losses or loot.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Use the agreed naval probability, sinking/damage branch, passenger fate and capacity-limited plunder.

6. Use base ship strengths 2/6/10/8/16/24 for caravel/merchantman/galleon/privateer/frigate/man-o-war. Apply the accepted attacker +50% and no land-terrain or land-fortification bonuses. Select the strongest legal defender, then stable ID. One saved combat draw chooses the winner, a second chooses sinking below 0.5 or damage otherwise. Both participants lose remaining movement.

7. Loot goods only, ordered by current European per-unit sale value, then stable goods identity and source hold. Fill compatible partially occupied holds before empty holds, taking whole units up to actual capacity. The victim's passengers never become prizes. Report taken goods, destroyed leftovers/passengers when sunk, or preserved leftovers/passengers and the repair destination when damaged. No repair endpoint means actual sinking with its losses. Save/load does not repeat loot or battle draws.

8. Before confirmation, show the actual strengths, win probability and the possible loss/repair/cargo consequences. Cancel or a stale offer leaves diplomacy, RNG, movement, goods and passengers unchanged. A completed battle gives visible results and leads naturally into the existing repair overview; no separate repair command is required. Privateer treaty exceptions remain R-0099.
