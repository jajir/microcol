# R-0237 — Prepare the Steam product and rights checklist

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P32; [Steam release estimate](../implementation-estimate.md#steam-release-readiness-linux-and-macos), [store/commercial scope](../implementation-estimate.md#missing-publishing-and-delivery-work).
4. Dependencies: stable product identity [R-0236](R-0236.md), player guide/support destination [R-0232](R-0232.md), store media [R-0238](R-0238.md). Account-side state was not inspected in the dated estimate.
5. Primary area: [A01](areas.md#a01).
6. Related areas: A03 player-facing copy/media; P32 publishing.
7. Origin: Steam publisher/store setup and shipped-content rights, not gameplay or mandatory Steamworks API integration.

## 1. Summary

1. The Steam product has an approved publisher, accurate store information, required disclosures and a rights-checked release-content list.
2. This requirement owns the reviewable product/checklist preparation; identity, agreements, bank/tax submission and account permissions require the publisher's authorized actions.

## 2. Behavior

1. Record account/app ownership and permissions, store language/platform/system requirements, pricing/regions, content survey/ratings and every shipped binary, art, font, audio and notice. The listing describes actual game content and supported platforms rather than planned features.
2. Keep a traceable rights/source/license and required credit for included material; omit developer files and the historical manual from product depots. Confirm release-time Steam checklist requirements against current official documentation before submission; the 2026-10-02 estimate is a planning snapshot.
3. Store data and legal/account tasks have named owners and completion evidence. No fee payment, publisher registration, disclosure or store submission is claimed by capture.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Prepare a product/content inventory and draft store/commercial fields for publisher review; check the live partner checklist when account access exists, then complete authorized account-side actions and record results.
2. Current result: captured release preparation; no Steam account state or permissions have been verified here.

## 4. Open questions

1. Who is the publishing entity and authorized account owner, and which target regions/prices are approved? These are genuine commercial decisions external to the codebase.
