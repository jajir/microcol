## 1. Summary

1. The player needs the privateer’s special diplomatic attack behavior without weakening ordinary ships’ treaty checks.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): R-0028 intentionally applies ordinary European war confirmation and excludes an unsupported privateer role.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Individually refined on 2026-10-17; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Refined on 2026-10-17 under the continuing authorized implementation run.

1. Use an eligible privateer against a valid foreign ship → apply the manual’s privateer hostility exception and explain its diplomatic consequence.

2. An ordinary frigate attacking a peaceful target still requires the existing declaration confirmation.

3. Privateer status does not waive adjacency, terrain, knowledge, movement or live-target checks; it does not grant land attacks.

4. Cancel or stale confirmation changes neither relation nor battle state. Save/load preserves the resulting diplomatic and unit state.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Use the privateer attack/counterattack exception without declaring war, preserving observed ownership and all ordinary battle validation.

6. Both directions of the exception preserve the existing relation, including a pre-existing war. This is permission for an otherwise legal naval attack, not diplomatic hostility toward the owner's other units. An ordinary frigate against another ordinary ship still declares war after explicit confirmation.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Reuse shared attack preparation and R-0097 naval outcomes. Add one model-owned query for the ship-to-ship privateer exception; do not change global European relations or make peaceful land units hostile. A legal attacking ship may attack a foreign privateer, or a privateer may attack a foreign ship, without declaration. Peaceful colony/land protections and ordinary ship attack eligibility remain in force.

2. Bind the order to the actual original relation as well as participants and roles. Revalidate the same exception and relation before RNG, AP, cargo or diplomacy changes. Reuse the existing strongest legal defender selection and all movement/visibility/terrain checks. AI uses the same model query; do not add a separate GUI-only attack route or grant noncombatant ships new attack ability.

3. The shared review visibly states that this action does not declare war, including when the normal advisor is hidden. Show observed participants using the existing visibility rules; do not reveal an unseen owner's identity. Preserve ordinary frigate-versus-ordinary-ship declaration, cancel and stale rejection behavior. The R-0097 outcome dialog and repair journey remain shared.
