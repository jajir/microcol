## 1. Summary

1. The player needs the privateer’s special diplomatic attack behavior without weakening ordinary ships’ treaty checks.

2. Current situation: R-0028 intentionally applies ordinary European war confirmation and excludes an unsupported privateer role.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Use an eligible privateer against a valid foreign ship → apply the manual’s privateer hostility exception and explain its diplomatic consequence.

2. An ordinary frigate attacking a peaceful target still requires the existing declaration confirmation.

3. Privateer status does not waive adjacency, terrain, knowledge, movement or live-target checks; it does not grant land attacks.

4. Cancel or stale confirmation changes neither relation nor battle state. Save/load preserves the resulting diplomatic and unit state.
