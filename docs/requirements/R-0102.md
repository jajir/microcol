## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to accept a tax increase or protest after seeing the consequences, with one persistent outcome.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Tax is persisted and used in trade/equipment quotes. There is no accepted demand/protest lifecycle or commodity boycott; the current quote calculation itself is already delivered.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected as requirement 16 of the Claude batch; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Accepted behavior; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Present a demand with the proposed rate and exact consequences. Accept changes the rate once; protest applies the agreed stock loss, support change and commodity boycott once.

2. Before protest confirmation, identify the colony, commodity and amount to lose. A stale stock/owner or invalid choice leaves all state unchanged.

3. Boycott blocks purchases and sales of that commodity, including affected European equipment paths and ordinary [custom-house exports](R-0085.md); other commodities remain tradable. The agreed revolution custom-house exception bypasses the boycott and retains its half-net proceeds rule.

4. Closing presentation does not choose an answer. Save/load retains an unresolved demand; repeated confirmation cannot apply it twice.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [support, production and congress](../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress), [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Use the explicit demand cadence, increment, protest stock choice, support effect and paused pending-choice behavior.

6. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): Use [national turn processing](../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing) for the Europe-event boundary. This requirement owns persistence of its deliberate pending-choice checkpoint and resumption of remaining work without replaying completed arrivals or updates.

7. Accepted capture rule (user decision, 2026-10-17): Liberty gained through protest stays with the colony and follows the [shared affiliation rule](../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress) on later European conquest or Crown occupation.
