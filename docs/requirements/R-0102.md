## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to accept a tax increase or protest after seeing the consequences, with one persistent outcome.

2. Current situation: Tax is persisted and used in trade/equipment quotes. There is no accepted demand/protest lifecycle or commodity boycott; the current quote calculation itself is already delivered.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Present a demand with the proposed rate and exact consequences. Accept changes the rate once; protest applies the agreed stock loss, support change and commodity boycott once.

2. Before protest confirmation, identify the colony, commodity and amount to lose. A stale stock/owner or invalid choice leaves all state unchanged.

3. Boycott blocks purchases and sales of that commodity, including affected European equipment paths; other commodities remain tradable.

4. Closing presentation does not choose an answer. Save/load retains an unresolved demand; repeated confirmation cannot apply it twice.
