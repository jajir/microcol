## 1. Summary

1. The player needs an alliance to have an agreed meaning and visible consequences, rather than a renamed peace label.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The current relation model deliberately supports peace/war only; alliances and Crown-driven diplomatic changes were excluded.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected for refinement on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Offer or receive an alliance through valid contact → show obligations and the proposed partner before acceptance.

2. Accept → persist the symmetric agreed relationship and apply only its defined privileges/obligations.

3. Refusal/cancel leaves the previous relationship. Breaking or ending an alliance is explicit and cannot bypass the existing confirmation of hostile acts.

4. Save/load retains the relationship and pending decisions. Crown/Franklin effects are identified dependencies before they can alter it.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [native and european relations](../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations)): Use the explicit alliance eligibility, mutual-defense/passage obligations, termination wait and Crown-war interaction.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Selected refinement: keep alliance attacks forbidden until explicit termination and its five-turn wait expire. Reuse shared diplomatic validation and add narrow allied passage permissions rather than redefining every foreign unit as friendly. Record actual attacks/captures separately from a war declaration for ten-turn eligibility. Inspect model relationship and game-rule operations; AI chooses bounded actions, GUI presents the same decisions under the [package responsibility map](../architecture.md). Keep rule validation before mutation and use current-schema fixtures.

2. Observation basis: the 2026-10-14 [migration baseline](migration-2026-10-14.md#verification-baseline). Source inspection and catalogue findings are not fresh acceptance of these examples; recheck the selected paths before choosing a design.

3. Verification to prepare: Extend relation transitions and AI permissions with player proposal/accept/refuse/terminate paths. Allocate any new scenario IDs during refinement; old unimplemented proposals do not reserve TC numbers. Follow the [functional test contract](../functional-tests.md).

4. Source inspection on 2026-10-16 found no persisted recent-attack history and no runtime Crown-forced-war event. Current movement blocks nonhostile foreign colonies and any foreign unit; allied entry must skip capture and must never grant workforce or cargo access. Mutual defense must plan all relation changes before attack mutation. This is preparation, not implementation or acceptance.
