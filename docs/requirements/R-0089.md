## 1. Summary

1. The player needs an alliance to have an agreed meaning and visible consequences, rather than a renamed peace label.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The current relation model deliberately supports peace/war only; alliances and Crown-driven diplomatic changes were excluded.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected for implementation as requirement 12 on 2026-10-17; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Accepted behavior; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Offer or receive an alliance through valid contact → show obligations and the proposed partner before acceptance.

2. Accept → persist the symmetric agreed relationship and apply only its defined privileges/obligations.

3. Refusal/cancel leaves the previous relationship. Breaking or ending an alliance is explicit and cannot bypass the existing confirmation of hostile acts.

4. Save/load retains the relationship and pending decisions. Crown/Franklin effects are identified dependencies before they can alter it.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [native and european relations](../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations)): Use the explicit alliance eligibility, mutual-defense/passage obligations, termination wait and Crown-war interaction.

6. Accepted Crown-war event (user decision, 2026-10-17; [native and european relations](../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations)): Every 20 complete rounds, select one random European colonial pair currently not at war, both before independence and both without Franklin. Apply the agreed Crown-war effect, including ending conflicting alliances. If no pair qualifies, skip the event; save/Continue does not repeat it.

7. Accepted overlap rule (user decision, 2026-10-18; [native and european relations](../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations)): when an alliance ends, including forced war, already co-located units stay in place and may leave. Further shared entry is forbidden without an alliance. Combat occurs from ordinary adjacent tiles after separation. Save/load retains valid positions without moving or deleting units.
