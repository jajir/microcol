## 1. Summary

1. The player needs stored horses to breed over time and a stable to improve that breeding, instead of remaining an inert building.
2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): horses can be stored, traded and used as equipment, but the existing colony update has no horse-breeding process. The manual building rows are already represented; adding fictional missing building types would not address this gap.
3. Scope: horse breeding and the stable's effect, including resources, capacity and continuity. Other industrial/religious building effects have separate owners.

## 2. Behavior

Refined on 2026-10-16; the accepted breeding and food-order rules apply.

1. With 0 or 1 stored horse, breed none; with 2 or 3, breed 1; with 4 or more, breed 2 before food/capacity limits. Charge one food only for each actual new horse, after resident consumption and population growth. With effective food 200, a colonist is born and no food remains for horses; with 201, a colonist is born and at most one horse is added.
2. Stables double the potential rate, and Large Stables has the same agreed provisional effect. Start with four horses and sufficient food: ordinary breeding adds two, Stables adds four. Build Stables through the construction queue: its effect begins on the following colony update, because construction completes after production.
3. Remaining food zero means no breeding; one remaining food permits at most one horse. At horse capacity minus one, add at most one and spend one food; a full horse warehouse adds none and spends none. No breeding occurs in an update that suffers famine, even if a survivor subsequently produces food.
4. Colony preview and actual result agree, including the interaction with resident consumption and famine. Opening a view does not breed horses.
5. Save immediately before/after the update and continue → preserve the stocks and produce no duplicate breeding or report.

6. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Use the agreed minimum stock, food/capacity limits and Stables multiplier; Large Stables provisionally has the same effect.

7. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): apply this requirement within [national turn processing](../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing). Resume the saved completed boundary without repeating production, arrivals or action replenishment; future pending choices must preserve earlier completed updates.
