# R-0132 — Enforce the 100-goods hold cap on every transfer

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P08; CARGO-001; UC-023 (Hra). See the [cargo rule](../gap-analysis.md#transport-ships-and-atlantic-travel) and [transfer use case](../use-cases.md#lode-naklad-a-obchodni-trasy).
4. Dependencies: existing goods-transfer operations and [R-0024](done/R-0024.md) wagon/warehouse UI; European trade quote and tax remain their own rules.
5. Primary area: [A06](areas.md#a06).
6. Related areas: A07 warehouse source/capacity; A03 quantity choice and rejection; A04 saved hold state.
7. Origin: CARGO-001 residual in the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md#transport-ships-and-atlantic-travel).

## 1. Summary

1. A player must never be able to put more than 100 units of one commodity in a goods hold, regardless of transfer route.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue source assessment at `ffae3396` credits existing clamped warehouse/carrier transfers but identifies an empty-hold Europe purchase operation without the same core cap. This is a source observation, not a fresh runtime check.
3. Scope: enforce one model-owned capacity rule across existing transfer and Europe purchase entry points; preserve completed wagon and passenger behavior.

## 2. Behavior

1. A goods hold contains at most 100 units of one commodity. Moving goods from warehouse, another carrier or Europe cannot exceed the source quantity, free space or 100-unit limit; partial quantities remain selectable.
2. At 99/100/101 offered units and empty/partly filled holds, the accepted amount never overfills. An incompatible commodity, no free space, stale source or invalid quantity rejects without moving goods or charging gold. The UI offers only a legal amount and explains changed-state rejection.
3. Cancel leaves source, hold and treasury unchanged. A legal transfer preserves the total goods quantity except for an explicitly confirmed trade. Save/Continue retains a valid hold and cannot turn a previously rejected amount into cargo.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Put the cap in the model's shared goods-hold admission, including the empty-slot Europe purchase API; UI reads model limits. Preserve existing transfer ownership, warehouse checks and pricing paths.
2. Not prepared for development. On selection, inspect all direct hold write/restore callers and current-schema fixtures; keep buy/transfer validation atomic.
3. Proposed checks: 99/100/101 and mixed-goods cases for warehouse, carrier and Europe paths, empty and partial holds, stale/cancel/no-gold-loss, disk round trip and named UC-023 player paths under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: captured only; no gameplay change or runtime test is claimed.
