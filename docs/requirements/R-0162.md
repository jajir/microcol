# R-0162 — Demand native tribute without duplicating gold or anger

2. Updated: 2026-10-20
3. Related: P19; tribute part of [NAT-016](../gap-analysis.md#native-nations-settlements-and-relations); [UC-065](../use-cases.md#domorode-narody) (Hra). See the [accepted tribute rule](../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations).
4. Dependencies: visitor chooser [R-0157](R-0157.md), brave/settlement strength [R-0150](done/R-0150.md)/[R-0173](R-0173.md), treasury [R-0159](R-0159.md), relation effects [R-0154](R-0154.md); [R-0174](R-0174.md) owns separate conquest.

## 1. Summary

1. An armed visitor may demand limited gold from a native settlement, but refusal still provokes anger.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` reports no native tribute transaction. This is source assessment, not tested gameplay.
3. This requirement owns role, strength/cooldown, payment and anger for one demand, not native conquest.

## 2. Behavior

1. Soldiers, dragoons and scouts may demand tribute; other roles cannot. A settlement pays `min(100, treasury)` gold, or up to 200 for a capital, only when the eligible armed force's strength is at least its defenders' strength. Otherwise it refuses. Actual gold leaves the settlement and reaches the demander once.
2. One European may demand from one settlement at most once per ten turns. A confirmed demand adds T+10 and local L+10 through R-0154 whether paid or refused. Merely previewing/canceling, an ineligible role or a cooldown rejection changes no treasury, history or anger.
3. UC-065 checks each eligible/ineligible role, threshold strength, ordinary/capital caps, empty treasury, refusal, tenth-turn boundary and Save/Continue without repeat payment/provocation.

4. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0162-refinement)): Count the demander plus its own soldiers, dragoons and scouts on the eight adjacent settlement tiles, excluding passengers and allied or distant troops. Sum base combat strengths without offensive terrain bonuses. Compare with actual defenders inside the settlement including the agreed settlement defense bonus; retain cooldown, payment caps and provocation on either confirmed result.
