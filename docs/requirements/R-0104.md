## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to select a founder, earn their arrival and inspect lasting Congress membership.

2. Current situation: The original sentiment requirement explicitly leaves Congress/founders open; construction metadata is not evidence of founder acquisition.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. After the agreed eligibility trigger, present candidates from the five founder categories and record the player’s chosen objective.

2. Accumulate the agreed bell progress once; reaching the threshold adds the founder once and reports arrival.

3. Congress shows membership and active effects. Reopening or Continue does not replay arrival or a one-time effect.

4. Cancel or invalid/stale choice preserves progress and membership. Each founder effect has a separately owned acceptance slice; acquisition alone does not complete UC-086–110.
