## 1. Summary

1. An eligible attacker can fight native defenders and conquer a native settlement, visibly changing the map only after a real victory.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue reports no native-settlement attack path. European-colony ownership transfer is a different completed/active slice and must not be reused as a false native conquest.
3. This requirement owns attack legality, settlement defense and removal. R-0175 owns possible treasure and Cortes' increase.

## 2. Behavior

1. Soldier, dragoon, scout and artillery roles may attack a known adjacent native settlement when ordinary attack prerequisites and relation confirmation are met. An unarmed colonist, wagon or treasure train cannot. A successful attack must overcome the settlement's actual defenders before conquest; a loss leaves it intact.
2. Spain receives its accepted +50% attack strength only against a native **settlement**, not a brave on open ground or a European colony. Strength explanation and battle result agree; no second bonus is added by a Spanish unit's equipment.
3. On victory, remove the conquered settlement once and retire its map interaction, then hand one conquest event to R-0175 for the treasure draw. The fate of associated claims is an explicit refinement question. Losing, canceling or stale confirmation changes neither settlement nor treasure. Continue does not recreate the settlement or roll treasure again.
4. UC-003/065 cover eligible and refused roles, defended win/loss, Spanish before/after modifier, ordinary/brave target exclusions, map disappearance and Save/Continue.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model validates target identity, defender selection and attack snapshot before any relation, movement, RNG or map mutation. Reuse R-0094/R-0173 resolution and the R-0150 native settlement record; emit a single durable conquest event for R-0175.
2. At refinement, decide fate of home-linked braves, missions/teaching and unclaimed land on removal. Do not silently convert the site into a European colony or use R-0096's resident-transfer path. UI presents visible eligibility and outcome from the model.
3. Proposed model and UC-003/065 checks cover Spanish-only modifier, actual defenders, no premature removal, canceled/stale attacks, dependent native state and once-only Continue under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no native conquest implementation or runtime acceptance is claimed.

## 4. Open questions

1. What becomes of surviving home-associated braves, an active mission or pupil, and native claims when their settlement is conquered? The accepted conquest/removal rule does not specify those dependent fates.
2. R-0150 chapter 4 item 1 owns the exact class/capital defense table question; resolve that prerequisite before this attack is ready, without making a second decision here.
