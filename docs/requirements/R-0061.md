## 1. Summary

1. The player needs expert tool production to conserve raw material while retaining the expert’s higher capacity.

2. Current situation: R-0023 supplies a purchasable master blacksmith. Expertise multiplies full output without multiplying full input; the accepted purchase journey still observes 3 ore producing 6 tools.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. At the agreed smith-house tier, an expert with six ore can produce six tools and consumes six ore; three ore yields three tools and zero ore yields none.

2. An ordinary worker retains the agreed three-ore → three-tool baseline with sufficient input.

3. Multiple workers share the actual ore stock; their combined consumption never exceeds it and no ore is consumed twice.

4. Preview, next-turn stocks and save/continue agree. Buying and transporting the expert remains the completed R-0023 behavior.
