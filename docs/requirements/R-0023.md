# R-0023 — Purchase an expert blacksmith and bring them home

1. Status: Refining
2. Updated: 2026-10-13
3. Related: P14, P05; IMM-004; UC-055 (Hra), limited expert-purchase variant. See the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md),
   [use cases](../use-cases.md) and [work packages](../implementation-plan.md#seznam-pracovnich-baliku).
4. Dependencies: [R-0022](R-0022.md) passenger transfer; existing Europe entry and funds.
5. Primary area: [A06](areas.md#a06).
6. Related areas: A07 [R-0009](R-0009.md) expert production; A05 specialty identity; A04 persistence.

## 1. Summary

1. Make the expert blacksmith reachable through ordinary free play so R-0009 can be accepted without a debug-only worker.

2. Existing capability: Europe has a purchase dialog with Galleon. TC22/TC_02 opens it and cancels without charge; Master Blacksmith is absent. Source inspection confirms expert constants are excluded from the priced registry and unsupported by the factory. The missing slice is specialist acquisition, not creation of a new general purchase screen.

3. One eligible specialty: expert blacksmith, bought independently of immigration candidates. Full university roster, recruitment pool and escalating ship prices remain separate.

## 2. Behavior

Target behavior for later implementation. The audit below separates existing
capability from missing steps; it does not accept these complete targets.

1. In Europe, discover a blacksmith purchase with specialty and exact model price. Confirm only when funds suffice and the current political state allows the purchase.

2. Acceptance deducts the quote once and creates one distinct expert on the player’s pier. It does not consume or replace an immigration candidate.

3. Cancel, insufficient funds, repeated/stale confirmation or lost eligibility creates no expert and charges nothing.

4. Load the expert on a ship and take them to an owned colony through ordinary controls. The work assignment identifies the specialty; R-0009 owns its precise production effect.

5. Save/load on the pier, aboard and in the colony preserves identity and specialty. Equipment changes must not erase expertise.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Extend the existing unit factory/type and player purchase operation for the one specialty, with authoritative eligibility and price; update current serialization fixtures.

2. Expose the purchase through the existing Europe buying flow. Do not emulate expertise through a UI label on an ordinary colonist.

3. Proposed `TC_buy_expert_blacksmith_test`: exact funds/one short, cancel, repeated confirmation, pier→colony and round-trip. Connect the successful journey to the planned R-0009 production scenario.

4. Verification: TC22/TC_02 passed on 2026-10-13: the complete visible purchase roster is Free Colonist, Frigate and Galleon; cancel preserves gold. Expert acquisition is still absent. Ship-purchase/trading regression remains covered separately by TC06. Final suite commands and publication evidence are maintained in the [functional test record](../functional-tests.md#free-play-audit-2026-10-13).

## 4. Open questions

1. The existing, currently unreachable MASTER_BLACKSMITH type already configures a price of 2,200 gold. Proposed first implementation: reuse that value rather than inventing another price. Q-10 still owns any later pricing-policy change; the current capability audit does not validate historical balancing.

2. Use the existing purchase dialog with a clear training category or a separate university screen? Blocking: no for this audit. Proposed implementation: reuse the existing purchase flow for this one specialty; a separate university screen is not necessary to test the missing transaction.
