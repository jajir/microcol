# R-0023 — Purchase an expert blacksmith and bring them home

1. Status: Implemented and locally verified; publication pending
2. Updated: 2026-10-13
3. Related: P14, P05; IMM-004; UC-055 (Hra), limited expert-purchase variant. See the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md),
   [use cases](../use-cases.md) and [work packages](../implementation-plan.md#seznam-pracovnich-baliku).
4. Dependencies: [R-0022](R-0022.md) passenger transfer; existing Europe entry and funds.
5. Primary area: [A06](areas.md#a06).
6. Related areas: A07 [R-0009](R-0009.md) expert production; A05 specialty identity; A04 persistence.

## 1. Summary

1. Make the expert blacksmith reachable through ordinary free play so R-0009 can be accepted without a debug-only worker.

2. Existing capability: Europe has a purchase dialog with Galleon. TC22/TC_02 opens it and cancels without charge; Master Blacksmith is absent. Source inspection confirms expert constants are excluded from the priced registry and unsupported by the factory. The missing slice is specialist acquisition, not creation of a new general purchase screen.

3. One eligible specialty: expert blacksmith, bought independently of immigration candidates. Full university roster, recruitment pool and escalating ship prices remain separate.

## 2. Behavior

Implementation is authorized by the active R-0019-and-later goal. These
manual-backed decisions define acceptance; they do not claim implementation.

1. In Europe, discover a Master Blacksmith hiring choice in the existing purchase dialog, identified as a specialist with the exact model price of 2,200 gold. Use a hiring caption to distinguish training from ships. Confirm only when funds suffice and the current political state allows the purchase. The model checks running/current player, live ownership, supported purchasable type, home-market access and funds before changing the treasury or unit registry. The fixed unit price is distinct from taxed equipment/goods trading.

2. Acceptance deducts the quote once and creates one distinct expert on the player’s pier. It does not consume or replace an immigration candidate.

3. Cancel, insufficient funds, repeated/stale confirmation or lost eligibility creates no expert and charges nothing. A dialog opening permits at most one accepted purchase; reopening permits another independent purchase. Validate the current state again on acceptance and explain rejection. Unsupported types must fail before charging or allocating an identity.

4. Load the expert on a ship and take them to an owned colony through ordinary controls. Landing leaves them outside the workforce. The name and work assignment identify the specialty; then assigning the blacksmith to the existing smithy uses the type's existing tools expertise. R-0009 owns the precise production effect; this requirement must make that real expert reachable, not substitute an ordinary colonist with a label.

5. Save/load on the pier, aboard and in the colony preserves identity and specialty. Equipment changes must not erase expertise.
