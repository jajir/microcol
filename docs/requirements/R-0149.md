## 1. Summary

1. A mission can add a native convert to an owned colony, where that person has distinct work and expiry rules.
2. The 2026-10-17 source catalogue at `8f3ce118` reports no convert acquisition/type/age path. This is a dated source assessment, not runtime proof.
3. This requirement owns one connected convert lifecycle from eligible mission event through placement, work and expiry; founding as an ordinary colonist, combat conversion and broader mission diplomacy remain outside it.

## 2. Behavior

1. Every fifth update of a nonhostile mission tests conversion once: 10% for ordinary or 20% for expert missionaries, doubled when its European owner has Sepulveda. This modifier applies only to an eligible conversion event, including an event after recruiting the founder, and is not an immediate convert grant. A success creates at most one convert, delivered to the nearest owned colony by land distance, then stable colony ID; with no reachable owned colony, skip without banking. Combat cannot trigger conversion.
2. The convert retains native affiliation and identity through Save/Continue. It may work outdoors at +1 over ordinary base output and may take otherwise legal non-manufacturing indoor work at −1; it cannot take manufacturing or another manually forbidden role, nor found a colony. The shared production order still applies; bonuses cannot create output from zero base.
3. A convert left unassigned for eight owner turns returns to its tribe once; assignment before expiry keeps it. The player sees acquisition, refused work and departure through normal feedback. A saved game does not reset age or replay a successful conversion/departure.
4. Controlled probability tests cover failure, ordinary/expert and Sepulveda thresholds, fifth-update boundary, nearest-colony tie, unreachable colony, no combat conversion, legal/illegal work and the eighth-turn boundary. Invalid work/founding commands leave state unchanged.
