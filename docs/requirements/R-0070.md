## 1. Summary

1. The player needs an explicit, consistent rule for what can be learned about foreign units after they leave observation. This is a proposed follow-up, not a retroactive defect in the accepted fog slice.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The delivered fog is accumulated exploration. A separate current-visibility layer and policy for remembered foreign objects were not accepted in R-0019.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected under the remaining-backlog authorization on 2026-10-16; refinement is underway; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Visit and then leave an area → keep explored terrain while foreign-unit information follows the agreed current/remembered-knowledge policy.

2. Unknown or stale foreign information must not become an actionable attack target or leak through tooltips, range overlays or reports.

3. Re-enter observation → update visible information consistently without erasing the explored map.

4. Continue a save → restore permitted knowledge without revealing hidden current positions.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [exploration, work and transport](../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport)): Retain discovered terrain but disclose current foreign units only inside present sight; this separate future behavior is now selected as policy, not implemented.

6. Accepted colony sight (user decision, 2026-10-17; [exploration, work and transport](../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport)): An owned colony reveals its own tile and eight neighboring tiles even with all residents inside and no unit outside. A foreign unit entering that area becomes visible; leaving all present sight hides its current position. Losing the colony removes that source of current sight while retaining explored terrain.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Inspection on 2026-10-16: `Player.isVisible` and `Visibility` represent accumulated explored terrain. Keep that meaning for terrain and `FirstColonyPlanner`; add a separate shared present-sight query combining R-0069 effective unit sight with the accepted radius-one sight of currently owned colonies. Never replace every `isVisible` call mechanically. `GamePaintService`, `RightPanelView`, `StatusBarMessageService`, movement animation events and target selection currently disclose objects through the explored predicate and must consume the appropriate current/remembered knowledge.

2. The accepted shared rule also remembers discovered colonies: last observed location, owner and date, visibly stale outside sight. Retain a player-owned snapshot rather than rendering a live foreign `Colony` outside observation; the current paint path reads live colony state. Update knowledge only from actual permitted observation, persist it, and do not refresh hidden ownership/population/buildings/cargo on load, hover or repaint. Determine the exact event/update owners and fixture representation before implementation.

3. Verification to prepare: Extend TC_26 with departure/re-entry, hidden-target paths, colony-only sight with all residents inside, foreign entry/exit, capture changing the sight owner and Continue without hidden-information disclosure. Allocate any new scenario IDs during refinement; old unimplemented proposals do not reserve TC numbers. Follow the [functional test contract](../functional-tests.md).

4. Current result: not implemented or accepted by this migration. Retain already working portions described above; record actual changes and checks only when this requirement is selected and delivered.
