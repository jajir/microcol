## 1. Summary

1. A rival with an eligible warship should threaten visible commerce when relations permit, using normal naval combat and cargo consequences.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` finds ships and ordinary combat paths but no purposeful naval strategy; this is source assessment only.
3. This requirement owns naval target selection and pursuit, not ship construction, voyage rules, privateer diplomacy or damage/loot resolution.

## 2. Behavior

1. At war, a rival chooses a known reachable hostile ship as a target, or patrols a known trade-lane region for an actual ship; it never attacks an abstract route object. Pursuit occurs only while its vessel can legally move/attack. Privateers use R-0099's exception rather than silently declaring war; other ships respect peace/alliance.
2. An actual naval encounter uses R-0097 damage, sinking, cargo and capture rules. No visible/reachable legal target, blocked travel, changed relation or lost attacker ends/replans in finite time without conjuring cargo or rerunning a battle.
3. Save/Continue retains objective or safely retires stale references. UC-068 observes a seeded commerce challenge, a peaceful no-attack case and bounded empty-water behavior.

4. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0171-refinement)): Prefer visible unarmed hostile carriers, then armed legal targets with at least 60% win probability; break equal priority by distance and then stable ID. Without a target patrol known sea within five tiles of an owned port without knowledge of hidden cargo or routes. A prepared scenario with reachable commerce must produce an encounter within ten turns. Retain the existing privateer attack exception and alliance restrictions.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. A08 naval AI chooses from known targets and issues shared model move/attack commands; A09 remains owner of outcome and diplomatic permission. Reuse R-0091 carrier objectives and R-0021 voyage state without changing either's pending policy.
2. On selection, agree target weighting, patrol geography and finite reference horizon; do not fold land attacks or native raids into this planner.
3. Proposed AI/model/UC-068 checks cover war/privateer/peace, known/unknown ship, blocked path, cargo conservation, repair/damage, bounded turn and Continue. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
