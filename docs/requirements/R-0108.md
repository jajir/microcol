## 1. Summary

1. The player needs all six specified ships to have their correct movement, cargo and availability, including corrections to already supported types.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Only frigate and galleon are currently registered ships. The frigate currently has four moves/one hold, while the reference requires six/four; the galleon has six moves/five holds rather than six/six. Constants alone do not establish complete acquisition, movement, persistence and rendering.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Use this ordinary movement/holds matrix for both existing and newly supported ships: caravel 4/2, merchantman 5/4, galleon 6/6, privateer 8/2, frigate 6/4 and man-of-war 6/6. Each needs a legal purchase, construction or event source.

2. Acquire, move, load to capacity, cross the Atlantic and unload → preserve owner, cargo and ship identity.

3. Full holds, incompatible passengers or invalid terrain reject without mutation. Noncombat vessels must not gain attack permission merely by sharing ship controls.

4. Save/load retains every new type and its state. Man-of-war and privateer special rules are explicit dependencies rather than generic-frigate behavior.

5. Shared behavior accepted on 2026-10-15 ([Q-02](../gap-analysis.md#q-02-calendar-and-starting-expeditions), [Q-08](../gap-analysis.md#q-08-combat-and-naval-outcomes), [Q-10](../gap-analysis.md#q-10-economy-production-and-education)): Use manual ship capacities/speeds, the chosen starting ships and purchase restrictions/prices; preserve the naval-combat boundary. These decisions unblock policy; individual design, dependencies and acceptance checks still require refinement.
