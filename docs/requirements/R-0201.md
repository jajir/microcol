## 1. Summary

1. The native advisor summarizes legitimately known nations, their relations and disclosed settlement facts.
2. This requirement owns viewpoint-filtered presentation, not native diplomacy or fresh exploration.

## 2. Behavior

1. List only known nations and current relation state. For a discovered settlement show permitted nation, class/capital and facts actually learned by contact or chief; do not reveal undiscovered locations, stock, teaching or two-square chief terrain before the corresponding event.
2. After introduction, an alarm change, new chief information or Save/Continue, reopening shows the right current/remembered facts without changing relation or consuming a visit. Another European player's private discoveries remain hidden.
3. UC-114 checks known/unknown nation, discovered/undiscovered settlement, pre/post chief, changed hostility and viewpoint isolation.

4. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0150](../gap-analysis.md#r-0150-refinement), [R-0152](../gap-analysis.md#r-0152-refinement), [R-0155](../gap-analysis.md#r-0155-refinement), [R-0159](../gap-analysis.md#r-0159-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior.
