## 1. Summary

1. Recruiting Drake makes the player's privateers stronger in both attack and defense.
2. R-0104 already persists Drake membership; no new recruitment path is needed. This analysis captures the remaining combat effect.
3. The bonus belongs in the shared naval strength calculation, not privateer diplomacy or cargo outcomes.

## 2. Behavior

1. An owned privateer has base combat strength 8 under R-0097. After its nation recruits Drake, apply +50% to its effective strength as attacker or defender, combined with the ordinary attack modifier by the accepted additive-percentage rule. Thus its base contribution becomes 12 before other applicable modifiers, not a second free ship or a treaty change.
2. An opponent's Drake does not strengthen this privateer; nonprivateer ships receive no Drake bonus. The battle preview and actual odds agree, and recruiting Drake affects existing and future owned privateers immediately.
3. Cancel/rejected battle makes no change or random draw. Save/Continue retains membership and yields the same strength without accumulating the modifier again. UC-087 checks both roles, before/after, opponent ownership, ordinary ships and continuation.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model's shared naval strength snapshot reads the combatant owner's R-0104 membership and adds one privateer-only +50% modifier; retain R-0097 outcomes and R-0099 peace exception unchanged. UI shows the model's breakdown.
2. Selected/refined on 2026-10-17: extend `CombatStrength` with the named additive founder modifier and include it in equality/hashCode used by frozen orders. `CombatStrengths.forBattle` reads the specific combatant owner’s congress members for privateers only; `DialogFight` renders the named localized breakdown. Exact privateer values are attack 12/defense 8 without Drake and attack 16/defense 12 with Drake. Model/UC-087 checks compare exact effective strength and probability in attack/defense with controlled results, alternate owner, ordinary ship and Continue under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
3. Current result: capture only; no gameplay change or runtime acceptance is claimed.
