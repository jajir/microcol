1. The player needs to buy an eligible specialist and use that person’s real skill after transport home.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Master blacksmith is supported and tested end to end. Other expert constants are not proof of a complete purchasable, persistable specialist roster.

## 2. Behavior

Acceptance refined on 2026-10-16; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Open university purchases → list the agreed eligible experts and their prices; keep New World-only specialties unavailable there.

2. Confirm one affordable purchase → debit once and place the chosen expert on the European pier with stable identity.

3. Cancel, insufficient funds or stale confirmation → no charge and no extra person.

4. Embark, save, cross, unload and assign the expert → preserve specialty and apply its agreed skill; equipping a military role does not erase expertise.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Use the agreed European eligibility and tier prices, retaining the untaxed 2200-gold master blacksmith.

6. European roster: expert farmer, fisherman, fur trapper, silver miner, lumberjack and ore miner; master carpenter, distiller, weaver, tobacconist, fur trader, blacksmith and gunsmith; firebrand preacher, elder statesman, seasoned scout, hardy pioneer, veteran soldier and Jesuit missionary. Schoolhouse/college/university prices are 1000/2000/3000, except the existing blacksmith at 2200. Fixed expert purchases are untaxed. Sugar/cotton/tobacco planters are excluded.

7. A production expert doubles only the matching job, never an unrelated job or zero yield. Farmer applies to land food and fisherman to water food. Building experts retain existing input/output accounting; political and building-tier modifiers remain owned by their requirements. Every specialist retains identity, specialty, equipment and cargo relationship through purchase, transport, assignment and disk reload.

8. The expanded purchase list remains usable at the supported window size, with English/Czech names, prices, accessible keyboard controls and cancellation. A scrolled or off-screen entry must be reachable without obscuring the close action.

9. Full acceptance also requires real non-production skills. Scout sight uses [R-0069](done/R-0069.md); hardy improvement timing follows the agreed work rules. Veteran acquisition must converge with the rank and strength contracts of [R-0093](done/R-0093.md), [R-0094](done/R-0094.md) and [R-0111](done/R-0111.md), preserving civilian specialty. Jesuit mission effects and scout rumor/native-diplomacy effects require their actual rule owners and player paths. A roster-only delivery must remain explicitly incomplete for these integration checks.
