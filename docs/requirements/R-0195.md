## 1. Summary

1. A final result needs a reproducible base score from living people, recruited founders, treasury and national sentiment.
2. This requirement owns those four components; R-0196 owns war penalties/bonuses and final difficulty multiplication.

## 2. Behavior

1. Count each owned living person exactly once across colony, map, cargo and accessible Europe: criminal/servant 1, free colonist/native convert 2, expert 4. Add 5 per distinct recruited father, `floor(gold/1000)`, and the displayed whole national support percentage.
2. No passenger or worker is counted twice; military veteran status and Continental mobilization of the same person do not add another person or discard its civilian scoring identity. Inaccessible Crown-seized home assets after declaration are not rebel population/treasury. For gold 999/1000/1999, the gold component is 0/1/1. A native convert contributes 2.
3. Return a named component breakdown and base subtotal for R-0196. Reopening preview or Save/Continue does not mutate score sources. UC-112 checks mixed civilian roles before/after veteran/Continental change, every location, 0/1/25 founders, gold edges and 0/50/100 support.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model result calculator snapshots real living owned identities, Congress membership, accessible treasury and R-0100 support; use stable unique unit IDs and one shared count across placements. GUI renders the model breakdown.
2. Proposed model/UC-112 checks cover no duplicates, role weights, gold floor, support display and reload under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
3. Current result: capture only; no scoring implementation or runtime acceptance is claimed.
