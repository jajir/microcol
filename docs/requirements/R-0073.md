## 1. Summary

1. The player needs an explicit waiting order that suppresses repeated activation and boards eligible people when a ship departs.

2. Current situation: R-0022 provides explicit individual passenger transfers. Persistent sentry orders and their automatic departure boarding behavior remain separate gaps.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Place an eligible unit on sentry → it stops normal activation until explicitly activated or awakened by the agreed nearby-foreign-unit condition.

2. Depart with a compatible owned ship → board eligible sentries according to available holds and an explicit selection order; retain equipment and identity.

3. People without the order are not silently loaded. Full or incompatible carriers leave passengers ashore with understandable feedback.

4. Cancel activation/boarding or reject a stale departure → no partial transfer. Save/load retains the order and does not board twice.
