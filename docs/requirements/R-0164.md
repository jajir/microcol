# R-0164 — Establish a mission and change local alarm each update

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P19; [NAT-018](../gap-analysis.md#native-nations-settlements-and-relations); [UC-066](../use-cases.md#domorode-narody) (Hra). See [accepted mission effect](../gap-analysis.md#native-and-european-relations).
4. Dependencies: native settlement [R-0150](R-0150.md), relation state [R-0153](R-0153.md), visitor choice [R-0157](R-0157.md), missionary/expertise [R-0163](done/R-0163.md); convert event [R-0149](R-0149.md) consumes the mission clock.
5. Primary area: [A08](areas.md#a08).
6. Related areas: A04 update/save clock; A03 visible owner/expertise; A07 convert destination; A10 Brebeuf/Sepulveda through their own owners.
7. Origin: NAT-018 persistent mission and alarm effect; R-0149 owns fifth-update conversion, not mission establishment.

## 1. Summary

1. A missionary can establish a persistent settlement mission that changes local alarm toward its owner and contacted rivals.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` has no mission association/update path; this is source assessment only.
3. This requirement owns mission owner, expert state and each-update relation effect. Denunciation, incitement and convert worker lifecycle remain separate.

## 2. Behavior

1. An eligible missionary may confirm establishment at a native settlement without an existing mission; the player sees owner, ordinary/effective expert status and current relation. An occupied settlement requires R-0165's contest rather than silent mission replacement. Cancel, occupied settlement or stale eligibility leaves missionary/settlement unchanged.
2. On each mission update, reduce owner-local L by 2 ordinary or 4 effective expert, and add 1 to each contacted rival's local L. Do not directly reduce tribal T; use R-0153's separate propagation/clamp. Brebeuf's expert effect from R-0163 applies to old and new ordinary missionaries, never twice to Jesuits.
3. A mission persists with its missionary owner and update count across Save/Continue; opening the settlement does not tick it. Its fifth nonhostile update supplies one eligible conversion event to R-0149, without itself creating an unowned convert.
4. UC-066 checks ordinary/expert/Brebeuf values, owner versus contacted rival isolation, fifth-update boundary, visible owner/status, cancel and Continue.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model settlement owner stores mission association and update count and emits typed local L deltas through R-0153. Reject ordinary establishment over an existing mission. Query R-0163 effective expertise at each update; expose owner/status to GUI and one nonhostile fifth-update event to R-0149.
2. On selection, validate missionary/settlement identity and persisted association; no model-to-GUI link. R-0165 owns rival replacement and death consequences.
3. Proposed model/UC-066 checks cover establishment/rejection, 2/4/+1, Brebeuf transition, update count, fifth event, loss/replacement and Save/Continue once. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no gameplay change or runtime acceptance is claimed.

## 4. Open questions

1. During hostile turns, does a surviving mission's fifth-update conversion clock pause, reset or continue counting with the event suppressed? The accepted rule says conversion is tested every fifth update of a nonhostile mission but does not define interruption semantics.
