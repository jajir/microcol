# R-0022 — Embark and disembark an individual colonist safely

1. Status: Refining
2. Updated: 2026-10-13
3. Related: P08; CARGO-002/003/007/008; UC-024/UC-025 (Hra). See the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md),
   [use cases](../use-cases.md) and [work packages](../implementation-plan.md#seznam-pracovnich-baliku).
4. Dependencies: Existing cargo model; [R-0004](R-0004.md) coastal access.
5. Primary area: [A06](areas.md#a06).
6. Related areas: A05 legal locations/ownership; A07 colony admission; A03 selection.

## 1. Summary

1. Complete the manual passenger transfer needed to land an expedition and bring a purchased expert home.

2. Existing capability: TC22/TC_03 boards the same colonist from the European pier and returns them through real drag/drop; identity and equipment remain unchanged. Existing TC01 exercises two colonists between a colony, ship and shore. These paths must be preserved; selective landing, invalid/full/foreign targets and precise movement accounting remain separate acceptance gaps.

3. One ordinary or equipped colonist at a time between own ship and legal shore/port/pier. Treasure, artillery, automatic boarding and passenger priority queues remain separate.

## 2. Behavior

Target behavior for later implementation. The audit below separates existing
capability from missing steps; it does not accept these complete targets.

1. An owned colonist can board an eligible owned ship at an allowed location when a cargo space is available; equipment travels with the person and uses the same passenger space.

2. Show legal destinations when disembarking. One selected passenger leaves; all other passengers and goods remain aboard.

3. Full ship, foreign ship, distant shore, inland destination or invalid passenger location rejects without losing the unit or changing another cargo slot.

4. Cancel a destination choice without issuing a model command. Success keeps the selected person discoverable at the destination and accounts for movement according to the agreed transfer rule.

5. Save/load before and after each transfer preserves exactly one location for the colonist and consistent carrier references.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Inspect existing `Cargo`, `CargoSlot`, unit places and dock/map callers; centralize missing preconditions in model transfer operations used by human and AI.

2. Add only the necessary selective-landing controls and shared error handling; use model eligibility and existing coastal-colony rules.

3. Proposed `TC_colonist_transport_test`: pier/ship/shore/colony journey, equipped passenger, full capacity, wrong owner, cancel and round-trip. Model tests assert one location and unchanged state on rejection.

4. Verification: TC22/TC_03 and existing TC01 passed on 2026-10-13: Europe pier round-trip and colony/shore passenger transfer work. Their limited scope does not accept all rejected/full/foreign or selective-landing cases. Final suite commands and publication evidence are maintained in the [functional test record](../functional-tests.md#free-play-audit-2026-10-13).

## 4. Open questions

1. How much movement does boarding/disembarking spend, including a unit with no remaining points? Blocking: yes; reuse the transport Q-11 decision. Proposed answer: document separate ship/passenger costs before implementation. Decision: unresolved.

2. Does landing in an owned colony put the passenger outside or immediately into work? Blocking: yes. Proposed answer: outside for explicit later assignment, keeping transfer separate from employment. Decision: unresolved.
