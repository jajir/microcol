## 1. Summary

1. Complete the manual passenger transfer needed to land an expedition and bring a purchased expert home.

2. The transport catalogue separates passenger capacity, selective disembarkation and movement accounting from existing goods transfers; Z01 covers goods, not this passenger contract.

3. One ordinary or equipped colonist at a time between own ship and legal shore/port/pier. Treasure, artillery, automatic boarding and passenger priority queues remain separate.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for individual refinement; capture does not approve implementation.

1. An owned colonist can board an eligible owned ship at an allowed location when a cargo space is available; equipment travels with the person and uses the same passenger space.

2. Show legal destinations when disembarking. One selected passenger leaves; all other passengers and goods remain aboard.

3. Full ship, foreign ship, distant shore, inland destination or invalid passenger location rejects without losing the unit or changing another cargo slot.

4. Cancel a destination choice without issuing a model command. Success keeps the selected person discoverable at the destination and accounts for movement according to the agreed transfer rule.

5. Save/load before and after each transfer preserves exactly one location for the colonist and consistent carrier references.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Inspect existing `Cargo`, `CargoSlot`, unit places and dock/map callers; centralize missing preconditions in model transfer operations used by human and AI.

2. Add only the necessary selective-landing controls and shared error handling; use model eligibility and existing coastal-colony rules.

3. Proposed `TC_colonist_transport_test`: pier/ship/shore/colony journey, equipped passenger, full capacity, wrong owner, cancel and round-trip. Model tests assert one location and unchanged state on rejection.
