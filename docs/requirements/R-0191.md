# R-0191 — Recognize a complete revolution victory

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-18
3. Related: P24; [GOV-019](../gap-analysis.md#government-taxation-and-independence); [UC-085](../use-cases.md#politika-a-valka-za-nezavislost) (Hra); [accepted victory set](../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention).
4. Dependencies: rebellion state [R-0105](R-0105.md), real REF roster/destruction [R-0185](R-0185.md)/[R-0186](R-0186.md), colony capture [R-0096](R-0096.md). Pair this evaluator with prior loss evaluation [R-0192](R-0192.md) for complete terminal acceptance.
5. Primary area: [A10](areas.md#a10).
6. Related areas: A09 combat/capture; A07 current colony/resident ownership; A04 terminal result once.
7. Origin: free-play victory residual; legacy campaign military-score flag does not prove all colonies free and all surviving REF locations counted.

## 1. Summary

1. Rebels win only after reclaiming every surviving rebellion colony and reducing all surviving royal ground forces to the accepted limit.
2. This requirement owns victory evaluation; R-0192 owns loss and must run first.

## 2. Behavior

1. Maintain the rebellion-colony set: colonies owned at declaration plus later founded/acquired by the rebel, with membership tracking capture and removal on destruction. Victory requires **every surviving member rebel-controlled** and **at most two REF ground units alive total**, including reserve in Europe, carried at sea and landed. REF ships alone do not prevent victory.
2. Evaluate after each completed gameplay action/update only after declaration and after R-0192 loss. Three surviving ground units or one Crown-held member prevents victory; two and all recovered win once. Outside unrelated colonies do not silently enter the set.
3. On victory, persist one terminal result and let scoring R-0195/0196 use completion order; Save/Continue cannot announce or score the same victory twice. UC-085 checks 3→2, hidden reserve/ship cargo, occupied/recovered colonies, same-action loss precedence and reload.

4. Accepted on 2026-10-18: defeated Continental and royal infantry/cavalry are destroyed together with their equipment. They do not downgrade, survive as colonists, or leave recoverable equipment. See the [shared defeat rule](../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention).

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model maintains stable rebellion-set identities and asks R-0185 for total living REF ground identities across all places; it does not infer survival from visible map units or military score. Invoke evaluator after committed state transitions, with R-0192 first.
2. Proposed model/UC-085 checks cover membership changes, every REF location, exact two-unit boundary, no premature win and one terminal event under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
3. Current result: capture only; no victory implementation or runtime acceptance is claimed.
