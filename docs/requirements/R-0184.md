## 1. Summary

1. La Salle automatically gives an eligible colony a stockade at three residents, including colonies that qualified before he joined.
2. This requirement owns only the father-triggered grant, not normal construction or the fortified-population restriction.

## 2. Behavior

1. At joining, each already owned colony with at least three internal residents and no stockade-or-better gains one stockade at no hammer/tool cost. Afterwards, an owned colony reaching three residents gains it once, whether founded before or after joining.
2. A fort or fortress is never replaced by a lesser stockade. Population 2 gives no grant; returning to three after a grant does not create a second building. On ownership transfer, an already eligible colony with no defense receives a stockade if its **new** owner has La Salle; existing completed defense stays intact under the shared rule.
3. Canceling founder choice changes no buildings. Save/Continue preserves grants and the actual fortified population restriction. UC-108 covers existing/future colonies, 2→3, higher defense, ownership change and reload.
