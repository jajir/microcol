## 1. Summary

1. After independence, the Crown must ship its recorded force and make legal landings and attacks with real unit families.
2. R-0185 accounts for the prewar reserve; this requirement consumes it through real ships and units instead of spawning generic colonists.

## 2. Behavior

1. Following declaration, men-of-war transport available royal infantry, cavalry and artillery within actual passenger/hold capacity from Europe into the map. Units retain stable REF identity as reserve, in transit, landed, captured or destroyed; only actual surviving ground units count toward R-0191 victory.
2. A ship follows legal sea movement and coastal access. A landing uses an available legal square, never displaces unrelated units or bypasses colony defenders. Subsequent Crown movement/attack uses R-0178 strengths and shared combat/capture, and can threaten a colony over bounded turns without an infinite no-action loop.
3. A blocked or lost ship/landing rechecks current state; no reserve unit is duplicated or silently deleted. Save/Continue mid-voyage/landing preserves passengers and action state. UC-082 checks all four families, ship capacity, legal coast, blocked landing, actual battle, reserve survival and reload.

4. Accepted on 2026-10-18: defeated Continental and royal infantry/cavalry are destroyed together with their equipment. They do not downgrade, survive as colonists, or leave recoverable equipment. See the [shared defeat rule](../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention).

5. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0186-refinement)): On each royal turn fill all available transport-capable men-of-war from actual reserves, cycling infantry, cavalry, artillery within actual hold capacity. Target the nearest known rebel coastal colony with a legal landing, ties by stable ID and then coordinates. Recompute a blocked route at most once per turn; after three unsuccessful turns try another target, otherwise wait safely. A prepared clear-route scenario must demonstrate landing and attack within ten royal turns, without teleporting or duplicating reserves.
