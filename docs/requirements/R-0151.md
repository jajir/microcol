## 1. Summary

1. Native braves need persistent nation and home-settlement associations and real turns before the European nations.
2. The 2026-10-17 source catalogue at `8f3ce118` reports no native actor or brave factory. R-0052 delivered the common order contract for actors that exist; it did not create natives.
3. This requirement creates actor/turn participation and home-linked braves. Attacking, patrol choices and mission/trade actions remain their separate requirements.

## 2. Behavior

1. Each supported native brave belongs to one native nation and home settlement; map selection can identify that relationship where legitimately known. Losing a home does not silently transfer the brave to another nation or European owner.
2. Present native actors take one prepared turn before England, France, Spain and Netherlands, independently of serialized order; absent actors take no dummy turn. Their actions use the delivered national turn boundary: any applicable preparation completes once, then unit decisions/actions, and the calendar advances only after the whole round.
3. A native turn with no legal action ends safely. Save before/after a native turn and Continue do not repeat preparation, duplicate braves or skip a European turn. Native braves obey shared legal movement/combat operations; this requirement does not invent hostile AI.

4. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0156](../gap-analysis.md#r-0156-refinement), [R-0174](../gap-analysis.md#r-0174-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.

1. Model/turn owner registers a native participant and persisted brave/home links through R-0052's ordering and readiness contract. Native AI receives its own turn callback but its later strategy [R-0156](R-0156.md) uses shared model commands; no model-to-GUI/AI-to-GUI dependency.
2. Keep European actor and Crown order unchanged. On selection, check native unit role/strength prerequisites with A05/A09 owners before readiness; no raid or conquest policy belongs here.
3. Proposed model and UC-005/060 checks cover all eight home associations, shuffled storage, no-native case, native-first order, empty turn, calendar once and Save/Continue. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no gameplay change or runtime acceptance is claimed.
5. Align the existing checks and shared model/AI/UI integration with the linked 2026-10-20 decisions; retain unresolved questions only with their rule owners and run UI regression in CI under the current user instruction.
