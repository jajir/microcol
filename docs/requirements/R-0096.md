## 1. Summary

1. The player needs conquest to resolve every defender and transfer the entire colony coherently.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): R-0026 supports armed capture after existing military defenses and safe ship escape. It does not establish the complete civilian-defense and outside-field capture rules.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Defeat military defenders first; a colony with only civilians still follows the agreed civilian-defense rule rather than becoming automatically undefended.

2. On accepted successful capture, transfer the colony and every affected worker, including workers in all eight fields, exactly once.

3. Reuse the accepted attacking-unit placement and safe escape policy for ships; no ship, cargo or unrelated peaceful occupant is silently deleted.

4. If no valid atomic outcome exists, reject before changing owners. Save/load retains the captured colony identity, workers and diplomatic state.

5. Shared behavior accepted on 2026-10-15 ([Q-08](../gap-analysis.md#q-08-combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Use strongest eligible defender then stable identity, civilian defense and the retained colony-capture/worker-placement decisions. These decisions unblock policy; individual design, dependencies and acceptance checks still require refinement.
