## 1. Summary

1. A missionary challenging a foreign mission risks death; native attitudes and expertise determine which missionary survives.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` reports no contest handler; no runtime result is claimed.
3. This requirement owns the confirmed contest and its two mutually exclusive outcomes, not generic attack or mission establishment.

## 2. Behavior

1. Before confirmation, show the risk and identified rival mission. Contest strength for each side is 1 ordinary or 2 effective expert, multiplied by `1 + (100 − hostility toward that missionary's nation)/100`. Challenger success is `own/(own+resident)`, clamped to 10–90%.
2. On success the resident missionary dies and the visiting missionary replaces the mission; on failure the visitor dies and the resident/mission remain. One confirmed contest makes one draw and one state transition; Save/Continue cannot replay it.
3. Cancel, absent rival mission, stale visitor or invalid role leaves both missionaries, mission and random state unchanged. Brebeuf expertise from R-0163 applies before calculating either side and does not double an already expert missionary.
4. UC-067 controls both outcomes, equal/unequal hostility and expertise, risk preview, cancellation and Continue.

5. Consume the accepted 2026-10-20 rules from [R-0174](../gap-analysis.md#r-0174-refinement); preserve this requirement’s own outcome and existing delivered behavior. These decisions do not establish this consumer’s implementation or acceptance.

3. Proposed model/UC-067 checks cover formula and 10/90 caps, success/failure survival, Brebeuf, cancel/stale atomicity, one draw and Continue. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
