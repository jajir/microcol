## 1. Summary

1. The player needs an independence decision governed by actual national support, with visible and persistent consequences.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The project has legacy rebellion/Crown paths, but the catalogue identifies missing support-based eligibility and complete mobilization. This is not a claim that rebellion code is wholly absent.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This selected change has been refined for the authorized batch; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Accepted behavior for the selected implementation.

1. Before spring 1800, national support below 50% denies declaration; support at least 50% and at least one owned coastal colony permits confirmation. Do not retain invented 1700 or three-colony gates. Coordinate the bundled `thrive` scenario with [R-0051](done/R-0051.md): its new start is 1490, so declaration readiness must come from explicit eligible support, not a later starting date.

2. Before confirmation, show the consequences. Cancel leaves the political state and units unchanged.

3. Confirm once → enter revolution and mobilize eligible colonies according to their local support and the agreed rule.

4. Save/load preserves the new political state and mobilized units without repeating them. Europe closure, REF/intervention and final victory remain explicit separate UC-081–085 work.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [support, production and congress](../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress), [revolution and intervention](../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention)): Declare at at least 50% exact national support before 1800 and muster eligible local veterans once by the agreed fraction; preserve separate full-revolution acceptance.

6. Accepted capture rule (user decision, 2026-10-17): Independence eligibility and local mobilization use support under the [shared affiliation rule](../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress). A captured colony contributes to its new European colonial nation with that nation's Bolivar bonus; Crown occupation retains the last colonial affiliation.

7. Accepted on 2026-10-18: defeated Continental and royal infantry/cavalry are destroyed together with their equipment. They do not downgrade, survive as colonists, or leave recoverable equipment. See the [shared defeat rule](../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention).

## 3. Implementation steps

1. `Player` owns eligibility and once-only declaration/muster, using R-0100 exact national/local support and current colonial affiliation. Reject wrong actor, paused preparation, already declared, date or coastal/support failure before mutation. Revalidate after existing veto callbacks before committing. Precompute all conversions before changing state; notify listeners only after the state is coherent.
2. For each owned colony with local support at least 50%, count its owner's armed veteran soldiers/dragoons on the colony tile, excluding field/building/cargo units and other owners. Convert the first `floor(V * exact support)` by stable ID, preserving unit identity, equipment and people. No national rounded percentage may substitute for exact local fractions.
3. Reuse shared model combat/persistence ownership for usable Continental roles (infantry 4, cavalry 5) and the prerequisite royal role bases (5/6) and accepted destruction outcome from R-0178. Keep civilian expertise and ordinary veteran progression intact. Include role identity in stale combat checks, names and saved state. R-0178's situational bombardment/ambush, full REF roster/deployment and victory/intervention remain separately open; do not claim their acceptance.
4. The existing declare button opens a cancelable localized consequence confirmation before model mutation. Explain mobilization and home-Europe closure; preserve existing mission veto and Crown listeners. The UI handles expected rejection if support/ownership/state changes before confirmation. Keep button availability refreshed when support or ownership changes. Existing home-Europe behavior is preserved; complete UC-081 closure work remains separate.
5. Extend TC03 with eligible support and retain its Crown landing assertion. Add TC81/F73 for below/exact-threshold eligibility, pre-1800 boundary, cancel/Escape, confirmed unequal-colony muster, preserved equipment/counts and Continue. Model tests cover exact fractions, 49/50%, date, one coastal colony, no legacy 1700/three-colony gate, per-colony 60/90/100% stable counts, wrong actor, stale/repeated/veto declaration, captured affiliation, persistence and actual Continental/royal defeat without ordinary downgrade. Main formats and runs targeted Docker checks before the batch boundary.
6. Update current-schema scenarios only as needed to express intended eligible support, preserving unrelated behavior. Record implementation and actual verification in the evidence pages; keep this status until required batch acceptance. Use the shared formatter and functional test contract. This is the twentieth selected task, not authorization to complete the independent REF/intervention requirements.
