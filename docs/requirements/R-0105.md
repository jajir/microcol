## 1. Summary

1. The player needs an independence decision governed by actual national support, with visible and persistent consequences.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The project has legacy rebellion/Crown paths, but the catalogue identifies missing support-based eligibility and complete mobilization. This is not a claim that rebellion code is wholly absent.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Before 1800, national support below 50% denies declaration; support at least 50% permits the agreed confirmation. Do not retain invented 1700 or three-colony gates.

2. Before confirmation, show the consequences. Cancel leaves the political state and units unchanged.

3. Confirm once → enter revolution and mobilize eligible colonies according to their local support and the agreed rule.

4. Save/load preserves the new political state and mobilized units without repeating them. Europe closure, REF/intervention and final victory remain explicit separate UC-081–085 work.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [support, production and congress](../gap-analysis.md#support-production-and-congress), [revolution and intervention](../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention)): Declare at at least 50% exact national support before 1800 and muster eligible local veterans once by the agreed fraction; preserve separate full-revolution acceptance.
