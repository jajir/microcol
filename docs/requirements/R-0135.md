# R-0135 — Let immigrants opt out and prioritize dock boarding

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P08/P14; CARGO-006 residual; UC-025 (Hra). See the [boarding rule](../gap-analysis.md#transport-ships-and-atlantic-travel), [accepted sentry selection](../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport) and [boarding use case](../use-cases.md#lode-naklad-a-obchodni-trasy).
4. Dependencies: [R-0073](done/R-0073.md) owns persisted sentry boarding and stable-ID choice; immigrant arrivals from [R-0075](done/R-0075.md)/[R-0076](done/R-0076.md).
5. Primary area: [A06](areas.md#a06).
6. Related areas: A03 dock controls/priority feedback; A04 saved preference; A05 persistent order identity.
7. Origin: CARGO-006 immigrant-default and queue-priority residual; no reimplementation of R-0073 sentry departure.

## 1. Summary

1. A newly arrived immigrant should be willing to board an outgoing ship, while the player can opt that person out or bring them forward in the dock queue.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `ffae3396` credits R-0073's actual stable-ID sentry boarding. It does not credit immigrant defaults or player priority controls.
3. Scope: saved immigrant boarding preference and player queue control, feeding the existing departure transfer. Ordinary sentry eligibility, capacity, costs and identity remain R-0073/R-0022.

## 2. Behavior

1. A new immigrant reaching the European dock starts willing to board. The player can turn that willingness off without moving or deleting the person, and can bring a willing immigrant forward among dock candidates; the current visible order reflects the choice.
2. On a valid departure, only willing eligible people board up to actual free holds. Equipment/identity and [R-0022](done/R-0022.md) movement costs are preserved; excess people remain at the dock. Existing R-0073 sentries still board by stable ID within their unchanged selection rule until the shared priority precedence is explicitly settled.
3. Canceling a preference change or an obsolete/wrong-owner choice changes neither queue nor cargo. Save/Continue retains each immigrant's opt-out and ordering state and cannot board a person again merely by loading.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Store default willingness and explicit priority with the model dock/passenger state. Reuse R-0073's one departure boarding operation, with UI offering only current eligible people. Avoid a parallel cargo mutation path.
2. Not prepared for development. Resolve how priority interacts with already accepted stable-ID sentry selection before changing a departure with both groups; inspect arrival and dock list owners after selection.
3. Proposed checks: paid and earned immigrant defaults, opt-out/restore, prioritized immigrants with 0/1/multiple free holds, mixed sentry group after precedence decision, cancel/stale, save/Continue and named UC-025 player paths under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: captured only; no gameplay change or runtime test is claimed.

## 4. Open questions

1. With fewer holds than willing immigrants plus existing sentries, does a player-prioritized immigrant precede all sentries, or does R-0073's sentry stable-ID group board first? The accepted stable-ID rule does not specify cross-group priority.
