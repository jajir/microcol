## 1. Summary

1. A player needs visible small/large rivers that persist and give the documented yield and connected-travel benefit.
2. The 2026-10-17 source assessment at `f902be8a` reports no river course/size state or corresponding production and movement branch. This is historical source inspection, not a player test.
3. This requirement owns river segments, size, persistence and their accepted effects. It reuses R-0125 movement and R-0124 output; it does not own road construction, map generation policy or all production modifiers.

## 2. Behavior

1. A river tile shows and retains whether the river is small or large and which adjacent segments form its course. Inspecting or loading it does not change that state. A route only receives river travel benefit when its traversed segment is connected along the course.
2. A small river adds 1 and a large river adds 2 to an **already positive** tile output before job and percentage modifiers. A zero output remains zero. The colony's preview and next production update use the same river value; a river is not counted twice at a crossing or after Continue.
3. Moving along a connected river segment in either direction costs exactly one-third move under the [accepted rule](../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport). Execution, path and highlighted range agree through [R-0125](done/R-0125.md). An adjacent tile without a connected course receives its ordinary terrain cost, and an invalid/stale move preserves location and points.
4. Save and Continue preserve river size, connections and movement/production effects, without replaying a move or producing goods on load. Whole UC-008 also includes special resources and remains separately open.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model/map own river size and segment connectivity, with current-schema persistence. Reuse the model's shared cost from R-0125 and output order from R-0124; UI draws and previews model state instead of computing bonuses independently.
2. Not prepared for development. On selection, inspect map topology and authoring, path/range and field-output callers; choose one stored segment representation that makes reciprocal connections testable. Treat map-generation placement as a separate P04 scope decision if not already specified.
3. Proposed checks: small/large +1/+2, zero-yield guard, connected and disconnected adjacent segments, both travel directions at one-third, route/range agreement, crossing once, save/Continue and a named UC-008/012 player path. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: captured only; no gameplay change or runtime test is claimed.

5. Generator dependency: The source does not yet specify how generated or authored maps choose river courses and sizes. Resolve placement through [R-0065](R-0065.md) before generated-map acceptance; fixed-map river effects can be refined independently.
