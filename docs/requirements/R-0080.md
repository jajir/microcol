## 1. Summary

1. The player needs coastal facilities to unlock fishing and ship construction at the correct levels.

2. Current situation: Building eligibility exists, but water-field work and ship production have separate effect gates. The catalogue flags missing dock checks and ship construction tied to the wrong upgrade tier.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Without a dock, a water fishing job is unavailable; a dock or its higher upgrades enables eligible ocean/lake fishing.

2. A dock or drydock alone does not enable the manual’s shipyard-only ship projects. A completed shipyard retains lower-tier capabilities and enables the supported legal ship projects.

3. Cancel or fail a job/project change → keep workers, queue and stocks unchanged. Explain the missing facility in the existing control.

4. Save/load preserves the upgraded facility and its capabilities; do not rely only on exact equality with the removed predecessor type.
