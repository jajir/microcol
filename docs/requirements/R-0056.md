## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to choose useful work using the selected worker’s actual opportunities, without discovering hidden moves after confirming.

2. Current situation: Existing colony controls move workers and choose crops, but do not provide the proposed ordinary-worker job menu with current/best available field yields.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Open a worker’s work choice → show supported jobs and the applicable yield for that worker, including the current field and the best available eligible field where relevant.

2. Choose an outdoor job → use an available field that satisfies the agreed best-yield rule. Equal best yields may select any tied field; never occupy another worker’s square.

3. Change crop for a worker already on a field → keep that field unless the player explicitly requests reassignment.

4. Cancel or confirm after the job/field becomes invalid → leave the original assignment and stocks unchanged and explain rejection.

5. Job previews and assignment reuse the [shared-square rules](../gap-analysis.md#shared-colony-working-squares), including global occupation and rejection of contradictory saved jobs. The resolved persistence policy is not a new open question.
