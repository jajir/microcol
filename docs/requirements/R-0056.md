## 1. Summary

1. The player needs to choose useful work using the selected worker’s actual opportunities, without discovering hidden moves after confirming.

2. Current situation: Existing colony controls move workers and choose crops, but do not provide the proposed ordinary-worker job menu with current/best available field yields.

3. Scope: the ordinary-worker choice and previews below, including the supported master blacksmith. Preparation follows the user’s implementation request; the wider specialist roster stays in R-0074. R-0055 acceptance remains the prerequisite for implementation.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed behavior on 2026-10-16; the authoritative [job-choice rules](../gap-analysis.md#colony-job-choices-and-yield-previews) resolve the supported-job and yield-label questions. Technical refinement and prerequisite acceptance remain in progress.

1. Open a worker’s work choice from an occupied field, indoor job or colonist at the colony center → show supported outdoor and producing indoor jobs with localized names. A field worker sees current-field and best eligible yields; an indoor or center worker sees the best eligible outdoor yield. Mark the master blacksmith’s tools specialty. Unavailable jobs show a reason, not a fictional output.

2. Choose outdoor work from an indoor job or the colony center → use the best eligible field for that worker and commodity. Exclude occupied/shared/invalid fields. Equal best yields use the existing stable field order; never occupy another worker’s square.

3. Change crop for a worker already on a field → keep that field. Provide an explicit move-to-best action showing destination and yield for deliberate reassignment. The worker’s own field is eligible when comparing yields; choosing it again does not duplicate the worker.

4. Opening, browsing or canceling changes nothing. If the worker’s original job, chosen field/slot, ownership or turn becomes invalid before confirmation, leave assignments, equipment and stocks unchanged, explain rejection and refresh choices. Do not silently redirect a stale confirmed choice to another destination.

5. Job previews and assignment reuse the [shared-square rules](../gap-analysis.md#shared-colony-working-squares), including global occupation and rejection of contradictory saved jobs. The resolved persistence policy is not a new open question.

6. Indoor choices show the worker’s potential per-turn output, the needed input and current stock, with a shortage warning. They do not present that potential as guaranteed warehouse growth or include the building’s automatic base production. Example: 3 tools potential, 3 ore required, 1 ore currently available must clearly show the shortage. Expert previews use the same multiplier as actual production.

7. Preserve manual job dragging and ordinary crop changes. Offer supported jobs only in existing producing buildings, keep their slot limits and preserve last-resident moves within the same colony. Non-producing buildings and teaching are separate features. Invalid equipment returns must fail before an old job or warehouse changes.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Source review on 2026-10-16 found local crop previews in `PanelColonyFields` and slot dragging in `AbstractPanelConstructionWithSlots`. `ColonyField` provides shared-square admission; `ConstructionSlot`/`Construction` own indoor output, inputs and expert modifiers. Keep previews and assignment validation in `model`, with UI presentation in `gui.screen.colony`, following the [package map](../architecture.md). A preview must not move a unit temporarily or mutate/save/reload the live model to estimate output.

2. Refine a small read-only job preview and confirmation contract that retains the original worker/place and proposed destination. Share outdoor yield computation with `ColonyField.getProduction` and indoor potential/input computation with `ConstructionSlot`; do not add independent UI formulas. Extend the existing indoor placement boundary where needed so the menu and drag path enforce the same owner, identity, turn, source, capacity and equipment rules before mutation. Current-schema persistence should need no new stored menu state.

3. Acceptance must compare one worker across two free fields with different yields and a better occupied/shared field; test current-field crop preservation, explicit relocation, ties, no eligible field, indoor-to-outdoor choice, expert marking, indoor shortage labels, cancellation and stale worker/destination rejection. Compare previews with actual output for isolated workers with sufficient inputs and with known shortages, separating worker output from base production and consumption. Extend model tests and ordinary colony UI journeys under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md); allocate stable scenario IDs during the final design review.

4. Current result: preparation only; no gameplay implementation or fresh R-0056 acceptance is claimed. The behavioral questions are resolved in the shared topic, but the exact implementation handoff and acceptance fixture still need refinement after R-0055’s remaining boundary matrix is accepted. Preserve existing source/migration evidence and do not treat R-0055’s tests as R-0056 verification.
