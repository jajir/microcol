# R-0007 — Account for food and population growth at the threshold

1. Status: Draft
2. Updated: 2026-10-13
3. Related: P10/P03; COL-005/006/009;
   [UC-034, Hra](../use-cases.md#kolonie-obyvatele-a-vyroba), consumption, birth and imports.
4. Dependencies: agreed update timing in [R-0002](R-0002.md), Q-10 food decisions;
   famine is handled by [R-0008](R-0008.md).

## 1. Summary

Make consumption, stored food and new-colonist creation agree at the food
threshold, including import and large-surplus cases. Retain working behavior
rather than implementing population growth from scratch.

Static source inspection on 2026-10-12 at
[`4b3a1b56`](https://github.com/jajir/microcol/commit/4b3a1b567bd9e8c8ad8af1587d9849ecee02bead);
no new gameplay test was run while preparing this draft.
[Colony](../../microcol-game/src/main/java/org/microcol/model/Colony.java)
already charges internal workers, creates one colonist at projected food of at
least 200, subtracts 200 and places the newborn outside the colony. It performs
one birth per invocation.
[ColonyWarehouse](../../microcol-game/src/main/java/org/microcol/model/ColonyWarehouse.java)
reports food capacity 200; that constant alone does not settle import/birth timing.

Scope is food and growth. Famine, changing all terrain yields, immigration from
Europe and an overhaul of colony-center production are separate changes.

## 2. Behavior

Use the existing colony view and turn report to show food, consumption and a
birth. Growth is automatic; it has no invented confirmation or cancellation.
Opening/closing the colony view itself never processes a turn.

Acceptance examples:

1. Three internal ordinary workers and two units outside → internal consumption
   is 6 food per colony update; the outside units add no colony food charge.
2. After production and consumption, 199 food → no birth and 199 retained.
3. After production and consumption, 200 food → one free colonist and 0 retained;
   201 food → one free colonist and 1 retained.
4. The newborn has the correct owner and outside-colony location, one identity
   and one birth report, and can use the existing selection/movement path.
5. Save before an update, reload and complete it → the same food and birth result;
   reload after it → no extra birth or charge.
6. Imported food crossing the threshold and surplus sufficient for several births
   follow the Q-10 decision before acceptance. Never solve the boundary by
   clipping food to 199 and silently discarding the birth-triggering quantity.

Normal nonnegative stock and rejected-transfer guarantees still apply. The chosen
birth policy must work in ordinary free play, not only a test fixture.
