## 1. Summary

Make a food shortfall kill exactly the intended resident and leave a valid world,
including when the last resident dies. Distinguish a zero reserve from starvation.

Static source inspection on 2026-10-12 at
[`4b3a1b56`](https://github.com/jajir/microcol/commit/4b3a1b567bd9e8c8ad8af1587d9849ecee02bead);
no new gameplay test was run while preparing this draft.
[Colony.eatFoodAndOptionallyMakeNewColonist](../../microcol-game/src/main/java/org/microcol/model/Colony.java)
already kills one random internal worker only when projected food is negative,
then clears food. Empty-colony handling goes through
`verifyNumberOfUnitsOptionallyDestroyColony`; the remaining non-food production
is recomputed after a death. These are observations, not decisions on the
unresolved victim-selection or production-order rules.

Population growth and production multipliers are outside this slice.

## 2. Behavior

Stored food covers a deficit first. Exactly zero food after accounting is safe;
an uncovered shortfall causes one death per update and a readable report.
The automatic event has no cancel action, and important feedback remains clear
without sound.

Acceptance examples for an agreed fixture with net food change −4:

1. Stored 5 → 1 remains, no death; stored 4 → 0 remains, no death.
2. Stored 3 → one resident dies, food becomes 0, one famine result is reported.
   No additional resident dies from that same update.
3. After a death → the dead unit is absent from its job and owner roster; all
   surviving assignments and colony identity remain consistent.
4. Next turn and save/continue → no replayed death or stale selected unit.
5. Final resident dies → the agreed Q-10 outcome occurs once, with correct map,
   selection, ownership, reports and remaining turn iteration.

Forecast messages must reflect the same model calculation as the outcome.
The final-resident expectation must be decided before accepting the requirement;
seeing a map image alone is insufficient proof of colony removal.
