1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-15
3. Related: P03; TURN-001/002; UC-005 (Hra). See the [rules](../gap-analysis.md), [use cases](../use-cases.md) and [work packages](../implementation-plan.md#seznam-pracovnich-baliku).
4. Dependencies: Existing participant identities, owner-turn boundary and save/load; order only currently available phases, without requiring new native or market systems.
5. Primary area: [A04](areas.md#a04).
6. Related areas: A06 Europe events/arrivals; A07 colony updates; A10 pending political decisions.
7. Origin: [R-0002](canceled/R-0002.md). Unfinished original outcome under the [migration](migration-2026-10-14.md).

## 1. Summary

1. The player needs Europe events, colony processing and unit actions to occur once per national turn in a predictable order.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): The calendar advances after the final stored player. Current turns process units, colonies and scheduled immigrants; the participant list does not enforce the manual nationality order.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. For the participants present, process natives, England, France, Spain and Netherlands in the manual order. Absent participants do not require dummy actors; Crown phases must be explicitly placed during refinement.

2. Within a national turn, process the available Europe events, then colonies, then allow movement/combat and management. Adding a future event must not process earlier phases again.

3. Save before and after a phase boundary and continue → production, arrivals, births and movement replenishment occur exactly once.

4. One complete round advances the shared calendar once; changing serialized participant order must not change gameplay order.

5. Shared behavior accepted on 2026-10-15 ([Q-02](../gap-analysis.md#q-02-calendar-and-starting-expeditions), [Q-03](../gap-analysis.md#q-03-support-production-and-congress), [Q-10](../gap-analysis.md#q-10-economy-production-and-education)): Apply the agreed calendar and per-system update boundaries once; the shared phase integration remains this requirement's design work. These decisions unblock policy; individual design, dependencies and acceptance checks still require refinement.
