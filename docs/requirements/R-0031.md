2. Updated: 2026-10-13
3. Related: P22; GOV-004, Q-03; UC-078 (Hra), sentiment-state slice. See the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md),

6. Related areas: A07 population/production; A03 support display; A04 persisted political state.

## 1. Summary

1. Create the missing political state needed to explain colonial support and later implement tax protest and independence eligibility.

2. The catalogue records bell production/storage but no sentiment calculation or population-weighted national aggregate in colony/player state.

3. Local and national sentiment, updates and display. Production bonuses/Tory penalties, founders, Congress and declaration/REF remain separate; this does not complete UC-078 or UC-118.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for individual refinement; capture does not approve implementation.

1. Each colony shows its population, political progress and local support; the national view uses the same model aggregate. Label local and national percentages distinctly.

2. Apply bell progress once at the agreed phase. Growth, famine and transfers update denominators without creating or duplicating political progress.

3. Use an explicit Q-03 formula with worked examples. Two colonies of unequal population must not be combined by an unweighted average of their displayed percentages.

4. Handle no colonies, zero population and rounding boundaries explicitly. UI never displays an invalid percentage or enables a political command from a rounded label alone.

5. Save/load preserves the necessary progress and yields identical support; reopening a report adds no bells. Human and AI colonies use the same calculation.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Define the minimal model state and pure calculation for colony/national support; inspect current BELL storage before choosing accumulation semantics.

2. Integrate updates with R-0002 and population operations, and expose read-only values in existing colony/national information. Keep formulas out of UI.

3. Proposed `TC_rebel_sentiment_test`: produce bells in ordinary play, change population, inspect local/national support and reload. Model tests cover unequal colonies, empty population and exact/rounded thresholds using accepted numeric examples.
