# R-0128 — Deplete finite mineral deposits through mining

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P04/P11; MAP-006; UC-009 (Hra). See the [map rule](../gap-analysis.md#map-terrain-and-exploration), [accepted reserve policy](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education) and [mining use case](../use-cases.md#mapa-a-pruzkum).
4. Dependencies: explicit special-resource identity and output from [R-0127](done/R-0127.md); the existing colony production update owns when mining output is credited.
5. Primary area: [A05](areas.md#a05).
6. Related areas: A07 colony field production; A04 saved reserve and turn continuity; A03 remaining-deposit feedback.
7. Origin: MAP-006 gap in the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md#map-terrain-and-exploration).

## 1. Summary

1. A player working silver, ore or mixed mineral land needs a finite deposit that can run out without changing the underlying terrain.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue source assessment at `f902be8a` reports no saved reserve or harvesting transition. It is not a runtime acceptance result.
3. This requirement owns depletion of an already represented deposit. [R-0127](done/R-0127.md) owns overlay identity and its initial yield; this requirement does not reimplement every resource bonus or ordinary field production.

## 2. Behavior

1. An explicitly identified silver deposit begins with 100 bonus units; an ore or mixed mineral deposit begins with 200, under the [accepted provisional policy](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education). Each actual mining update can extract no more bonus output than remains, and consumes only the bonus actually produced.
2. When the reserve reaches zero, its mineral bonus ends, but the underlying terrain and any ordinary baseline output remain. A non-mining worker or an update with no mineral bonus output does not consume reserve. A last extraction smaller than the usual bonus stops at the remaining amount rather than producing a full bonus and going negative.
3. The field preview and actual production agree about an exhausted deposit. Invalid or stale production state cannot spend reserve without producing the corresponding mineral output. Inspecting the tile, changing jobs, saving or loading does not replenish it.
4. Save and Continue preserve the exact remaining reserve across multiple updates, including zero, without repeating an extraction. Whole UC-009 requires silver, ore and mixed examples; the earlier UC-008 overlay check alone does not establish depletion.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Keep remaining reserve in the model/map state beside R-0127 resource identity. Decrement it at the colony's actual mineral production boundary, under the existing update order, and save the count in the current schema. Preview reads the same available bonus without mutating it.
2. Not prepared for development. On selection, inspect the production update's field aggregation and determine the exact ordering for simultaneous ore/silver extraction from a mixed deposit before readiness.
3. Proposed checks: silver 100 and ore/mixed 200 boundaries; ordinary, zero-output and last-partial extractions; unchanged non-mining reserve; baseline after exhaustion; preview/real update agreement; save/Continue at positive and zero reserve. Add a named UC-009 player path at refinement under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: captured only; no gameplay change or runtime test is claimed.

## 4. Open questions

1. When a mixed deposit offers ore and silver during one update, does its 200-unit reserve cover both bonuses together, and in what order are simultaneous bonus outputs capped? The accepted reserve quantity does not specify that allocation.
