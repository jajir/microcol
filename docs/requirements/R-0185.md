# R-0185 — Grow and disclose the prewar royal expedition roster

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-18
3. Related: P24; roster part of [GOV-014](../gap-analysis.md#government-taxation-and-independence); [UC-082](../use-cases.md#politika-a-valka-za-nezavislost) (Hra); [accepted REF growth](../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention).
4. Dependencies: bell production [R-0100](done/R-0100.md), difficulty/profile [R-0064](done/R-0064.md), shared royal roles [R-0178](R-0178.md), Congress membership/report shell [R-0104](done/R-0104.md).
5. Primary area: [A10](areas.md#a10).
6. Related areas: A04 roster/remainder persistence; A03 Congress REF counts; A09 role strengths.
7. Origin: GOV-014 pre-declaration roster residual; current campaign invasion and ordinary colonist substitutes do not establish a persistent free-play REF.

## 1. Summary

1. Players should see a real prewar REF whose families and growth reflect Crown bell pressure before independence.
2. This requirement owns roster accounting and disclosure, not shipping or battlefield deployment; R-0186 owns those later actions.

## 2. Behavior

1. Each Crown begins with `4+d` royal regular infantry, `2+d` royal cavalry, two artillery and two men-of-war, using its nation's difficulty index. Before declaration, each accumulated `ceil(500/k)` bells produced by the colonial nation adds one ground unit cycling infantry, cavalry, artillery. Preserve the threshold remainder.
2. Whenever ground count exceeds six times ship count, add one man-of-war. After declaration, bell production no longer grows the roster. Destruction of a real REF unit does not recreate it; distinguish reserve, shipped and destroyed counts.
3. Congress displays actual family totals and state without exposing an invented force. Save/Continue preserves growth/remainder and does not double-add a unit at a threshold. UC-082 checks starting profiles, exact threshold, cycle, ship boundary and post-declaration freeze.

4. Accepted on 2026-10-18: defeated Continental and royal infantry/cavalry are destroyed together with their equipment. They do not downgrade, survive as colonists, or leave recoverable equipment. See the [shared defeat rule](../gap-analysis.md#revolution-and-intervention).

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model/Crown owns persisted roster identities, growth remainder and state transitions, using R-0178 combat-role definitions and R-0100 bell accounting. Congress report reads these values; shipping consumes roster units through R-0186 rather than constructing new ones.
2. Proposed model/UC-082 checks cover each difficulty, bells just below/at threshold, ground-to-ship ratio, no postwar growth, destruction and reload under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
3. Current result: capture only; no REF implementation or runtime acceptance is claimed.
