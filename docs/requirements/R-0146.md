1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P10/P11; [COL-001](../gap-analysis.md#colony-workforce-food-and-storage); [UC-031](../use-cases.md#kolonie-obyvatele-a-vyroba) (Hra).
4. Dependencies: terrain and resource output from [R-0124](R-0124.md)/[R-0127](R-0127.md); existing colony turn and food accounting in [R-0057](done/R-0057.md)/[R-0058](done/R-0058.md).
5. Primary area: [A07](areas.md#a07).
6. Related areas: A05 terrain/resource values; A04 owner-update and saved-state continuity; A03 colony production preview.
7. Origin: COL-001 and UC-031 center-square residual; colony founding and its mountain/sea boundary are already [R-0027](done/R-0027.md), and staffed/building production is separate.

## 1. Summary

1. A colony produces food and a terrain-dependent second commodity from its center square without assigning a resident there.
2. The 2026-10-17 source catalogue at `8f3ce118` reports surrounding-field and building totals but no center-square production; this is a source observation, not a runtime acceptance result.
3. This requirement owns unattended commons output and its display/accounting, not colony founding, staffed field jobs, or the terrain yield matrix.

## 2. Behavior

1. After founding a colony on eligible land, the player can inspect food and the local commodity attributed to its center before ending a turn. No worker can be assigned to obtain this base output.
2. On each owner production update, the commons adds its food and terrain-dependent commodity once to the same production/food and storage accounting as other colony output. An otherwise unworked colony still receives it; a save and Continue do not repeat an update.
3. Apply the center's eligible special-resource effect to the appropriate output, except Prime Timber does not enhance commons production. A resource cannot conjure an unrelated output contrary to the shared terrain/resource rules.
4. Compare at least two contrasting center terrains and a resource/Prime Timber pair. The colony view's forecast, settled goods and food agree. Founding failure or merely opening the view produces nothing.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. The model's colony production owner derives center output through shared terrain/resource data and includes it exactly once in preview and actual owner update; GUI reads the resulting values. Keep the center distinct from the eight assignable surrounding fields.
2. On selection, reconcile the exact second-commodity lookup with the R-0124 baseline and R-0127 overlays before readiness; preserve existing R-0057/R-0058 food and storage boundaries.
3. Proposed model and UC-031 player checks cover unworked centers, terrain contrast, eligible resource and Prime Timber, preview-versus-settlement, and save/Continue without duplicate output. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no gameplay change or runtime acceptance is claimed.

## 4. Open questions

1. What exact food and second-commodity amounts does each legal center terrain produce, and how is the one second commodity selected when several could apply? COL-001 establishes the two outputs and resource exception but does not supply a complete commons table. The staffed-field matrix R-0124 must not silently become that table.
