## 1. Summary

1. The player needs starvation to remove the agreed resident and update production and warnings consistently.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Colony already kills one random internal worker when projected food is negative and resets food to zero, then recomputes production. Existing famine journeys do not prove every boundary and victim role.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Exactly zero food after production/consumption → no starvation death. A negative result → exactly one internal resident dies under the retained random-victim policy.

2. The deceased worker contributes no subsequent production; any surviving producer consumes only available input and no stock becomes negative.

3. The report identifies famine and the loss once; visiting units and cargo passengers are not selected as internal famine victims.

4. With different possible victim jobs, preview and final report explain the resulting stocks. Save/load must not replay an already processed famine.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Retain random internal famine victims, recompute committed production from survivors and distinguish the variable preview from the final report.
