## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a truthful strength explanation before battle, without presenting an unimplemented probability model as a win chance.

2. Current situation: R-0028 fixes legal attack initiation. Existing descriptive soldier/dragoon strengths and fixed win chance do not implement the manual’s ordinary bases 2 and 3 with a consistent modifier explanation.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. An ordinary soldier has base strength 2 and an ordinary dragoon 3 before the agreed modifiers.

2. The preview identifies each applicable attack, terrain and effective-fortification modifier and shows the final strengths using the agreed stacking/rounding.

3. Equipment or terrain changes update the explanation. Opening/canceling it spends no movement and draws no combat result.

4. The same saved position yields the same strength. Until R-0094 integrates these values into resolution, the display must not label their ratio as win probability.
