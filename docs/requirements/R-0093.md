## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a truthful strength explanation before battle, without presenting an unimplemented probability model as a win chance.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): R-0028 fixes legal attack initiation. Existing descriptive soldier/dragoon strengths and fixed win chance do not implement the manual’s ordinary bases 2 and 3 with a consistent modifier explanation.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected for linked refinement on 2026-10-16; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Agreed acceptance, refined from the shared combat rules on 2026-10-16.

1. An ordinary soldier has base strength 2 and an ordinary dragoon 3 before the agreed modifiers.

2. The preview identifies each applicable attack, terrain and effective-fortification modifier and shows the final strengths using the agreed stacking/rounding.

3. Equipment or terrain changes update the explanation. Opening/canceling it spends no movement and draws no combat result.

4. The same saved position yields the same strength. Until R-0094 integrates these values into resolution, the display must not label their ratio as win probability.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [combat and naval outcomes](../gap-analysis.md#combat-and-naval-outcomes)): Use one effective-strength calculation and its worked examples; deliver the strength-based explanation together with R-0094's matching resolver.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Source inspection (2026-10-16, main `92c5b806`): `AttackOrder` currently freezes identity/location/diplomacy only; `AbstractUnit.findAttackTarget` chooses the first eligible defender and `executeAttack` uses a fixed chance. Introduce one immutable model-owned strength breakdown for the quoted ordinary land battle, consumed by both `DialogFight` and the resolver. Bind confirmation to the same roles/equipment, effective fortification, terrain and highest colony fortification; a changed quote must be rejected before diplomacy, movement or random draw.

2. Visual source check: [manual](../Sid_Meiers_Colonization_manual.pdf), printed p.31/PDF37 and terrain card/PDF135. Reuse `TerrainType` defense fields: forest/nonforest grassland, prairie, savannah, desert and tundra are 50/0%; swamp is 75/25%; arctic 0%, hills 100%, mountains 150%. Correct existing swamp constants, which are currently 50/0, rather than adding a separate GUI table. The card also specifies plains 50/0% and marsh 50/25%; their missing representations and tundra forest support belong to the wider P04/MAP-001 terrain work, outside these ordinary-combat requirements. This change corrects defense metadata for every currently supported legal terrain/forest combination, including hills 100% and mountains 150%; it does not claim complete MAP-001 coverage. Add attack +50%, effective unit fortification +50%, and only the highest stockade/fort/fortress +100/+150/+200%. Percentages add before multiplying the base; display rounding does not feed the calculation. Ordinary soldier/dragoon bases are 2/3. Preserve the agreed native/revolutionary exceptions when their role integrations are delivered; do not label an unsupported naval/native preview with ordinary land odds.

3. Show the base, applicable modifiers and final attack/defense values in the existing battle dialog, with the actual selected defender and declaration warning. Read-only inspection and Escape/cancel draw nothing and preserve the world. Choose highest effective defense among eligible targets, then stable unit ID, while preserving the existing hostile-before-treaty-break selection rule. Keep combat base values in the combat calculation: the existing military-strength metric also prices peace/tribute and statistics, so changing it would silently alter separately agreed behavior.

4. Tests must cover the hand-calculated 3-vs-4 example, ordinary foot/mounted equipment, terrain chart boundaries, pending/effective fortification, highest building only, strongest defender/tied IDs and quote invalidation. Extend the real TC35 confirmation paths rather than replacing their movement/diplomacy assertions. Deliver together with [R-0094](R-0094.md); R-0106 is now integrated at `1f56c85b`. `AttackOrder.getCombatStrengths()` exposes an optional immutable `CombatStrengths` with attacker/defender `CombatStrength` breakdowns (base, attack/terrain/unit-fortification/colony-fortification percentages and total), plus the exact attacker-win ratio. Unsupported role pairs have no ordinary-land odds. A newly computed breakdown must match the quote before execution. Main owns dialog/TC35 integration; the bounded worker owns model, persistence and model tests. Implementation and fresh battle acceptance are pending.

5. Implemented and locally verified on 2026-10-16 in `codex/r93-combat`: full model suite 1681 passed, subsequent fortified-loss tests 5 passed, and repaired TC35 plus page-contract selection 15 passed. See [execution evidence](../functional-tests.md#r-0093r-0094-ordinary-land-combat-2026-10-16) for the diagnosed GUI-thread failure and regression scope. Integrated after packaging/Javadoc passed; full CI and publication remain pending.
