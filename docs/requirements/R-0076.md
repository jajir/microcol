## 1. Summary

1. The player needs religious production to determine immigration progress and arrivals rather than an unrelated fixed schedule.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): Player currently grants a human colonist on an elapsed-turn schedule. It does not accumulate national cross production into an immigration threshold.

3. Scope: national cross accounting and ordinary earned immigration using the persistent pool from R-0075. Actual Brewster membership and its interactive choice remain an integration dependency on [R-0104](R-0104.md). This does not close all of UC-054.

## 2. Behavior

Refined on 2026-10-16 from the agreed production and national-turn rules.

1. Produce crosses in two colonies → add both actual production amounts to one visible national progress total exactly once. Europe shows current crosses, the next target and whether an arrival awaits the next national turn. Opening or closing Europe never changes this progress.

2. At the next national preparation, before colony production, consume each reached threshold → receive a randomly selected current candidate on the European pier and replace only that candidate. This automatic arrival costs no gold and does not advance the paid-passage count. Production later in that preparation contributes to the following arrival phase; a newly reached target therefore waits until the next national turn. Paid passage remains unavailable while an earned arrival is pending.

3. Equal elapsed turns with different cross production produce different progress. At Conquistador, ordinary thresholds start at 100, 125 and 150: 230 available crosses yield two arrivals and leave 5 toward 150. England starts at 67. At target minus one there is no arrival; exact target yields one and zero surplus. Human and computer colonial players use the same accounting and existing Europe-access eligibility.

4. Save before/after arrival → preserve progress, candidate identity and the next threshold without duplicate arrivals. Opening Europe does not generate immigrants.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [difficulty and national effects](../gap-analysis.md#difficulty-and-national-effects), [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Aggregate cross production, consume increasing thresholds with surplus retained, and apply England's reduced target; remove the old periodic grant.

6. Shared update boundary (2026-10-15): apply this requirement within [national turn processing](../gap-analysis.md#national-turn-processing). Resume the saved completed boundary without repeating production, arrivals or action replenishment; future pending choices must preserve earlier completed updates.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. 2. `Colony.startTurn` credits only newly committed positive cross production to the owner's recruitment pool. Preserve existing colony warehouse accounting as local production history; never aggregate that cumulative stock again or transfer it into national progress after capture. `RecruitmentPool` owns checked addition, candidate replacement, threshold consumption and independent earned counts; `Player` owns unit creation and arrival events.
