## 1. Summary

1. The player needs religious production to determine immigration progress and arrivals rather than an unrelated fixed schedule.

2. Current situation: Player currently grants a human colonist on an elapsed-turn schedule. It does not accumulate national cross production into an immigration threshold.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Produce crosses in two colonies → contribute to one visible national progress total exactly once per update.

2. Reach the agreed threshold → receive the eligible candidate on the European pier and replenish the same recruitment pool once.

3. Equal elapsed turns with different cross production produce different progress under the agreed formula; account explicitly for the English national advantage.

4. Save before/after arrival → preserve progress, candidate identity and the next threshold without duplicate arrivals. Opening Europe does not generate immigrants.
