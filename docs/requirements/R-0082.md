## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a clear restriction when trying to remove the third resident from a fortified colony, while remaining free to rearrange work inside it.

2. Current situation: The existing empty-colony cleanup is not the manual’s minimum-three-residents rule for stockades and higher defenses.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. With a stockade or higher defense and three internal residents, an action removing one from the colony is rejected before equipment or stocks change.

2. With four residents, allow one to leave when other conditions hold; show the resulting three.

3. Moving a resident between internal jobs does not reduce population and remains allowed.

4. Famine/combat outcomes follow an explicit forced-loss policy rather than reusing a voluntary-removal rejection. Save/load retains the real population and fortification.
