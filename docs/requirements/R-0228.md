## 1. Summary

1. One selected high-value game outcome receives a short cue at the moment it actually occurs, with the same information available visually.
2. The asset and trigger are recorded in the shared audio catalogue with their source and usage rights.

## 2. Behavior

1. The user selected **mission victory + minimal playback support** on 2026-11-01. A short, pleasant success cue accompanies a newly committed successful human mission result (completed mission objectives or confirmed independence victory), once as the existing victory message begins. Loss, timeout, retirement and ordinary free-play calendar completion remain silent. Never infer success from localized text, opening a report, a preview or a rejected action.
2. The existing victory text and result presentation remain the complete visual equivalent, including when muted or audio is unavailable. One completed playthrough emits at most one cue; duplicate notifications, reopening messages, loading/Continue and replaying a finished save do not replay it. A genuinely restarted/new playthrough can emit its own victory cue. No cue backlog or overlapping victory cues; ignore duplicates while a cue is playing.
3. Music continues independently. Until R-0227 adds category controls, the cue follows the existing saved volume and zero means silence; `soundEnabled=false` always suppresses it. Playback starts asynchronously and cannot delay or alter the result, stocks, turn, save or navigation. Missing/unsupported asset or audio device fails safely and releases resources; stopping the application stops the cue.
4. Use one original, reproducibly generated short cue, with editable source and documented rights. Catalogue its identifier, success meaning, exact trigger/timing/recipient, visual companion, format, volume/overlap policy and actual verification. Do not claim listening on a platform unless it happened; generated audio and automated checks are not listening evidence. No additional effects or music are authorized here.

Use a gentle short envelope and conservative level, with no abrupt boundary or clipping. Document repeatable generation and original-source rights in [the audio catalogue](../audio.md#katalog-a-sablona-noveho-zvuku). Update the responsibility map/package documentation only where ownership changes.
4. Add focused model/JUnit tests for explicit success versus every non-success result, duplicate/reload/new-playthrough boundaries, zero/global mute and failure isolation/cleanup.
