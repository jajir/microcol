## 1. Summary

1. The player needs the next ship/artillery quote to reflect previous purchases and survive Continue.

2. Current situation: European prices are immutable type values; a successful purchase does not persist demand-related price growth.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Inspect a supported ship or artillery offer → show its current total quote before confirmation.

2. Buy successfully → debit that quote once and update the appropriate next purchase price.

3. Cancel, insufficient gold or stale quote → do not advance purchase counts or change treasury.

4. Save/load retains price history. Buying a specialist does not accidentally change ship prices.
