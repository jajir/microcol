## 1. Summary

1. The player needs the next ship/artillery quote to reflect previous purchases and survive Continue.

2. Migration observation (2026-10-14): European prices are immutable type values; a successful purchase does not persist demand-related price growth.

3. Scope: per-player purchase history and confirmed quotes for supported European types. Ships are available now; artillery integration depends on R-0095 and remains explicitly open until that real type is available.

## 2. Behavior

Refined on 2026-10-16 from the agreed economy rule.

1. Inspect a supported ship or artillery offer → show its current total quote before confirmation.

2. Buy a caravel for 1000 → debit 1000 once and show 1100 next time. Buy it again → debit 1100 and show 1200. Other ship types and other players retain their own prices. Specialist and ordinary colonist prices remain fixed.

3. Cancel, fall one gold short or confirm a price made stale by another purchase → create no additional unit, do not advance purchase counts or change treasury. Reopening shows the current quote; the model never silently charges a newer price. A queued duplicate confirmation buys only once.

4. Save/load retains price history and created ships. Buying a specialist does not change ship prices or count; purchase prices remain untaxed. A purchase that would overflow the next supported quote is refused before charging or creating a ship.

5. Accepted behavior (2026-10-15; [economy, production and education](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education)): Increase only the purchased type's next quote by the agreed tenth-of-base increment; preserve counts and reject overflow before payment.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Source inspection on 2026-10-16 at `2a5dbac6`: `Player.buy` and `BuyUnitPanel` both use immutable `UnitType` base prices. Keep those bases and the existing transaction owner; introduce a typed per-player purchase-count map in `PlayerPo`. The sparse map defaults to empty, omitted type entries mean zero prior purchases; explicit null, negative, unknown or unsupported entries are rejected. Save copies the map. No migration or new game option is needed.

2. `Player.getUnitPurchasePrice` owns the checked formula. The confirmed-price overload of `buy` revalidates live player, Europe access, current price, funds, unit identity space and prospective next price before mutation. Existing immediate model callers quote current price. `BuyUnitPanel` retains the displayed quote and passes it to the shared transaction; the existing modal duplicate guard and rejection path remain.

3. Model checks cover two successive purchases, independent types/players, fixed specialist prices, exact/insufficient funds, stale offers, identity/arithmetic limits and saved history. `TC_48_F39_ship_purchase_prices_test` covers cancel, two purchases, unavailable third, Continue and a changed quote. Reuse TC30 specialist and TC46 recruitment regressions and the page verification contract. Main applies the shared formatter and executes Docker UI checks, model tests, packaging and strict documentation validation.
