## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a rival that can run a small legal colony-to-Europe economic cycle.

2. Current situation: The first-colony planner does not implement a recurring supply/export cycle. Human cargo and Europe operations already provide reusable capabilities.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected for individual refinement on 2026-10-17; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Acceptance agreed with the user on 2026-10-19; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Given a known colony, suitable carrier and stock, the rival selects a bounded supply/export objective and loads only available goods.

2. Transport and sell through normal routes and market rules; credit actual proceeds and preserve cargo capacity and ownership.

3. Full holds, blocked arrival or unavailable stock produce a defined retry/alternative and a finite turn, not duplicated goods or stalled play.

4. Save midway and continue → retain the same objective/cargo with no repeated sale. Trade automation for the human is a separate feature.

5. Accepted policy (2026-10-19): export lumber only above a 50-unit colony reserve, with at least 50 units available for the shipment. The basic 100-unit warehouse can therefore hold the reserve and minimum shipment together. Never load more than the available surplus or carrier capacity. At Europe, use ordinary taxed sales and buy tools toward a 50-unit colony supply target only while retaining 200 gold; deliver through normal cargo operations. If no amount is affordable, return without inventing supplies. Existing in-transit supplies must count toward the target to avoid duplicate purchases.

6. Accepted recovery: a blocked route/arrival or unavailable legal transfer preserves the objective and actual cargo, ends the finite turn and retries on the next owner turn. Revalidate owner, colony, carrier, stock, capacity and trade permission before every operation. Destroyed/captured objectives must be retired rather than retaining dangling references. These policy details are agreed; implementation and acceptance are pending.

7. Workforce dependency settled (2026-10-17): use the [accepted food-first, then lumber policy](../gap-analysis.md#rival-colony-workforce) in R-0090. The export, supply and reserve choices above were accepted on 2026-10-19.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Source inspection on 2026-10-17 at main `5fc6a55a`: `SimpleAiPlayer.turnStarted` returns after Congress selection when a colony already exists; ordinary movement avoids high sea. The existing first-colony `founder`/`carrier` fields do not establish a persistent trade objective. Reuse R-0090's accepted [food-first workforce policy](../gap-analysis.md#rival-colony-workforce); extend the AI objective lifecycle under the now-agreed trade policy; reuse normal model voyage, cargo and European trade operations. AI chooses bounded actions, GUI presents the same decisions under the [package responsibility map](../architecture.md). Keep rule validation before mutation and use current-schema fixtures.

2. Observation basis: the 2026-10-14 [migration baseline](migration-2026-10-14.md#verification-baseline). Source inspection and catalogue findings are not fresh acceptance of these examples; recheck the selected paths before choosing a design.

3. Verification: Include a basic 100-capacity warehouse (49 surplus waits; 50 surplus can depart while retaining 50), partial affordability, in-transit supply deduplication and destroyed objectives. Add TC85/F76 as a deterministic rival shipment journey and inventory/treasury conservation checks using existing voyage/trade operations. Allocate any new scenario IDs during refinement; old unimplemented proposals do not reserve TC numbers. Follow the [functional test contract](../functional-tests.md).

4. Selected as the first implementable requirement of the 2026-10-19 batch. AI owns a bounded single-carrier objective per rival; store stable colony/carrier IDs and its phase with the player, validate saved references and retire destroyed/captured objectives on the next owner turn. Use ordinary cargo, taxed market and voyage operations. Start with the lowest-ID eligible coastal colony and available empty ship; wait finitely if none exists. No human route automation or military expansion is added. The Europe phase must run even though the current unit movement loop visits map units only. All rejected operations preserve stock, gold and cargo.
5. Main inspection confirmed the existing AI only assigns workforce for established colonies and otherwise avoids high sea; current PlayerPo has no trade objective. Preserve the working founder/workforce paths and their TC34 checks. Main owns refinement, actual verification and documentation. Implementation and acceptance remain pending.
