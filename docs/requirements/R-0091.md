## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a rival that can run a small legal colony-to-Europe economic cycle.

2. Current situation: The first-colony planner does not implement a recurring supply/export cycle. Human cargo and Europe operations already provide reusable capabilities.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Given a known colony, suitable carrier and stock, the rival selects a bounded supply/export objective and loads only available goods.

2. Transport and sell through normal routes and market rules; credit actual proceeds and preserve cargo capacity and ownership.

3. Full holds, blocked arrival or unavailable stock produce a defined retry/alternative and a finite turn, not duplicated goods or stalled play.

4. Save midway and continue → retain the same objective/cargo with no repeated sale. Trade automation for the human is a separate feature.
