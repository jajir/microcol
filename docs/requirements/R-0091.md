## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a rival that can run a small legal colony-to-Europe economic cycle.

2. Current situation: The first-colony planner does not implement a recurring supply/export cycle. Human cargo and Europe operations already provide reusable capabilities.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. Selected for individual refinement on 2026-10-17; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Given a known colony, suitable carrier and stock, the rival selects a bounded supply/export objective and loads only available goods.

2. Transport and sell through normal routes and market rules; credit actual proceeds and preserve cargo capacity and ownership.

3. Full holds, blocked arrival or unavailable stock produce a defined retry/alternative and a finite turn, not duplicated goods or stalled play.

4. Save midway and continue → retain the same objective/cargo with no repeated sale. Trade automation for the human is a separate feature.

5. Proposal awaiting the user's answer (2026-10-17): export lumber only above a 50-unit colony reserve, with at least 50 units available for the shipment. The basic 100-unit warehouse can therefore hold the reserve and minimum shipment together. Never load more than the available surplus or carrier capacity. At Europe, use ordinary taxed sales and buy tools toward a 50-unit colony supply target only while retaining 200 gold; deliver through normal cargo operations. If no amount is affordable, return without inventing supplies. Existing in-transit supplies must count toward the target to avoid duplicate purchases.

6. Proposed recovery: a blocked route/arrival or unavailable legal transfer preserves the objective and actual cargo, ends the finite turn and retries on the next owner turn. Revalidate owner, colony, carrier, stock, capacity and trade permission before every operation. Destroyed/captured objectives must be retired rather than retaining dangling references. These policy details are proposed, not implemented or accepted.

7. Workforce dependency settled (2026-10-17): use the [accepted food-first, then lumber policy](../gap-analysis.md#rival-colony-workforce) in R-0090. The export, supply and reserve choices above remain proposals.
