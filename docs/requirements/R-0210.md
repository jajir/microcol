## 1. Summary

1. Every printed terrain row and legal forest/bare variant must yield the correct production, move cost and defense in a real game.
2. This requirement owns the complete table evidence, not a second terrain engine.

## 2. Behavior

1. Check every card row and each legal forest/bare state against independent expected production cells for food, sugar, tobacco, cotton, furs, lumber, ore, silver and fish, plus exact move thirds and defender bonus. Keep absent/zero cells zero; do not smooth barren output.
2. Check small/large rivers, resources/depletion, road and plow overlays in separate paired cases, including forest clearing and a reload. Compare actual update, action range/path and battle preview/resolver to the same source cell.
3. UC-007–009/012/021/073 remains partial until the full row×variant×column ledger and overlay boundaries pass on one named build; defects return to R-0123–0128 or combat owners.
