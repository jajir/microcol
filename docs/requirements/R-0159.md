## 1. Summary

1. Native settlement trade requires real stock/treasury, local demand, prior land contact and an eligible loaded carrier.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` reports no settlement market/contact records; this is source assessment only.
3. This requirement owns persistent shared market data and admission/quote inputs. The actual transfer of goods/gold belongs to R-0160/0161.

## 2. Behavior

1. An ordinary settlement starts with 1000 gold and a capital with 2000; each owner update adds 20/40, capped at twice the initial treasury. It offers three locally producible goods by surplus then goods ID, starts with 100 of each and restocks 10/update up to 200. Its three wanted tradable goods have lowest local production, ties by goods ID.
2. A land introduction to the tribe must precede its trade. Thereafter a loaded wagon can trade at an eligible settlement, or a loaded ship at a coastal settlement. Empty cargo, first-contact trade and an inland ship are refused without movement/cargo/gold changes.
3. Angry/hostile native relations refuse trade; an uneasy settlement refuses ship trade. A village refuses the same bought commodity on consecutive expeditions except muskets. The player sees the actual admission reason. Save/Continue retains contact, stock, treasury, wanted/last goods and pending trade state without restocking twice.
4. UC-064 checks all gate combinations, capital/ordinary treasury-restock bounds, local demand/three-stock ordering and repeated commodity exception. R-0160/0161 separately verify price and settlement transactions.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model settlement owner stores treasury, goods stock, demand derivation, land-contact and last expedition commodity. Expose immutable limits/quotes to GUI; model revalidates carrier, relation, stock and contact before either transaction. Use R-0153 T/L and shared cargo operations.
2. On selection, connect local-production values to R-0124/R-0127 and native terrain identity. Do not treat arbitrary European market holdings as settlement stock.
3. Proposed model/UC-064 checks cover stock/restock and treasury caps, demand ties, contact, wagon/ship/coast, mood, last-good/musket exception, stale rejection and Continue. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no gameplay change or runtime acceptance is claimed.
