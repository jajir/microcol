## 1. Summary

1. The player needs safe job changes when two colonies share a working square, without losing a worker or counting its output twice.

2. Current situation: Founding now checks global field availability, and crop validation precedes detachment. Manual placement still checks the local colony field; the founding helper is not a complete shared assignment contract.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Proposed acceptance for refinement; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. Colonies at (10,10) and (12,10) share (11,10). If one works that square, the other cannot place a second worker there, including across different owners.

2. Vacate the square → a valid worker may take it. Changing crop on the same square creates no second worker or second production contribution.

3. Invalid crop, stale occupied target, missing colony or invalid ownership → preserve the old job, equipment and both colonies’ stocks.

4. Moving the last resident between jobs in the same colony preserves the colony. Canceling a drag changes nothing; save/load preserves globally unique assignments.
