# R-0300 — Curved rivers with readable resource icons

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-26
3. Related: P04 map/exploration; UC-008 and UC-012, Hra, existing river and resource journeys in the [functional test contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Dependencies: [R-0290](done/R-0290.md) river corridors and deposits; [R-0126](done/R-0126.md) connected river travel; [R-0127](done/R-0127.md) resource identity; the [current visual brief](../graphics.md#soucasne-zadani-vzhledu).
5. Primary area: [A03](areas.md#a03), map appearance and readable overlays.
6. Related areas: A05 supplies river topology, sizes, visibility and resource identity; A07 supplies unchanged deposit and fishing outcomes.
7. Origin: user screenshot and request on 2026-10-26 for five alternatives to straight river segments, with bonus icons above rivers.

## 1. Summary

1. A player inspecting the map should see natural-looking connected rivers and clearly recognize any resource bonus on their squares.
2. Source inspection at `688e4ca7` on 2026-10-26 finds that terrain painting includes resource icons and the map then paints rivers over it. This explains the overlap in the supplied screenshot; it is not a new runtime verification.
3. This request prepares five visual proposals. It captures the required overlay correction alongside the river appearance change; no proposal has yet been selected for gameplay implementation.

## 2. Behavior

1. When a known river enters the map view, its course follows smooth bends instead of a chain of straight segments and sharp corners. Small and large rivers remain distinguishable, with continuous tributaries, junctions and mouths.
2. A silver or ore icon on a known river square is drawn above the river and stays readable. It keeps its existing identity and placement; the river does not cut across it. This ordering is required for every proposed style, not an open design choice.
3. Scrolling, redrawing and Continue preserve the course's visual shape. Hidden terrain and river connections remain hidden. Bridges, settlements, units and selection remain legible; rendering changes no river links, movement, fishing, deposits or saved game state.
4. Five alternatives are proposed: **Jemné oblouky**, restrained smooth bends with constant width; **Ručně kreslené koryto**, smooth bends with gently irregular width; **Výrazné meandry**, broader alternating bends; **Tenká inkoustová kontura**, curved water with a thin dark bank outline; **Světlé břehy**, curved water bordered by a narrow light bank. All follow the existing flat palette and hand-drawn brief. These are visual proposals, not implemented or verified gameplay.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. After selection, refine the chosen appearance within the existing GUI painting responsibility in the [architecture map](../architecture.md). Reuse model river connections and resource identities; keep decorative curvature deterministic and within the intended river corridor.
2. Inspect all callers of `PaintService.paintRiver`, `PaintService.paintResource`, terrain previews and `GamePaintService.paintRivers`. Correct the shared rendering order so resource icons follow river/bridge painting without painting resources twice or changing visibility/highlight semantics. Review the main map and any preview using the affected helpers.
3. Extend `TC_90_F81_river_effects_test` and `TC_91_F82_resource_overlays_test` as appropriate: small/large courses, diagonal links, junctions, mouths, river deposits, bridge visibility, fog boundaries and Save/Continue. Keep current gameplay assertions, fixture conventions and dedicated page verification methods. Add focused geometry or ordering checks where needed and inspect screenshots at normal tile size.
4. Follow the shared [Java formatter](../code-formatting.md), [functional contract](../functional-tests.md) and [local acceptance policy](../publishing.md#local-acceptance-policy) for implementation. The current change captures proposals only; documentation checks cannot establish that the game rendering has been fixed.
