## 1. Summary

1. A scout may meet a native chief once for information, gold or a risk of death; later visits repeat known information without another draw.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `80b0e180` reports no chief outcome handler. This does not claim runtime verification.
3. This requirement owns one accepted encounter and its persistent result, not general role selection or native market trades.

## 2. Behavior

1. Before confirmation, show the settlement's mood/risk. For content use information/gold/death 70/25/5%; uneasy 75/15/10%; angry 60/10/30%; hostile 40/0/60%. A seasoned scout halves the death percentage and adds that difference to information.
2. A confirmed encounter draws once. Information reveals only this settlement's goods, teachable skill and terrain within two squares; gold transfers up to 100 from its real treasury, capped at 200 for a capital, without creating money; death removes the visitor. The player receives the actual outcome.
3. Track one encounter per visiting European nation and settlement. Later eligible visits repeat permitted information, with no second gold/death draw. Cancel before confirmation leaves the visitor, visit record, treasury and random state unchanged. Save/Continue retains the consumed encounter and outcome.
4. UC-062 compares ordinary/seasoned scouts and four moods under controlled draws, capital/empty treasury, information scope, cancel and repeat visit.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model owns per-nation/settlement encounter record, controlled draw and atomic outcome. Read real R-0159 treasury and R-0150 information. GUI shows preview/results through R-0157; avoid client rerolls.
2. On selection, connect the R-0074 scout role and settlement skill/goods. Information may reveal the authorized neighboring two-square terrain, but no terrain or foreign detail beyond that scope. Preserve current-schema identity and random state.
3. Proposed model and UC-062 checks cover all distributions, seasoned death transfer, gold caps, all three outcomes, repeat visit, cancellation and Continue. Follow the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: capture only; no gameplay change or runtime acceptance is claimed.
