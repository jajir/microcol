# R-0141 — Assign a carrier and repeat its trade route

1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-17
3. Related: P16/P06; ROUTE-003; UC-029/116 (Hra). See the [route rule](../gap-analysis.md#automated-trade-routes), [accepted pause policy](../gap-analysis.md#exploration-work-and-transport) and [route use case](../use-cases.md#lode-naklad-a-obchodni-trasy).
4. Dependencies: saved route structure [R-0139](R-0139.md), stop processing [R-0140](R-0140.md), shared movement [R-0125](done/R-0125.md) and Atlantic travel [R-0021](done/R-0021.md). Editing/canceling an assignment belongs to [R-0142](R-0142.md).
5. Primary area: [A06](areas.md#a06).
6. Related areas: A05 carrier movement/orders; A04 owner-turn continuation; A03 route progress/reason display.
7. Origin: ROUTE-003 gap in the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md#automated-trade-routes).

## 1. Summary

1. A player needs to assign a wagon or ship to a saved itinerary, choose its first stop and let it repeat until canceled.
2. The 2026-10-17 catalogue at `ffae3396` finds no saved route assignment or recurring navigation. This is source inspection, not a runtime check.
3. This requirement owns assignment, current stop, movement and repeated cycles; it reuses stop transactions and does not create a second goods-transfer rule.

## 2. Behavior

1. Assign an owned wagon to a legal land route or ship to a legal sea route and select a first stop. A non-carrier, wrong owner, incompatible route or unreachable first stop rejects without replacing the unit's existing state.
2. On later owner turns, the carrier follows legal steps within its movement, processes each reached stop once through [R-0140](R-0140.md), advances to the next ordered stop and repeats after the last. A full ship or empty warehouse does not stop the turn; a transfer may be a no-op. A single-stop or coincident-stop route cannot process its stop indefinitely within one owner update without movement. A lost/inaccessible stop pauses the carrier with a visible reason rather than substituting another destination or spinning.
3. Save/Continue mid-leg, at a stop and between two cycles retains assignment, current stop and travel progress without replaying movement or cargo. Cancel/repair behavior is [R-0142](R-0142.md); two complete circuits demonstrate the recurring order.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Model owns a persisted carrier assignment and current-stop index, advanced at the existing unit owner-turn boundary. Reuse model movement, sea travel and R-0140 stop transaction; UI reads progress/pause state.
2. Not prepared for development. On selection, inspect route/carrier location and turn ordering, including a saved stop-processing marker; do not advance an already completed stage when loading.
3. Proposed checks: ship/wagon versus non-carrier, selected first stop, exact movement, two circuits, single/coincident-stop finite update, full/empty no-op, lost stop pause, save/Continue and named UC-029/116 player paths under the [functional contract](../functional-tests.md).
4. Current result: captured only; no gameplay change or runtime test is claimed.
