## 1. Summary

1. The river and forest overlap in the supplied screenshot. The player needs
   readable river courses and a useful reason to settle beside them without
   filling the same square with trees, fields, a colony and resource symbols.
2. Source inspection at `05301270` on 2026-10-26: rivers are land overlays,
   add +1/+2 to already-positive output and permit connected travel for one
   third of a movement point. Forests, plowing, colonies and land resources
   can coexist with them. Fishery resources currently require ocean or lake;
   fish already use the ordinary food stock. These are source observations,
   not fresh gameplay acceptance. See the existing [river contract](done/R-0126.md).
3. Recommend an open river corridor with fishing, roads and rare silver as
   its only resource overlay. This deliberately changes the economic role
   of river squares; simply reducing tree artwork would be a smaller visual
   alternative if the existing economic rules should remain intact.

## 2. Behavior

1. **All following rules are proposals, not adopted behavior.** Both river
   sizes remain traversable land with a visible watercourse. No forest,
   plowed field or colony occupies that same square. A colony may stand
   beside it under the ordinary founding and land-ownership rules. Roads
   are allowed; a crossing is drawn as one small bridge, without introducing
   a separate bridge construction action. This is a map-scale convention,
   not a claim that rivers cannot run through real forests or towns.
2. An available river square among a colony's eight neighboring squares
   accepts one worker. Fishing produces ordinary food, not a new commodity,
   and needs no dock. Proposed base output before worker/political effects:
   small river 3, large river 4 per turn where the underlying bare terrain
   already supports food; mountain and arctic river sources produce none.
   A fisherman receives the existing double food expertise (6/8); a farmer
   receives no fishing expertise. Other worker modifiers retain their rules.
3. Fishing replaces crop, timber, fur and ordinary mineral production on
   that river square. The current blanket +1/+2 river modifier is not added
   again. Roads improve travel, not fishing or river silver output. Ordinary
   adjoining land keeps its production; merely being beside a river grants
   no passive food, irrigation or extra production. One worker cannot work
   two squares, and two colonies cannot work the same square simultaneously.
4. Permit only the existing silver deposit as a rare river resource,
   representing a deposit on its bank. Proposed silver output is 2 before
   profession effects, available only while its existing 100-unit reserve
   supplies production under the shared depletion rules. The worker chooses
   fishing or silver, never both in one turn. Exhaustion removes silver
   output and preserves any fishing. No automatic gold income or new
   resource type is introduced. Fish are inherent in the river, so no fish
   bonus or repeated fish icon is needed; other bonuses remain off rivers.
5. Keep connected river travel at one third of a point in either direction;
   road and river discounts do not stack. Merely crossing or approaching a
   river grants no river discount. Ships still cannot sail through river
   land. Keep underlying terrain movement/defense; removing a forest also
   removes its movement cost and defense contribution. No additional river
   combat rule is proposed.
6. In the colony view, show fishing and its expected food output, or silver
   and its remaining deposit. For example, assigning an ordinary colonist
   to an available large river gives 4 food on the next production update
   before other modifiers, rather than food merely for adjacency. A denied
   founding/plowing action explains the river restriction and changes no
   unit, tools, land payment or orders; canceling changes nothing. A stale
   job choice is rejected before mutation. Save/Continue preserves jobs,
   reserves and output without production or depletion on load.
7. Visually prioritize the watercourse and any road crossing; a permitted
   silver marker sits compactly on the bank and remains discoverable through
   inspection. Keep units, ownership and fog readable under the current
   [visual brief](../graphics.md#soucasne-zadani-vzhledu). New maps must retain
   usable non-river founding sites and access to nearby timber. Losing river
   forests and crop boosts must not accidentally make starting colonies
   unable to feed themselves or obtain building material.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Refine only after selection. If adopted, reconcile the shared
   [terrain](../gap-analysis.md#map-terrain-and-exploration),
   [production](../gap-analysis.md#economy-production-and-education) and
   [pioneer](../gap-analysis.md#equipment-and-pioneers) rules and affected
   active requirements. Preserve historical R-0126/0127/0128 evidence.
2. Under the [package responsibility map](../architecture.md), reuse
   `Terrain`, `WorldMap`, `ResourceOverlay`, founding/pioneer model operations,
   `ColonyField` and worker expertise. The river remains an overlay, not a
   fifteenth base terrain. Model validation owns legal combinations and
   atomic rejection; GUI and AI consume the same legality and yield queries.
   Review AI settlement/food choices, colony work previews, map rendering,
   bilingual help, persistence validation, generator and authored fixtures.
3. Original World source inventory on 2026-10-26: 28 river squares, including
   10 with forest and three with currently proposed-incompatible resources
   (mixed minerals, oasis, ore); no authored colony stands on a river.
   Reposition affected forests/resources deliberately when implementing,
   preserving a viable landing/founding journey. Update bundled maps and
   fixtures directly; no save migration or silent alteration on opening a
   player's save. Reject incompatible required state with a useful reason.
4. Planned verification: extend `RiverProductionTest`, `RiverMovementTest`,
   `WorldMapResourceTest`, `FoundingPreconditionsTest`, `PlowFieldTest`,
   `RoadProductionTest` and `GeneratedWorldTest` for legality, zero-food
   sources, expertise, non-stacking and depletion. Adapt existing
   `TC_90_F81_river_effects_test`, `TC_91_F82_resource_overlays_test`,
   `TC_102_F93_mineral_depletion_test` and affected founding, pioneer,
   terrain/help and Original World journeys, retaining stable IDs and shared
   page verification methods. Check fishing without a dock, job switching,
   rejected founding/plowing, bridge readability, fog and Save/Continue.
   Implementation requires the normal formatting, targeted checks and
   mandatory [local acceptance](../publishing.md#local-acceptance-policy).
5. Current result: proposal captured only. No gameplay, map data, artwork or
   runtime verification changes are part of this documentation task.

## 4. Open questions

1. Adopt the proposed river economy and same-square restrictions, or retain
   current gameplay and simplify only rendering? Blocking for implementation:
   the screenshot demonstrates visual crowding, not consent to replace the
   existing production rules.
2. If selected, adopt the proposed 3/4 food, current-terrain zero-food
   exceptions, no-dock fishing and finite silver-only exception? Blocking
   for implementation: these are concrete starting values for discussion,
   not tested balance. The exact generation frequency and authored silver
   placement must be settled during refinement.
