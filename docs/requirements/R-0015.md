# R-0015 — Add fixed French cities and units to free play

1. Status: Refining
2. Updated: 2026-10-13
3. Related: P02, P20, P21; SETUP-003, DIP-001; UC-004/UC-068 (Hra). See the [rule catalogue](../gap-analysis.md),
   [use cases](../use-cases.md) and [work packages](../implementation-plan.md#seznam-pracovnich-baliku).
4. Dependencies: [R-0014](R-0014.md) profile identity.
5. Primary area: [A02](areas.md#a02).
6. Related areas: A08 opponent strategy and relations; A04 participant turn order.

## 1. Summary

1. Agreed 2026-10-13: add France as at least one additional nation at fixed map positions, with cities and units.

2. This is a scenario-content starting point; it does not require the previously proposed configurable rival roster, arbitrary opponent counts or intelligence sliders.

3. The present audit records the target and existing capability. Actual French scenario data and long-term economic AI are subsequent implementation; R-0027 remains the AI planning slice.

## 2. Behavior

1. The reference free-play scenario includes the human nation and France, with French-owned cities and units at explicit repeatable coordinates.

2. Choose legal fixed locations separated from the human expedition. Use at least two cities and suitable supported units as initial implementation data; exact names/counts/coordinates can be chosen during implementation without another setup-design approval.

3. French cities and units have consistent ownership, valid unique identities and legal places. The name France must not be a cosmetic label on human-owned objects.

4. Persist the added world content and its controller association; save/load preserves French ownership and the human player.

5. Do not promise national advantages, sophisticated AI or editable rival settings merely because France appears on the map. Record those gaps separately.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Extend the selected bundled free-play map data and existing player/controller initialization; inspect name/king assumptions before adding the fixed nation.

2. Validate map positions, colony identities, inhabitants and units using the existing model/schema. Use supported unit types and reuse current AI rather than inventing a second rule engine.

3. Add a UI start/inspection/continuation scenario verifying French-owned cities and units. More complete DIP-001 behavior remains with R-0027.

## 4. Open questions

1. Resolved 2026-10-13: include at least France; no configurable opponent roster is required for this slice.

2. Resolved 2026-10-13: use fixed places with cities and units. Exact legal coordinates and initial content are routine implementation choices, not a blocking product question.
