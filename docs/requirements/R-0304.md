1. Status: new
2. Updated: 2026-10-27

Longer inland bends remain separate, unselected work.

## 1. Summary

1. The implemented river bends are too short to reproduce the chosen proposal. The desired course has long, flowing bends spanning several squares, with restrained width variation and readable resource bonuses.
2. A river reaching the sea should widen between opening banks and become part of the ocean. A dark river stroke pasted over an intact coastline does not communicate an outlet.
3. This capture supplies four visual examples for refinement. It does not report an implemented river mouth or replace the completed R-0300 evidence.

## 2. Behavior

1. Proposed river appearance follows the supplied reference: long smooth bends, clean tributary joins, distinguishable small/large watercourses and gentle width variation. The square grid should not dictate a repeated small wave.
2. Proposed coastal composition opens the shoreline at the river mouth, widens the water between its banks and blends the short terminal reach into the ocean. No closed coast outline crosses the outlet and no dark rounded cap extends into open water.
3. Selected on 2026-10-27: use one two-arm delta with the small elongated island from [variant B](../graphics-assets/river-mouths-2026-10-27/selected-b.png). Both arms open into the sea. The island is decorative, not new playable terrain. Use this one appearance for supported mouths; no per-map/per-mouth style selection, stored variant IDs or selection UI. Keep the shared flat-colour style. The earlier alternatives remain historical proposals.
4. Resource icons remain above river and bridge artwork. Fog, unexplored neighbours, bridges, selection, movement and production retain their existing meaning. Panning and Continue preserve the shape; loading must not rewrite a player's map.
5. Existing R-0301/R-0302 geography excludes river squares touching water, including diagonally, except the single Original World endpoint at (25, 13) explicitly authorized in [R-0313](R-0313.md) on 2026-10-27. [R-0315](R-0315.md) renders existing known coastal endpoints only; it does not introduce further placement exceptions or change generation, authored geography or saves.

## 3. Implementation steps

1. Source inspection at `0ad22e8c` finds `RiverShape` building individual tile bands with a 2.2-pixel local bend and tile-centre controls. It receives river size/known links but no water/coast context. This explains the short-wave appearance and missing outlet treatment; it is source analysis, not a new runtime test.
2. For implementation, derive smooth tangents across a known connected run while keeping its water inside the agreed logical river corridor. Handle tributaries, ends and junctions explicitly, preserve shared banks and stable world-coordinate geometry, and place bridges on the actual local course. Keep presentation in `gui.util` and map composition in `gui.screen.game.gamepanel` under the [architecture map](../architecture.md).
3. The selected single mouth is implemented separately in [R-0315](R-0315.md); no new map-format field or variant registry is needed. Resolve the coastal-rule dependency before changing generation or fixtures. The model owns any terminal connection/placement exception; the GUI uses only known coastline and river data to compose the mouth. Do not invent hidden ocean connections or new playable islands from decorative geometry.
4. Retain TC90/F81 and TC91/F82 gameplay and overlay checks; add coverage for long-bend continuity, actual coastal openings, size transitions, hidden coast, diagonal approaches, bridges, panning and Continue. Any placement change also updates the relevant generated/fixed-map scenarios. Apply the shared formatter and the model, targeted UI, smoke and documentation checks from [local acceptance](../publishing.md#local-acceptance-policy).
5. This requirement retains the broader inland-curve proposal and the selected mouth design. Runtime mouth delivery and its verification limits are tracked in [R-0315](R-0315.md). Four SVG proposals were rendered and visually inspected, and browser inspection confirmed the first view and carousel transition to the delta. These checks validate the examples, not game implementation.
