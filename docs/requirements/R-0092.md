## 1. Summary

1. The player needs a rival that can prepare and found another viable colony without abandoning the first.

2. Current situation: R-0027’s deliberately bounded objective ends with the first settlement; it has no continuing expansion policy.

3. Scope: the outcome and examples below. This is captured work awaiting individual refinement; it does not reopen the delivered origin scope or close a whole UC.

## 2. Behavior

Acceptance refined by the 2026-10-20 user answers; inherited resolved decisions remain agreed.

1. When the agreed food/population/resources permit expansion, choose a known legal second site and a suitable founder/transport.

2. Travel and found through the existing naming, spacing, ownership and assignment rules; do not reveal unexplored sites to the planner.

3. If no suitable known site or available founder exists, preserve the first colony and finish the turn without a planning loop.

4. Save/load preserves preparation and travel; after arrival there is exactly one new colony with a stable name and identity.

5. Accepted dependency (2026-10-17; [rival colony workforce](../gap-analysis.md#rival-colony-workforce)): Preserve the first colony's food-first, then spare-worker lumber policy when preparing expansion. The expansion trigger and reserves are now agreed in the [2026-10-20 rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0092-refinement).

6. Agreed 2026-10-20 ([shared rule](../gap-analysis.md#r-0092-refinement)): After releasing a founder, retain at least two residents (or the higher existing fortified-colony minimum), nonnegative food balance and food stock covering five turns of remaining residents’ consumption. Use a spare unarmed colonist, available legal transport and a known legal site. In a prepared reference scenario with site and transport available, establish the second colony within 20 owner turns; this is a test horizon, not a universal in-game deadline.
